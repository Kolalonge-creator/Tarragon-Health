# WellNation Nigeria (wellnationnigeria.com)

## Important framing note before the findings

WellNation Nigeria appears to be part of a small family of similarly-branded "Well Nation" wellness businesses, not a single obviously-unified company — this matters for how a competitive report should characterize it:
- **wellnationnigeria.com** — "WELL NATION NIGERIA," Lagos, Nigeria phone (+234), positions itself as a Nigeria-focused "healthcare ecosystem integrator" spanning wellness, telehealth, diagnostics, insurance, and pharmacy.
- **wellnationafrica.com** / Google Play package `com.wellnation.africa` / App Store app "WNAfrica" (developer "Well Nation Ltd.") — a South Africa-based (+27 phone) corporate wellness technology company ("YOUR GO-TO WELLNESS TECHNOLOGY PARTNER IN AFRICA"), centered on a wearables/gamified-challenges platform and its own "YOO Wellness" wearable device line.
- **wellnation.com** / LinkedIn "Well Nation" (233 followers) — reads as a separate/parent corporate-wellness brand ("powerful, comprehensive, low cost, and easy to set up" wellness solution for businesses).
- The WellNation Nigeria site's own contact email domain is `@wellnation.com` (not `@wellnationnigeria.com`), which is a signal of some brand/backend relationship to the wellnation.com entity, but no page explicitly states the corporate relationship between the three properties. This should be flagged as an inference, not a confirmed fact — see Gaps below.

## What does the platform/app actually do? (Patient/clinician features, chronic disease management, screening, lab/pharmacy integration, telemedicine, wearables, insurance/HMO integration, payment model/pricing tiers)

### Takeaway
WellNation Nigeria describes itself as a five-part "ecosystem integrator" (wellness/preventative care, telehealth, diagnostics, insurance, pharmacy) rather than a direct clinical-care provider, but the public site gives no specifics on actual product functionality, chronic-disease-management workflows, screening protocols, or a payment model — no pricing tiers or plan names are disclosed anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- Five core service components stated on the homepage: (1) **Wellness & Preventative Care** — "Personalized health risk assessments, wellness programs, and engagement tools designed to promote healthier lifestyles and early intervention"; (2) **Telehealth** — "Access to qualified clinicians through trusted telehealth platforms," licensed practitioners complying with Nigerian regulations; (3) **Diagnostics** — "Testing delivered through a nationwide network of accredited laboratories, supporting accuracy, quality, and appropriate turnaround times"; (4) **Insurance Solutions** — products developed with licensed Nigerian insurers and risk consultants; (5) **Pharmacy Support** — access to licensed pharmacy partners and medication services — [wellnationnigeria.com](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- Services page detail, Wellness & Health Risk Management: "Health risk assessments, Biometric screenings, Lifestyle coaching, Preventative wellness programs, Corporate wellness programs," explicitly stated to **not** replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment — [wellnationnigeria.com/services](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- Services page detail, Virtual Healthcare: "General consultations, Specialist referrals, Follow-up care coordination, Digital prescriptions where legally permitted," delivered through licensed clinicians and partner telehealth platforms (i.e., WellNation Nigeria does not appear to run its own telehealth clinician pool — it routes to partner platforms) — [wellnationnigeria.com/services](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- Services page detail, Diagnostics: "Routine blood panels, Preventative screenings, Corporate health checks, Specialized diagnostic testing," via partnerships with licensed, accredited laboratories (no specific labs named) — [wellnationnigeria.com/services](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- Services page detail, Insurance and Risk Integration: "Health insurance integration, Group corporate coverage solutions, Risk management advisory, Wellness-linked insurance engagement programs," working with licensed Nigerian insurance providers, brokers, and consultants (none named) — [wellnationnigeria.com/services](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- Corporate/Institutional Solutions: tailored offerings for "employers, SMEs, multinational corporations, healthcare providers, and government institutions" — [wellnationnigeria.com/services](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- No chronic-disease-specific program (e.g., hypertension/diabetes pathway), no mention of AI/automated triage, no mention of doctor tiers/escalation SLAs, no mention of a patient app's specific feature set (dashboards, vitals logging, med reminders) anywhere on the site — [wellnationnigeria.com/services](https://wellnationnigeria.com)
- The related pan-African sister brand "Well Nation Africa" (wellnationafrica.com, South Africa-based) offers a materially different, wearables/gamification-centric corporate wellness product: "connectivity to 150+ wearable fitness devices, apps and gamified challenges," goal setting/monitoring, nutrition/fitness tracking, rewards, and "individual and corporate wellness challenges," plus a proprietary "YOO Wellness" wearable device line — [wellnationafrica.com](https://www.wellnationafrica.com/), [Google Play listing search](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wellnation.africa&hl=en_NZ)

### Inferences
- WellNation Nigeria's own copy strongly implies a "coordination/marketplace" business model — aggregating third-party clinicians, labs, insurers, and pharmacies under one brand/UI — rather than Tarragon Health's model of directly employing its care-team doctors and building integrated clinical/escalation infrastructure. This is a meaningfully different competitive posture (integrator/broker vs. vertically-integrated care provider) that a comparison report should call out explicitly.
- No evidence was found that WellNation Nigeria itself (as opposed to its South African sister brand) has a live, functioning consumer app; the product-functionality claims on wellnationnigeria.com are stated at a marketing/aspirational level, not demonstrated via product UI, screenshots, or an app store listing under the Nigeria brand name.

### Gaps
- No specifics found on chronic disease management workflows (e.g., hypertension/diabetes pathways), screening cadences, escalation/doctor-response SLAs, or care-team structure.
- No specifics found on which labs, HMOs/insurers, or pharmacy chains are actual named partners (site explicitly declines to name any).
- No pricing tiers, plan names, or payment model (subscription vs. pay-per-service vs. employer-sponsored) were found anywhere.
- Could not confirm whether WellNation Nigeria has its own dedicated mobile app distinct from the "Well Nation Africa"/WNAfrica app — see Mobile Apps section below for what's confirmed there.
