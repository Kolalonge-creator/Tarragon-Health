# MyMedicalBank — Competitor Research Note

## Marketing website: homepage, product pages, pricing, about

### Takeaway
MyMedicalBank (mymedicalbank.com) positions itself as a broad healthcare *marketplace/booking platform* (not primarily a records vault, despite the name) spanning telemedicine, home-based care, diagnostics, health insurance comparison, caregiver training, a health-facility directory, and — as of 2026 — a free AI symptom/lab-result assistant. The site is a traditional server-rendered jQuery/Bootstrap/PHP site (not a modern SPA), positioned as "Nigeria's #1 Digital Health Platform," with pricing that is transactional/per-service rather than subscription-tiered (in contrast to Tarragon's flat per-service + chronic-programme model).

## Product functionality: platform features, PHR angle, chronic care, screening, integrations, payment model

### Takeaway
MyMedicalBank is structured as a modular "product suite" (per its own brochure) of at least 9 named components — MMB HomeCare, Health Market, Health Jobs, MyMedicalBank™ (EMR/PHR), Directory, Resources, Health Risk Calculators, Training, Community — plus a 2026-era AI Health Assistant layer. It is a general-purpose healthcare booking/marketplace + home-care staffing platform with light EMR/PHR and preventive-screening features, not a chronic-disease-management platform with escalation SLAs, doctor tiers, or a wearables pipeline of the kind Tarragon Health has built — no evidence was found of chronic-condition care plans, red-flag vitals escalation, or a doctor-tier authority ladder.

### Inferences
- MyMedicalBank's core differentiator, based on available evidence, is closer to a healthcare/home-care **staffing and booking marketplace** (verified health-worker supply, home visits, a jobs board for healthcare workers) than a personal-health-record vault, despite the "Bank" branding — the EMR/PHR ("MyMedicalBank™") is one of nine listed components, described mainly as an institutional tool for hospitals/health organisations rather than a patient-first longitudinal record with the kind of structured problem-list/allergy/medication-lifecycle architecture Tarragon documents in `docs/PATIENT_HEALTH_RECORD_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- No public evidence was found of chronic-disease-specific care pathways (e.g., a hypertension/diabetes protocol, red-flag vitals escalation, doctor-tier authority ladder, or an escalation SLA) — this is a structural difference from Tarragon Health's core wedge (chronic disease management as the primary product, not one item in a general marketplace).
- The commission-based (10%) + subscription-based (₦50,000/year) B2B monetization model described in 2021 press, alongside pure per-service consumer pricing, suggests MyMedicalBank monetizes primarily as a two-sided marketplace connecting supply (health workers/facilities) and demand (patients/corporates), rather than a payer-style recurring subscription model.

### Gaps
- No evidence found (positive or negative) of wearables/device integration, Bluetooth clinical-device pairing, or any chronic-condition dashboard — cannot say definitively whether this is simply unbuilt or just not marketed; treated as "not found."
- Could not confirm current (2026) headcount of onboarded health workers, states covered, or active-user counts — the only concrete figures found (800 professionals, 27 states) are from a 2021 interview and may be stale.
- Could not access a live "About" or "Features" subpage beyond the homepage, FAQ, pricing page, and brochure — a dedicated feature-by-feature breakdown page (if one exists) was not located separately from the homepage's own service tiles.

## Tech/backend signals: stack, hosting, analytics, engineering signals

### Takeaway
MyMedicalBank's public-facing web platform is a traditional server-rendered PHP application (Fat-Free Framework, a lightweight PHP micro-framework) using jQuery/Bootstrap-era front-end tooling, hosted behind Cloudflare, with Mixpanel product analytics (EU region) — this is a materially older/more traditional stack than a modern React/Next.js SPA, and stands in contrast to Tarragon Health's Next.js 16/TypeScript/Supabase stack. No public GitHub org, engineering blog, or public API/developer docs were found.

### Cited Findings
- HTTP response headers from `www.mymedicalbank.com` show: `server: cloudflare`, `x-powered-by: Fat-Free Framework`, a `PHPSESSID` cookie (confirming a PHP backend), and CORS headers allowing `GET, POST, PUT, DELETE, OPTIONS` — [direct HTTP header inspection of mymedicalbank.com, Sept 24 2026]
- Page source of the homepage shows: Bootstrap (`bootstrap.min.css`), `normalize.css`, Font Awesome (`font-awesome.min.css`, plus a Font Awesome Kit script `kit.fontawesome.com/ddc72f91d2.js`), `icomoon.css`, an `owl.carousel.css` carousel library, `prettyPhoto.css` lightbox library, `jquery-ui.css`, a custom `scrollbar.css`, `chartist.css` (a lightweight charting library), AOS (Animate On Scroll, `unpkg.com/aos@2.3.1`) for scroll animations, `jquery.dataTables` for tabular data, SweetAlert (`sweetalert.css`) for modals, Protip (`protip.min.css`) for tooltips, and Ladda (`ladda-themeless.min.css`) for button loading states — [direct HTML source inspection of mymedicalbank.com, Sept 24 2026]
- Product analytics: Mixpanel is loaded and initialized client-side with `autocapture: true` and `record_sessions_percent: 100` (i.e., full session recording is enabled), pointed at the EU API host `api-eu.mixpanel.com` — [direct HTML source inspection of mymedicalbank.com, Sept 24 2026]
- A Zendesk widget script block is present in the page `<head>` (commented "Start of mymedicalbank Zendesk Widget script" / "End of..."), indicating Zendesk is or was used for customer support — [direct HTML source inspection of mymedicalbank.com, Sept 24 2026]
- Google Sign-In is integrated (`apis.google.com/js/platform.js` plus a `google-signin-client_id` meta tag), and ShareThis social-sharing widgets are present (`platform-api.sharethis.com`) — [direct HTML source inspection of mymedicalbank.com, Sept 24 2026]
- A job posting for a "Full-Stack Engineering Intern (JavaScript & PHP)" confirms PHP as an active backend language for the engineering team, consistent with the Fat-Free Framework header signal — [Jobnow Nigeria listing for MyMedicalBank](https://jobnow.ng/full-stack-engineering-intern-javascript-php-at-mymedicalbank-limited/)
- A separate job posting exists for a "Medical Doctor at a Reputable Clinic" via MyMedicalBank Limited, indicating the company also directly staffs/recruits clinicians, not just software roles — [Jobnow Nigeria listing](https://jobnow.ng/medical-doctor-at-a-reputable-clinic-mymedicalbank-limited/)
- LinkedIn's "Technical Lead" role is held by a named individual, Ayoola Falola, per the brochure's management team table — [Brochure PDF](https://www.mymedicalbank.com/docs/Brochure_MyMedicalBank.pdf)

### Inferences
- The combination of Fat-Free Framework (PHP), jQuery-era UI libraries (owl.carousel, prettyPhoto, DataTables, SweetAlert, Ladda), and a `PHPSESSID`-based session model indicates the core web platform was built as a classic server-rendered multi-page PHP application rather than a modern API-driven single-page app — likely built incrementally since ~2016-2019 rather than re-platformed on a modern JS framework. This is a meaningfully different (older) engineering approach than Tarragon's Next.js/TypeScript/Supabase stack.
- Full session recording via Mixpanel (`record_sessions_percent: 100`) suggests the company invests in granular UX/funnel analysis, which is a reasonable practice for a marketplace business optimizing booking conversion.

### Gaps
- No public GitHub organization or open-source repositories were located for MyMedicalBank.
- No public engineering blog or public API/developer documentation was found.
- Could not determine mobile app tech stack (native Swift/Kotlin vs. React Native/Flutter) from public signals — no reverse-engineering of app binaries was attempted (out of scope / would require store binary inspection tools not used here).
- Could not confirm current hosting provider for backend/database infrastructure (only the Cloudflare edge/CDN layer and PHP application-server signature were visible from HTTP headers).
