# Presibo (presibo.com) — Competitive Intelligence Research Note

## Marketing website: homepage, product/pricing/about pages — copy, tone, UX patterns

### Takeaway
Presibo's site positions it as an "AI-powered continuous chronic care platform" for Nigerians with hypertension/diabetes, combining a mobile/web app, connected BP-cuff/glucometer hardware, and doctor video consults, priced as a consumer subscription (₦5,000/month individual tier) plus a B2B clinic SaaS tier and an agent-network onboarding fee. Tone is mission-driven/startup-earnest, built around a personal founding story (a family member's undetected hypertensive crisis) and heavy use of grant/accelerator badges (Tony Elumelu Foundation, Orange Corners, MIT Solve, LSETF) as trust signals rather than customer logos or press logos.

### Inferences

- Heavy reliance on grant/accelerator badges (TEF, Orange Corners, MIT Solve, LSETF) rather than customer counts, press logos, or investor names as homepage trust signals is consistent with an early-stage, pre-Series-A startup that has not yet closed institutional funding.
- The site's own "Family portal (1 member)" bundled into the ₦5,000 Individual tier, plus dedicated "Elder Care" and "Family" tiers, indicates Presibo is explicitly building family/caregiver access into its core product — a contrast point worth flagging against Tarragon Health's "individual enrolment only, no family plans" stance (per Tarragon's internal CLAUDE.md).

### Gaps
- Could not independently verify the Elder Care/Family tier pricing or the "POH Terminal"/device bundle economics beyond homepage mentions — a dedicated `/devices` and `/elder-care` page fetch was not completed in this research pass.
- Could not confirm whether Presibo has HMO/insurance integration — no mention found anywhere in this research.

## Product functionality — patient/clinician features, chronic disease support, screening, integrations, telemedicine, payment model

### Takeaway
Presibo's core product is a chronic-disease (hypertension + diabetes) continuous-monitoring app: manual/connected-device vitals logging (BP, glucose, weight, SpO2, temperature), AI-generated insights and "Health Score," medication/appointment reminders, in-app doctor chat and video consultations, and a "Doctor Chat" feature for prescription refills and result explanation — plus a separate B2B "Cloud-based EHR/workflow" product for clinics and a "Presibo Flow AI" triage tool aimed at frontline health workers. No lab-network, pharmacy-fulfillment, or HMO-integration features were found documented.

### Cited Findings
- Core loop described on homepage: log vitals → complete "personalized health challenges" for rewards → video consult with "certified physicians and fitness trainers" → receive "tailored AI health insights and meal analysis" — [presibo.com](https://presibo.com)
- Liners.com's product description (third-party, treat as secondary) adds: blood pressure and glucose tracking with trend charts, oxygen level and temperature logging, hydration reminders, a "Health Score" for weekly progress, secure video consults with physicians and fitness trainers, and a "Cloud-based EHR and workflow system for healthcare facilities" — [Liners.com](https://liners.com/presibo)
- Google Play app listing (app is published under the name **"Presibo GoTrack"**, package `com.presibotrack.app`, category Health & Fitness) describes itself: "Presibo is a health and lifestyle tracking app featuring: • Health metrics tracking • Doctor chat • Video consultations • Appointment booking • Fitness & nutrition tracking • Personalized health insights" — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.presibotrack.app)
- Founder's dev.to technical post frames the intended data/clinical flow explicitly as: "Device → AI → Triage → Human Response → Care," with a stated design principle that "AI should assist healthcare professionals, not remove them from the loop" — [dev.to](https://dev.to/jerrynwobodo/building-presibo-the-technology-behind-continuous-healthcare-n22)
- "Presibo Flow AI" is described in press as "an intelligent triage and diagnostics assistant designed to empower frontline health workers with real-time decision-making tools," reportedly supporting voice/text symptom reporting in Yoruba, Igbo, and Hausa for instant triage — [ThisDayLive](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/12/09/presibo-founder-tony-elumelu-foundations-5000-grant-recipient-jerry-nwobodo-accelerates-africas-digital-health-transformation-with-ai-powered-automation/); [Jerry Nwobodo bio page](https://presibo.com/jerry-nwobodo)
- Presibo's MIT Solve 2024 submission described three core components: telemedicine (remote consults), "AI-powered emergency response using GPS technology for rapid dispatch," and cloud-based patient data management for cross-provider care coordination — targeting patients, healthcare professionals, medical facilities, AND ambulance operators as four distinct stakeholder groups — [MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/87441)
- MIT Solve submission listed the tech stack as "JavaScript, Python, AI, GPS, IoT, and cloud infrastructure with React/React Native interfaces" — [MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/87441)
- MIT Solve submission stated MVP-stage traction: 3,750 enrolled users, 23 partnered doctors, 9 healthcare facility partnerships across Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, with a planned Kenya expansion "within one year" — [MIT Solve](https://solve.mit.edu/solutions/87441)
- Founder's dev.to post states Presibo "started as a software idea during COVID-19, initially focused on prescriptions" before pivoting to "continuity of care," on the reasoning that "chronic diseases like hypertension and diabetes do not work on appointment schedules" — [dev.to](https://dev.to/jerrynwobodo/building-presibo-the-technology-behind-continuous-healthcare-n22)

### Inferences
- The MIT Solve submission's emphasis on GPS-dispatched emergency/ambulance response and ambulance operators as a named stakeholder group is a materially different scope than what the current marketing site emphasizes (daily chronic-vitals tracking) — this may reflect an earlier/broader product vision (2024 submission) that has since narrowed toward the chronic-care subscription product now live on the site, or it may be a still-active but unmarketed capability. Treat the emergency-dispatch claim as unconfirmed against the current live product.
- The gap between the MIT Solve-reported 3,750 users / 23 doctors (2024 figures) and the Google Play app's apparent installs (see Mobile Apps section — extremely low volume) suggests either most usage happens via a different channel (WhatsApp, web app, or an older/different app build) or that reported 2024 traction figures have not been sustained/reflected in current app-store presence.

### Gaps
- No documented lab-network, pharmacy-fulfillment/delivery, or HMO/insurance-integration features found anywhere (site, press, MIT Solve, dev.to) — likely genuinely absent from the product, but cannot rule out an unmarketed capability.
- No detail found on how the "AI insights"/triage engine actually works (what model, what thresholds, human-in-the-loop specifics beyond the "Device → AI → Triage → Human Response → Care" slogan).
- Could not confirm current (2026) user/doctor/facility counts — only the 2024 MIT Solve figures were found.

- No public GitHub org, no engineering blog beyond the single dev.to post, and no developer/API documentation found — consistent with a small, resource-constrained early-stage team rather than a company investing in public developer-relations or open technical transparency.
