# S07 research: Today screen, BP logging, trends and reminders (short)

Studied for behaviour only, nothing copied. Platforms from spec line 68 and Module 4 rows 4.1 to 4.7: Noom, MyTherapy, MyFitnessPal, DarioHealth, Propeller Health, Omada Health, Strava, Oura, WHOOP, CareClinic. Public pages only; where a vendor publishes nothing the line says UNKNOWN. Several help-centre pages (Noom, MyFitnessPal) were blocked, so those points come from search summaries and are marked UNVERIFIED. Every clinical number below is PROPOSED: it goes in versioned configuration (`packages/shared/src/proposed-config`) and needs Chief Medical Officer sign-off (INV-16). Nothing here is a final threshold.

## 1. Today screen (row 4.1)

- **MyTherapy** shows one chronological timeline of due medicines, measurements and symptom prompts. Closest to our need. Adopted: one list, one highlighted next step.
- **Noom** shows a card list for the day (lesson, logging, weight). A public teardown says new users see too many tasks at once. Adopted the single "next step" emphasis; not the long card stack.
- **MyFitnessPal** redesigned its Today tab in 2026 and users complained of extra taps and a hidden diary (UNVERIFIED, press coverage). Rule adopted: the BP log is at most 2 taps from Today.
- Unsuitable for Nigeria: food, calorie and weight cards (Western databases, many patients own no scale). Empty state must still say what to do next with no data and no network.

## 2. Quick log (row 4.2)

- **MyFitnessPal** has a minimal "Quick Add", recents first, and queues logs when offline (UNVERIFIED). Adopted: BP only on the first screen, last values as a hint, save to the phone first (already built in S06).
- **CareClinic** records body position, place and a "what I was doing" note per reading. Adopted as optional fields only. Place is not captured (data minimisation, NDPA).
- **DarioHealth** is device-first and markets "no manual entry". Unsuitable: our patients type numbers. Source stays labelled `manual` or `device`; the two are never merged silently.
- Unsafe: a reward or streak that is higher for "better" numbers. A high reading counts as done.

## 3. Guided technique and plausibility (row 4.3)

- **Dario BP guide** (public PDF): rest 10 minutes, no caffeine, tobacco or exercise for 30 minutes, cuff at heart height, same arm, no talking, 5 to 10 minutes between repeats. **CareClinic**: sit quietly 5 minutes. Adopted as a short checklist (HLP-003). PROPOSED default: 5 minute rest, with an honest note that longer is better.
- Clinical sources (secondary read; the Nigerian guideline PDF was not parsed, so quote it only after a human read): AHA/AMA self-measured BP statement, ISH 2020, ESH 2023, WHO HEARTS. Agreed: validated upper-arm cuff, back supported, feet flat, empty bladder. Protocol: 2 readings at least 1 minute apart, morning and evening, 7 days; at least 3 days is acceptable.
- **Omada** publishes an averaging gate: at least 3 readings over at least 3 days, or at least 4 over at least 2 days. Adopted: below the gate the app says "not enough readings yet", never a thin average.
- **Plausibility**: none of the four vendors publishes digit-transposition or impossible-value checks. Dario's cuff measuring range (systolic 60-255, diastolic 30-195, pulse 40-199) shows what a consumer device can physically report. The live app already rejects above 260/160 and requires systolic above diastolic, justified in `apps/web/src/lib/validation/vitals.ts` by TH-CP-HTN-001 section 5.4. The spec text (line 311) is looser (300/200). See OQ-66.
- **Home thresholds differ from clinic thresholds**: office 140/90 (WHO, ISH, Nigeria); home about 135/85 (ISH), 135/75 (ESH), 130/80 (AHA/ACC). Contested, so stored as a configurable pair, never one hard-coded number.
- Unsafe: reusing US 130/80 as the only target; a default "personal band" shown as if a clinician set it. Label it "starting suggestion, not yet confirmed" until `set_by` and `set_at` exist.
- Severe values (180/120 or above): AHA public guidance is emergency if symptoms, otherwise rest, recheck, same-day contact. This is grading, owned by S11 and S12 (OQ-67); S07 only captures the symptoms.

## 4. Trends (row 4.6)

- **Oura and WHOOP** show personal baselines, three bands, and an explicit "pending or unscorable" state in their APIs (WHOOP `score_state`). Adopted: an explicit "not enough data" state and one fixed axis for 7 and 30 days.
- Academic home-BP visualisation work (BMC Med Inform Decis Mak 2021) found a shaded goal band reads better than two lines, and smoothing reduces weight on outliers. Adopted: shaded band plus a text label; but on sparse data show raw dots plus a daily mean and no smoothing curve, because a curve implies readings never taken.
- Already built (Design Phase 1, #865): a Skia 7 and 30 day chart (`ui/kit/TrendChart.tsx`, `lib/bp-trend.ts`) with dashed lines at the app's above-target levels and tap-to-inspect. S07 extends it rather than adding a second chart: personal target band, averaging gate, sparse-data and morning/evening rules below, and a list alternative.
- Rules adopted: break the line across gaps of more than 2 days; fewer than 3 readings shows a list, not a trend; label averages by count ("average of 12 readings"); morning and evening as separate markers; out-of-band points use a different shape, not colour alone; blue and orange, not red and green; summary sentence above the chart and a "show as list" toggle for screen readers; axis text at least 12 sp; at most about 60 points; no animation.
- Wording: "above your target range", never "uncontrolled". An amber state is never drawn as a red alarm.
- Daily readiness for wearable users (row 4.6, second half) is deferred. It is optional, needs wearable data most patients do not have, and Oura and WHOOP show the risks (opaque score, score anxiety). It must never feed a safety alert.

## 5. Reminders (rows 4.1, 4.5; spec 8.3)

- **MyTherapy**: reminders repeat until taken or skipped, snooze defaults to 30 minutes, four states (taken, skipped, snoozed, missed), users ask for "reschedule". Adopted the four states plus scheduled, stored as events. Not adopted: unbounded repeats.
- **Propeller**: nudges and a weekly summary; escalation to a coordinator for frequent bad events. Adopted the weekly summary idea only; escalation is S12.
- Local notifications so they work offline. Constraints from Expo SDK 54 docs: iOS keeps only 64 pending, so schedule a rolling window and top it up; Android needs an exact-alarm permission for exact times and a channel created before scheduling.
- Unsuitable for Nigeria: Tecno, Infinix and Itel battery managers can delay or drop alarms (general knowledge, NOT verified for these models). Mitigation: derive "overdue" from the schedule, not from delivery; test on real hardware; never describe a reminder as a safety guarantee.
- INV-07: a notification never names a condition, reading or result. The live dose reminder names the medicine (see OQ-68).

## 6. Goals and streaks (row 4.5)

- **Noom** gives one small daily reward for finishing 3 tasks. **Strava** counts a weekly streak and restores it on late sync (banners cannot be turned off). **MyFitnessPal** has a login streak that needs a network.
- Harm evidence: a 2017 survey found 73 percent of 105 people with eating disorders said MyFitnessPal contributed; a 2025 review of 27 studies finds the link consistent but not proven, pointing at goal-setting, gamification and extreme targets. Strava segment chasing and social comparison are linked to pressure.
- Adopted: weekly "days done this week" with a best-ever figure and a grace window; a freeze earned (for example 1 per 7 days, capped at 2) and shown as a freeze, never as a reading; late entry counts but the real entry time is stamped; never a red zero; the patient can turn it off. Lally 2010: one missed day does not break habit formation.
- Avoid: leaderboards, kudos, sharing, calorie or weight framing, a streak that fails when the phone is offline.

## 7. Offline entry and edit history (row 4.7)

- **CareClinic** is the named reference, but its public pages show no audit trail, edit history, duplicate handling or offline queue (UNKNOWN). No vendor documents these, so S07 designs from first principles on top of S06.
- Our rule: vitals are append-only on the server; a correction goes through the existing `data_correction_requests` and `record_corrections`; an unsynced local row can be removed from the phone (S06). The patient sees the history of her own corrections.
- **Strava** stores UTC time, local time and timezone on every activity and dedupes by an external id. Matches what S05 and S06 already do (`client_reading_id`, `client_recorded_at`). Streak and Today day-boundaries bucket on a stored `local_date` in Africa/Lagos so a skewed clock cannot move a day.

## 8. Voice logging (row 4.4)

Nigeria-specific, no platform reference. S07 leaves an interface only. Any Nigerian-language voice needs native review (the OQ-61 pattern; D-13: English and Pidgin at launch).

## 9. Reusable standards and code (permissive only)

- FHIR R4 vital-signs BP profile (LOINC 85354-9, systolic 8480-6, diastolic 8462-4, UCUM mm[Hg]): export mapping later, not the on-device model.
- Open mHealth blood-pressure schema: field names only; its licence was not confirmed in this pass, check before reuse.
- No new native dependency is needed: expo-sqlite, expo-notifications, Skia, svg, reanimated, haptics and `@tarragon/i18n` are installed. Habitica is GPL and CHT is AGPL: concepts only. Do not copy any vendor's code, text, badges or visuals.
