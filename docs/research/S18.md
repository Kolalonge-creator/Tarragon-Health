## Second pass (2026-10-06): full pages, where they could be read
Labels: **read** = the page was fetched and read; **snippet** = a search result only; **inferred** = reasoning. Pages that blocked the fetcher (Doctolib help centre and blog, the Hims 10-K, several PDFs) are snippet-level.

**Availability and pay.**
- Amwell Medical Group (read, providers.amwell.com/faq and /programs/urgent-care): its continuity programme requires at least 10 declared hours a week inside a fixed Monday to Friday band; urgent care has an Associate tier with no time requirement and a Partner tier with a set weekly schedule at a higher rate, so committed availability is paid more. Adopted: a displayed (not enforced) pilot floor of 10 declared hours a week for a contracted clinician (`contracted_min_declared_hours_per_week`, PROPOSED).
- Wheel (read): contractors set their own schedule with no stated minimum; one NCQA-aligned credentialing pass; malpractice cover provided. OpenLoop (read): clinicians own availability including evenings and weekends; a 20 hour weekly minimum appears in a snippet only and is not used.
- Doctolib (snippet): availability is built from opening hours, absences, visit-type rules and shared resources; an absence can keep or move booked appointments and can name a replacement. Matches the typed-block model and the swap and reassignment paths.

**Continuity and capacity.**
- Omada (read): a personal coach on day one who stays; it argues high coach-to-member ratios dilute quality and publishes no ratio. Adopted: the named lead; the cap of 60 stays our own configurable number, to be defended with measured response times.
- Babylon (read, CQC report PDF, 2019): salaried GPs plus per-session locums, a rota with senior staff on call 24/7, licence, appraisal and training checks and test consultations before a clinician saw patients, indemnity provided, and the service itself said it was not best placed for long-term conditions or monitored drugs. Lesson for a hypertension pack, which is the opposite use case: continuity and monitoring rules must be stricter than a consult service's.
- Matching on skills and licence only is what Wheel and Amwell publish; our reliability tiebreak is our own addition and is kept as the last tiebreak only.

**Rota rules (NHS Employers 2016 doctors-in-training FAQ, read).** 72 hours in any 168; at most 7 consecutive shifts then 48 hours rest; at most 4 consecutive long (over 10 hour) shifts; 13 hour shifts and 11 hours rest (on-call shifts up to 24 hours); at most 3 rostered on-calls in 7 days except by agreement. Adopted as configurable warnings with a written override: 11 hours rest, 3 shifts in 7 days, **72 hours in 7 days and at most 4 long shifts in 7 days (added in this pass)**. Not adopted: rest after a disturbed night (the FAQ wording only hints at it).

**Nigeria (second pass).**
- No telemedicine operator licence exists; a practitioner on a platform must hold MDCN registration (read, a 2025 legal guide). The Code of Medical Ethics names continuity of management and the registration status of whoever is consulted as risk areas (read). A vendor blog's claims of a telemedicine registration and a fixed indemnity amount are unverified and are not encoded.
- Annual practising licence runs to 31 December (read, news report of a 2025 grace to 7 January; snippets for fees). Existing S15 eligibility already stops a rota slot when a licence is not valid through the shift.
- The NARD ultimatum of 1 October 2026 (read) and a strike or threat in each recent quarter mean the rota must not rely on resident doctors during an ultimatum; recorded as OQ-135.
- Doctors' density about 4 per 10,000 and about 55,000 practising (read) supports not quoting a public ratio.
- The MDCN wording on treating family or friends was not retrievable; the conflict model rests on the GMC position and the self-declared plus hand-back plus clinical-lead mechanism.

**Changes made because of the second pass.** The weekly-hours and long-shift fatigue warnings; the displayed weekly declared-hours floor; an urgent-cover swap that applies on acceptance when the shift starts within 4 hours (a rota must not stall on a reviewer; it is audited and the reviewers are told at once); a fix so reviewer notices from the rota link to the rota rather than the credentialing pages.

**Still unpublished.** Re-credentialing cadence for the platforms; any published caseload; any published alert-response standard; conflict-of-interest rules between clinician and patient; fatigue and swap rules for any platform (our design here is original); Nigerian operator rotas, ratios and on-call pay.
