# S18 research: lead clinician, availability and rota

Studied for behaviour only; nothing copied. Evidence is search-snippet level (US-only search tool), so thin where marked. Date 2026-10-06.


Evidence is snippet-level, US-only search. Where nothing is published it says so; those parts of the design are original, not benchmarked.

| Reference | What is documented | Take for S18 |
|---|---|---|
| Wheel | Fixed weekly hour commitments incl. weekend duty; pay per visit or hour, terms disclosed up front; staffing layer so no continuity model | ADOPT declared hour blocks, terms visible before commit. REJECT per-visit-only pay for on-call (pays nothing while waiting) |
| OpenLoop | Clinicians self-set schedule; scheduler matches availability to urgency; per-consult pay incl. a small paid no-show | ADOPT self-declared blocks. ADAPT a paid no-show (S30 decision, not S18) |
| Teladoc / Amwell / MDLIVE | Moved from open on-demand to shift scheduling; Amwell 2-hour blocks; anonymous queue | ADOPT 2-hour block granularity. REJECT anonymous queue as the model for care-pack patients |
| Omada | Named coach on day one, kept for the whole programme; cardiometabolic specialist on top for hypertension; caseload cap not published | ADOPT named lead at enrolment, kept unless a trigger fires. ADAPT: Omada's lead is a coach, ours is a clinician so we add reassignment triggers |
| Doctolib | Recurring typed "openings", bookings separate, slots = openings minus bookings, per-type caps | ADOPT as data model for `availability_blocks` (typed, capped, computed capacity) |
| Hims & Hers | Physician-owned medical groups, group malpractice cover as a recruiting lever | ADOPT group indemnity as a later lever (S15 already tracks it). REJECT the US legal structure |
| Babylon (lesson) | Collapsed 2023; regulator objected to expansion on safety and weak integration; growth outran clinical capacity | Capacity-gated enrolment: expose "lead capacity available" so checkout and the go-live guard can refuse to sell past declared capacity. Never let sales outrun leads |
| Clafiya, Mobihealth, Kangpe | Nurse/CHO-led (Clafiya), low-bandwidth consults; nothing published on rota, caps, COI, reassignment | Design from first principles. Not benchmarkable |
| PagerDuty (on-call tooling) | Escalation as ordered rules with ack timeouts; layered rotations, time-bound overrides; **if nobody is on call, no incident is created** | ADOPT overrides with acceptance, ordered escalation. REJECT silent-uncovered: for us uncovered fails loudly (ops incident + clinical lead) |
| NHS 2016 junior-doctor rules | 11 h rest, max 7 consecutive shifts, max 3 on-calls per 7 days, max 13 h shift | ADOPT as configurable warnings (not Nigerian norms; clinical lead reviews). Feeds OQ-112 working hours / post-call rest |
| Continuity evidence | Observational links between usual-provider continuity and BP control; meta-analysis supports team-based care | Supports a lead plus team, not a 1:1 promise. Matches CLAUDE.md: never promise one continuous doctor |
| Nigeria context | MDCN licence annual (renew by 31 Dec), CPD required; NARD strikes (Nov 2025, announced again Apr 2026); dual jobs and power/data cuts are inference only | Backup on every on-call slot; coverage-forecast view; push then email fallback (founder OQ-113); licence expiry is an automatic reassignment trigger |
| Abridge, Suki, Nabla, Ambience, Corti, Glooko | Scribe and device vendors | Not relevant to S18 (S23 and S70). Skipped on purpose |
| Panel-size ratios (Omada, Cadence, Livongo etc.) | None published | `max_lead_patients` stays config (PROPOSED 60). Do not cite any ratio. Calibrate from observed load |
| Conflict-of-interest | GMC: avoid treating anyone with a close personal relationship. No platform publishes its exclusion mechanism | Self-declared plus hand-back-raised conflicts, checked at assign, reassign, claim and on-call |

Not verified: Amion, QGenda, Opsgenie, Shiftboard, MDCN COI wording, Omada caseload caps.
