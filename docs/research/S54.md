# S54 research: Module 8, pharmacy and further detail (rows 8.9 to 8.16)

Date 2026-10-07. Sources: the S08 and S28 research (`docs/research/S08.md`, `docs/research/S28.md`), the two competitor reviews of 2026-10, the S51-S55 plan, and public product descriptions as known to the author. Nothing here was hands-on tested and no price or licence data was fetched. Reference platforms were studied for behaviour only; nothing is copied.

**8.9 Send a prescription to a chosen partner pharmacy, with prices compared first.** Yodawy and mPharma are the references (Yodawy: nearest-pharmacy ordering, chronic prescription tracking, monthly refill, insurance; mPharma/Mutti: a pharmacy network and price-lock for members). Complaints: a comparison that ranks by what the platform earns, hidden fees, stock that is stale by the time the patient arrives, and a prescription that can only be filled at one place. Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria: ranking by margin, home delivery (founder rule D5), a price shown for a medicine the pharmacy does not stock, a comparison that treats a pack of 30 and a pack of 10 as equal. Tarragon version: the patient always may download the signed prescription and take it to any pharmacy (unchanged and prominent); when partner pharmacies exist, the patient sees a price and stock comparison across verified, licensed partners and then chooses one in the S28 chooser (the patient chooses, never the clinician; S28 founder decision 2026-10-07). The comparison is ordered by total price then name and nothing in it, or anywhere a clinician can read, carries an earning.

**8.10 Refill countdown and running-low reminder tied to the user's pharmacy.** Medisafe and Yodawy remind from remaining pills and a refill date; the complaint is a reminder that does not say where to collect. S08 built the countdown from the pill count and the refill date. S54 adds the chosen pharmacy to the refill notice payload (the notice text stays generic, INV-07) and shows "collect at X" in the app.

**8.11 Verified-batch sourcing and partner quality rules.** mPharma and Medsaf frame counterfeit control as a sourcing rule: buy only from registered suppliers and trace batches. Unsuitable here: Tarragon cannot verify a batch itself and must not imply it. Tarragon version: a partner cannot have an active, comparable listing unless its licence is verified and in date and it has attested that it sources only from NAFDAC-registered suppliers (attestation recorded with who and when); a `verified_batch` flag on a price row can only be set by an admin or partner manager, never by the pharmacy itself, and the patient screen says what it means in plain words ("checked by Tarragon staff against the supplier paperwork"), never "genuine".

**8.12 Pharmacist chat for medicine questions.** Yodawy lists pharmacist consultation; Medisafe has none. Complaints in similar services: chat used for diagnosis, pharmacists reading far more of the record than the question needs, no record of what was said, no emergency handling. Tarragon version: a thread between a patient and a pharmacist of one partner pharmacy about a medicine, in the app only (WhatsApp is removed, F-02), the pharmacist sees only the messages and the medicine named, every pharmacist read is audited (INV-10), a deterministic red-flag screen on the patient's words points to the emergency steps, and a pharmacist can hand a question to the patient's care team through the existing care messages. Messages never go in a notification.

**8.13 Complex schedules.** Built in S08 (several times a day, alternate days, tapers, with-food notes). No change.

**8.14 Pill count alongside the refill countdown.** Built in S08. Known gap: a taper needs a manual count. No change.

**8.15 Schedules for dependants under their own profiles.** Medisafe manages family profiles from one account. Tarragon built guardian logging (S08) but the phone plans reminders and the catch-up sheet for the signed-in person only (OQ-70, OQ-76). S54 plans reminders for every dependant the guardian manages, with the dependant's first name and no medicine (INV-07 allows a first name), and asks the guardian the catch-up questions per dependant.

**8.16 Clinicians never see which pharmacy earns Tarragon more.** Nigeria-specific. Fixed first in the S53 pre-fix (column grants, admin view, ledger limited to admin and finance). S54 adds the comparison, which must not leak the same thing: it returns price, stock, a verified-batch flag and distance fields only, and the proof reads its output as a patient and a clinician and looks for any earning column.

**D5 No home delivery.** The founder removed delivery. Live counts were read before changing anything (see the migration header). Patients see pickup only, no delivery fee is added, the staff courier controls are hidden, and a CHECK keeps `fulfilment_method` at pickup. Tables and functions stay (OQ-16).
