# S43 research: Health Passport, record, vaccination, emergency card (functions 2.1 to 2.9)

Method: reused the competitor reviews already in the plan (`docs/plans/S41-S45-build-plan.md` section 2 Module 2, `docs/research/competitor-review-global-2026-10.md`, `competitor-review-africa-2026-10.md`) and the CMO sign-off pack (`docs/plans/S41-S45-cmo-signoff-pack.md` section B). No new web research was run in this session. Evidence tags: **[P]** from the plan documents (themselves sourced), **[M]** my reading of the live platform, **[D]** a decision of this session. Nothing here is copied from a product; the designs are original.

## 2.1 Single source-labelled timeline (Apple Health, Eka Care)
- Best products label every sample with where it came from, so a reader can tell a measured value from a typed one **[P]**.
- Complaint: a flat feed that mixes a clinician's note with a guess. Unsafe: a label the writer can choose (a patient could call their own entry "from a clinician").
- Nigeria: shared phones and paper records mean provenance matters more than polish.
- **Premium version:** one `trust_tier` on every item (lab-pushed, clinician, device, patient, read from a photo and unconfirmed, read from a photo and confirmed, imported, system), derived by a trigger from the item's real source and a real clinician actor, never accepted from a writer **[D]**. Timeline coverage audited: vitals, prescriptions, documents, vaccinations, conditions, encounters, messages all feed it; symptoms and procedures added **[M]**.

## 2.2 History: conditions, allergies, procedures, family history, blood group, genotype (CareClinic; Nigeria-specific)
- Designed from the requirement. Conditions, allergies and blood profile already exist with provenance **[M]**; procedures did not exist, and family history had no screen at all **[M]**.
- Risk: a patient typing "verified by clinician" into their own row. Risk: a clinician-confirmed entry silently deleted by the patient.
- **Premium version:** a patient-written row cannot claim a clinician source or a verification (database trigger); editing a verified item un-verifies it; removal is a tombstone kept for audit; staff read through one audited function **[D]**.

## 2.3 Photo capture of paper, with confirmed extraction (Eka Care)
- Best products extract reports, prescriptions and vaccine certificates from photos **[P]**. Lesson recorded in the plan: never auto-commit OCR, keep the original, confirm per field **[P]**.
- Complaints: handwriting is misread with high confidence; a wrong value looks official once it is in the record. Unsafe: letting any extracted number reach alerts or a risk score.
- Nigeria: HEIC photos from iPhones (handled by the existing converter), poor light, low bandwidth (photo is stored first, reading is optional), shared phones.
- **Premium version:** suggestions only in `patient_documents.extracted`; the patient accepts or edits each field; unaccepted fields and the raw text are dropped; a rejected reading leaves no values; nothing outside the capture module may read the table (repo scan test); the model call is a registered system (AI-018) behind a go-live guard that starts off **[D]**.

## 2.4 Symptom journal linked to the timeline and checker sessions (CareClinic, MyTherapy)
- Both tools make logging fast and show patterns **[P]**. Complaint: journals that cannot be taken to a clinician.
- The platform already logs symptoms offline-first with a client id **[M]**. Gap was the link to the checker session and the timeline.
- **Premium version:** `symptoms.assessment_id`, a timeline row per entry, and a journal read that shows the triage grade the deterministic engine gave (never a model) **[D]**.

## 2.5 Multi-year biomarker trends with reference and target ranges (Function Health, InsideTracker)
- Best products chart every analyte over years **[P]**. The InsideTracker complaint, recorded in the plan: a global "optimal" band medicalises normal variation **[P]**. Founder asked for this to be built.
- Unsafe: joining different units into one line, drawing a range the lab did not send, showing a held or withheld result, showing a sensitive positive.
- **Premium version:** the laboratory's own range on every point; a target only ever labelled "your care team's target" and only when an active care plan sets it; mixed units grouped and flagged, never converted; released and non-withdrawn results only; no trend for a sensitive positive; the screen says how a result moved, never what it means **[D]**.

## 2.6 Vaccination registry against the national schedule, reminders free on every account (Tarragon decision)
- Designed from the requirement. The only verified source in the pack is an undated NPHCDA table; HPV and typhoid conflict between sources **[P]**.
- Unsafe: reminders from an unsigned schedule; a global rule for R21 malaria, which is phased by state.
- **Premium version:** the schedule is versioned data with a CMO-signed row; version 2 is an unsigned draft with HPV two doses and no typhoid (founder, 2026-10-07); the live reminder cron does nothing until a signed schedule carrying that config is active; R21 is a per-state flag **[D]**. The registry stays free: no plan or feature check was found on the vaccination tables, queries or screens (a search, not an exhaustive audit) **[M]**.

## 2.7 Emergency card: offline, printable, QR, lock screen (Apple Medical ID; Nigeria-specific)
- Apple Medical ID puts the card on the lock screen **[P]**. Risk on a shared phone: anyone holding it sees the person's health details **[P]**.
- Already built: printable card, plain-text QR, offline cache, opt-in live link **[M]**. Gap: the patient could not choose the fields.
- **Premium version:** patient-chosen fields applied to the printed card, the QR text, the live link (inside the database, so it cannot be read around) and the phone's offline cache; "use the minimum" in one tap; lock-screen presence is a separate opt-in, off by default, with the shared-phone warning beside it **[D]**. The lock-screen widget itself needs a native build and is not built.

## 2.8 Time-limited share link with expiry, log, revocation (Eka Care, Lifen)
- Plan lesson: links travel over WhatsApp, so short expiry, view cap and optional PIN **[P]**. Estonia's access log is the model for showing the owner who looked **[P]**.
- Found in the live S09 function: the vitals section raised on every opening (a column name that does not exist) and the lab section compared a status to a value that is not in its enum. Neither had ever been exercised **[M]**.
- **Premium version:** a plain GET only previews (a link unfurler or mail scanner spends no view and reads no record), only a deliberate POST opens it; token held only as a hash; 72 hour default from versioned config; optional PIN with lockout; view cap counted under a row lock; instant revoke; every outcome logged and shown to the owner; expired, revoked or capped links answer HTTP 410; mental health and reproductive health have no section in the closed set (a medicine or result inside a general section is not classified by what it is for, so that exclusion is partial, see OQ-S43-4) **[D]**.

## 2.9 Doctor summary PDF for any facility (CareClinic)
- Printable reports are a strength of the journalling apps **[P]**. A facility that does not know the patient needs one page it can trust and photocopy.
- **Premium version:** one A4 page, black on white, no logo or fills; every line tagged with who stands behind it; has no mental or reproductive section (items inside the medicines and results lists are not classified, OQ-S43-4); released lab results only, with the lab's range **[D]**.

## Shared lessons carried into the design
- Provenance is a database fact, not a UI label.
- The safe default for anything that leaves the patient's control is off, short and revocable.
- Features that depend on an unsigned clinical decision (vaccination reminders, the reading model) are inert until the right person signs or switches them.
