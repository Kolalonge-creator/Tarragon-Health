# S56 research note: Module 10 (1 of 2), check-ins, screening, crisis, human care

Sources: `docs/design/S55-S60-build-plan.md` section 4.2 (two web research passes, tagged V verified by a fetched or searched source, U unverified), `docs/research/competitor-review-global-2026-10.md`, `docs/research/competitor-review-africa-2026-10.md`. No product was installed or copied. Anything tagged U is a lead to check, not a fact.

## 10.1 Check-ins shown beside BP and sleep (Wysa, Samsung Health)
- Wysa and Samsung Health put mood in a daily tap and show it as a trend line next to activity and sleep (U, app-store descriptions only). Both let a person tag why (work, family), which makes the trend readable without free text.
- Complaints (U): mood scores become a number to judge yourself by; reminders that name the app's purpose are visible on a lock screen. Oura-style single scores cause "orthosomnia" (U), so the plan says no single sleep score.
- Unsuitable here: charts on a small low-data phone, shared handsets, no way to hide a mood entry from a family member.
- Tarragon version: a fixed tag list (not free text, so a tag can never carry a diagnosis), mood and stress beside blood pressure and sleep as a plain table per day (no chart, no cause claimed, no score), the data stays patient-private and never feeds a risk score, and shared-phone mode (hidden by default, quick-hide, optional local PIN, notifications that name nothing).

## 10.2 PHQ-9 and GAD-7 with a follow-up pathway and change over time (Wysa, HelloBetter)
- Wysa and HelloBetter repeat PHQ-9 and GAD-7 on a schedule and show the change; HelloBetter pairs it with a guided programme (U). Wysa reports to NHS DCB0129 and has a human coach tier (V).
- Complaints (U): a high score with no human follow-up; cut-offs presented as diagnoses.
- Unsuitable here: assuming a person will act on a number alone; a score on a screen a family member may see.
- Tarragon version: the existing governed screens stay (cadences, bands, concern classification, EPDS); add `questionnaire.completed` on the outbox (ids only), a real `clinical_tasks` follow-up for a moderate or high result (existing type `symptom_review`, DRAFT due times in PROPOSED config, behind a CMO-switched guard that is OFF), and change over time as the raw difference only. No "reliable change" or "clinically significant" label, because that is a threshold the CMO has not signed.

## 10.3 Crisis pathway (Wysa)
- Wysa keeps an always-visible SOS to local helplines and routes self-harm to a human tier (V). Woebot's consumer app closed 30 June 2025, with the founder citing regulatory uncertainty for LLM chatbots (V), which is the reason there is no model anywhere in this path.
- Complaints (U): helplines that no longer answer; a card behind three taps. Nigerian lines (SURPIN, MANI) exist but the numbers in the research came from directory listings (U) and must be dialled before use.
- Unsuitable here: a number shown as verified that nobody phoned; a card that needs data to open; promising a callback time nobody has staffed.
- Tarragon version: the card is on the same screen as the result, bundled in the binary (INV-06), with the emergency number, a nearest-hospital action and no helpline numbers: founder decision 2026-10-07, there are no usable crisis helplines in Nigeria, so the card says go to the nearest hospital now (helplines can be added later behind a verified-only gate). The callback time is shown only once the CMO confirms it. F1 already creates the class 1 task, the urgent event and the neutral page; this session reuses that and adds none.

## 10.13 Booking hand-off and human care (Nigeria-specific)
- No reference platform was studied for this row (the spec marks it Nigeria-specific); designed from the requirement: a positive screen should lead to a person without the patient re-telling the story.
- Tarragon version: the patient chooses to send their latest mood or anxiety screen to the care team; that creates a task (existing type `admin_clinical`) and a hand-off row a tied clinician reads through the audited function. Onward referral to local services (Module 15) is not built, so the hand-off row is the documented seam it will read (`state = 'open'`).

## Privacy findings that shaped the build (not competitor-sourced)
- BetterHelp paid $7.8M to settle FTC claims over sharing sensitive data with advertising platforms (V). Hence the scan test that no analytics or ad SDK is reachable from a wellbeing screen.
- Reading this repository found five mental-health tables readable by every org staff account, a clinician alert and an emergency event carrying the screen's band and the words "thoughts of self-harm" (readable by org staff and by a Care Circle supporter holding only medical_history), a sponsor report that counted such an alert as a clinical review, and an approval function that let any staff account approve a psychiatry booking. All are closed in this session (see `docs/design/S56.md`).
