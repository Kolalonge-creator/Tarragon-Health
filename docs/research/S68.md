No product page was opened for Ovia, BabyCenter, Huckleberry or Glow in this session: the lines on them are the build plan's, tagged U. ## 16.9 Postnatal checks, postnatal depression screening, breastfeeding support and log
- Ovia and BabyCenter cover postpartum recovery, feed logging and baby milestones; Huckleberry, Baby Tracker and Glow Baby are strong on feeds, sleep and nappies (**U**).
- Complaints: over-alerting breeds anxiety; streaks and "you are behind" counters; ads next to a loss (**U**).
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria: a feed log that needs an account on a shared phone; any "normal range" for feeds or output stated as fact without a clinician signing it; an EPDS cut-off copied from a UK source (Nigerian validation studies report cut-offs from 7 to 12 depending on language and period, **V** in the build plan).
- Premium Tarragon version: a calm, deletable feed log; week 1 and week 6 checks for mother and baby generated from the delivery; the EPDS prompted at week 6 (a proposal); the screening result routed by versioned cut-offs (10 to 12 review within the week, 13 or more within 48 hours, PROPOSED, not signed) with ANY non-zero item 10 going to the crisis route at once whatever the total, enforced in the database so a third writer can never miss it (INV-01). Support text is a CMO-review placeholder, invisible to patients, so no clinical claim is invented.

## 16.10 Child growth charts against WHO standards, milestones, immunisation, child danger signs
- WHO Anthro and the `anthro` package give LMS z-scores for weight-for-age, length/height-for-age, BMI-for-age, weight-for-length/height, MUAC-for-age (**V**: read and compared). The `anthro` PyPI package ships the WHO tables but applies the restricted-tail adjustment to every indicator while WHO applies it to weight-for-age, BMI-for-age and weight-for-length/height only, and it does not apply the 0.7 cm length/height correction to weight-for-height (**V**: read in `anthro.py`). It has no head-circumference or 5 to 19 year tables. Our golden cases compare only where the two agree and say so.
- Data source used: the 20 official WHO workbooks (expanded z-score tables, 2006 standards to day 1856, 2007 reference months 61 to 228) downloaded from cdn.who.int by `scripts/who-growth/build_who_lms.py`; 21,584 rows; nothing typed from memory (**V**). The WHO 5 to 10 year weight-for-age workbooks are published under file names that start `hfa-` (content checked: sheet name `wfa`).
- Best products: growth charts with percentile bands, a "measured lying or standing" switch, MUAC colour bands (**U**). Complaints: US (CDC) references, no length/height correction, panic from a single odd point (**U**).
- Nigeria: shared phones, parents measuring at home with a MUAC tape (6 to 59 months), low literacy, no Pidgin (D-14, English only). An implausible value (WHO flag ranges) must not silently drop a possibly sick child, so it is flagged, kept and still routed with "please check the measurement".
- Immunisation: the NPHCDA site (nphcda.gov.ng) was fetched; its pages in the search index did not expose a schedule table and the home page has none (**V**). News reports say the R21 malaria vaccine is given to children from 5 months in four doses at 5, 6, 7 and about 15 months (**U**, press, not the agency). The live catalogue (`vaccination_catalog`, codes `child_*`) was NOT edited; the open points are in OQ-360. IPV: reports say a second IPV dose was added in 2021 at 14 weeks, and the catalogue holds one dose at 14 weeks (**U**, OQ-360).
- Premium version: the chart reads the person's measurement against a versioned WHO table and says which version; severe and moderate acute malnutrition rules from configuration; reminders generic and never naming a vaccine or condition (INV-07); never "delay a dose" wording.

## 16.11 Seamless transitions: cycle, pregnancy, parenting
- Ovia moves a person through trying, pregnant and parenting with content that follows (**U**). Complaints: baby content shown after a pregnancy loss; stages guessed from a due date; ads (**U**).
- Our rule: a stage changes only when a person (or a clinician) confirms an event. The app may ask after a time, never move on its own. Transitions are data (`lifecycle.rules`). A recorded loss returns to tracking, hides baby and pregnancy content for a configured hold, and keeps the person's loss note private (not even a caregiver with a reproductive grant, a Care Circle supporter or a sponsor sees it). Copy for the loss path is a founder or CMO placeholder.
- Deletion: the founder's split by who recorded it (decision B3): what the person typed can be deleted after a grace window with an audit receipt; what a clinician recorded or acted on is sealed, not erased.
