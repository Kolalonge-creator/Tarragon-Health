# S74 research: partner network and commerce (Module 21)

Method note: written from the repository and product knowledge, without live browsing in this session. Treat the reference-platform lines as design inputs, not verified claims.

- 21.1 Laboratories (Everlywell, LetsGetChecked). Strong: booking, kit tracking, results posted to the account. Complaints: slow results, unclear reference ranges, results shown without context. Unsuitable here: results sent straight to the patient (INV-03/04), card-funded kits (D-06: patient pays the lab). Tarragon: results enter through a `LabResultsSource` (manual today, API stub), always held by the database until a clinician releases; a lab can never touch another lab's order.
- 21.2 Pharmacies (mPharma, Yodawy, JD Health). Strong: stock visibility, verified sourcing, chat. Complaints: counterfeit fear, price opacity, chat used for clinical advice. Unsuitable: price lists (OQ-283), delivery (OQ-261). Tarragon: a declared and admin-checked sourcing statement plus a per-supply NAFDAC number, and a patient-started in-app chat with no staff read path.
- 21.3 Clinics (Doctolib, Practo, Helium Health). Strong: slot calendars with holds. Complaints: double booking, no-shows. Tarragon: slots, 10-minute holds, capacity under a row lock, no practice software, dormant under module 28.
- 21.4 HMO (Reliance Health). Eligibility belongs to S71; capitation is banned (I8). Seam only.
- 21.5 Device shop (Withings, DarioHealth). Not built, decision S74-1 (no `shop_orders`).
- 21.6 Portal (Eka Care, Doctolib). Strong: statements with line detail and a way to query. Tarragon: statements from collected money only, partner sees own issued statements, can dispute, never edits.
- 21.7 Quality rules (Nigeria-specific). Licence expiry (PCN/NAFDAC), turnaround, upheld complaints, score; removal from new work by rule, reinstatement by a person with a reason; an expired licence is a hard stop.

Premium choices: every number is PROPOSED config; removal never touches work in flight; the partner sees the same figures Tarragon acts on.
