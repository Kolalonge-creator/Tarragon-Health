# S19 research: red event paging and escalation

Studied for behaviour only; nothing copied. Evidence is search-snippet level (see `docs/research/S18.md` for the same caveat), 2026-10-06.

**Escalation ladders (PagerDuty documentation).**
- An escalation policy is an ordered list of rules; the first rule notifies, each next rule fires after a timeout if nobody acknowledged. An acknowledgement pauses escalation until its own timeout expires, then escalation resumes. Adopt: ordered steps with timeouts held as data, and an acknowledgement that stops the ladder.
- If nobody is on call, PagerDuty creates no incident at all. Reject: for a red event "nobody on call" must be the loudest case, so it goes straight to level 2 and opens an incident.
- Schedules have layers and overrides; gap detection is not automatic. Adopt: the S18 rota gap check, and the pager treats a rota whose primary is no longer eligible as not covered.

**Triage priority (Manchester Triage System, NHS).** Priority classes carry target times and a red class means immediate attention; the system is explicit that the clock starts at arrival, not at the first look. Adopt: the escalation clock starts when the page is sent, and acknowledgement is recorded separately from resolution.

**Acknowledgement vs resolution (incident tooling, NHS 111 clinical advisor practice).** "I have seen it" and "it is handled" are different states; closing without a note hides what happened. Adopt: acknowledge stops the clock, a separate close needs a written note, and an open page is what ties the clinician to the patient's chart (INV-12) so access ends when the page is closed or after a fixed window.

**Channels in Nigeria.** Push needs data and a live app; power and data cuts are common, and doctors hold more than one job. D-12 and OQ-113 allow push, the in-console alarm and email only (no SMS paging). Adopt: send push, in-app and email together at every step rather than waiting for a push to fail, mark the notification critical so quiet hours never gate it, and keep the wording neutral (INV-07).

**Unsafe or unsuitable.** Paging text that names a condition or reading (INV-07); an "ack all" button that lets anyone silence a red page without being the person responsible (here only the paged clinicians or the CMO can acknowledge, and ops are told but cannot silence); a ladder that depends on a single clinician's phone.

**Not benchmarked.** No public source gives acknowledgement targets for primary care telehealth in Nigeria; 5 and 10 minutes are the spec's PROPOSED values, held in versioned configuration for the CMO to set.

## Second pass (2026-10-06): full pages, where they could be read
Labels: **read** / **snippet** / **inferred** as in `docs/research/S18.md`. Several NHS and Joint Commission PDFs and one PagerDuty page could not be parsed; those facts are marked.

**Escalation tooling.**
- PagerDuty (read): if nobody is on call at the first level the incident escalates at once to the next level; if the whole policy has no coverage no incident is created, and the page warns of silent loss; levels are at least 5 minutes apart when a level holds more than one person; notification rules are separate for high and low urgency, with push, phone, SMS, email and Slack and a staggered-channel best practice; only high urgency incidents get state-change notices. Our design already escalates at once when nobody is eligible, never loses an uncovered event, and keeps non-red work out of the ladder.
- Opsgenie (read): default example escalates at 0, 5 and 10 minutes; acknowledge or close stops escalation; alerts repeat until acknowledged, up to 20 times; there is an optional re-notify after acknowledge and an optional auto-close. Our 5 and 10 minute steps match the vendor example (they are not a clinical standard). **Changes made:** the lead alert repeats at most `lead_repeat_max` times (12) and then stops with a sev1 incident rather than looping for ever; repeats are push and in-app only so a sleeping lead's inbox is not flooded; a page acknowledged but not closed after `unclosed_alert_minutes` (60) tells the lead and ops once (the same idea as re-notify after acknowledge); auto-close is deliberately not offered for an unacknowledged red page.

**Targets.**
- Manchester Triage System (read): red is immediate, orange 10 minutes to first clinical contact (German table; an international version allows 240 for the lowest category; some sources quote different middle values). These measure first clinical assessment, not acknowledging a page, so they are a yardstick and not our acknowledgement target. Our 5 and 10 minutes stay PROPOSED, CMO to set.
- Joint Commission NPSG.02.03.01 (snippet): organisations must define critical results, who reports to whom and the acceptable time, and evaluate timeliness; no fixed minutes are mandated. This is why the steps are configuration and why `paging_overview` shows the timer state: the steps need to be written down and measured.
- Alert-fatigue reviews (snippet): override rates of roughly 46 to 96 percent across studies. Supports keeping page volume low (red only) and capping repeats.

**Nigeria and channels (second pass).** Grid supply averages about 6.6 hours a day (snippet) and a Lagos feeder study found roughly 640 outages a year (read); only about a third of rural adults use mobile internet (read, GSMA); Tecno and Infinix handsets limit background apps unless battery saving is turned off and auto-start is allowed (vendor guidance, snippet only). Push can therefore fail silently, which is why a page goes by push, in-app and email together and why the ladder is driven by acknowledgement and not by delivery. **Change made:** a "make sure alerts reach you" checklist on the on-call page (notifications on, battery saving off, auto-start allowed, email on the phone, ask for cover before a shift if power or data fail). Voice or SMS paging stays out (D-12); a voice rung for the CMO tier is recorded in OQ-135 as an option, not built.

**Still unpublished.** A paging acknowledgement number anywhere in the sources read; whether 5 and 10 minutes are clinically validated; measured push delivery on Tecno and Infinix in Nigeria; MDCN rules on part-time or locum rota duty.
