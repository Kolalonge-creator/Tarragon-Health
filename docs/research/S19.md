# S19 research: red event paging and escalation

Studied for behaviour only; nothing copied. Evidence is search-snippet level (see `docs/research/S18.md` for the same caveat), 2026-10-06.

**Escalation ladders (PagerDuty documentation).**
- An escalation policy is an ordered list of rules; the first rule notifies, each next rule fires after a timeout if nobody acknowledged. An acknowledgement pauses escalation until its own timeout expires, then escalation resumes. Adopt: ordered steps with timeouts held as data, and an acknowledgement that stops the ladder.
- If nobody is on call, PagerDuty creates no incident at all. Reject: for a red event "nobody on call" must be the loudest case, so it goes straight to level 2 and opens an incident.
- Schedules have layers and overrides; gap detection is not automatic. Adopt: the S18 rota gap check, and the pager treats a rota whose primary is no longer eligible as not covered.

**Triage priority (Manchester Triage System, NHS).** Priority classes carry target times and a red class means immediate attention; the system is explicit that the clock starts at arrival, not at the first look. Adopt: the escalation clock starts when the page is sent, and acknowledgement is recorded separately from resolution.

**Acknowledgement vs resolution (incident tooling, NHS 111 clinical advisor practice).** "I have seen it" and "it is handled" are different states; closing without a note hides what happened. Adopt: acknowledge stops the clock, a separate close needs a written note, and an open page is what ties the clinician to the patient's chart (INV-12) so access ends when the page is closed or after a fixed window.

**Channels in Nigeria.** Push needs data and a live app; power and data cuts are common, and doctors hold more than one job. D-12 and OQ-113 allow push, the in-console alarm and email only (no SMS paging). Adopt: send push, in-app and email together at every step rather than waiting for a push to fail, mark the notification critical so quiet hours never gate it, and keep the wording neutral (INV-07).

**Unsafe or unsuitable.** Paging text that names a condition or reading (INV-07); an "ack all" button that lets anyone silence a red page without being the person responsible (here only the paged clinicians or the CMO can acknowledge, and ops are told but cannot silence); a ladder that depends on a single clinician's phone.

**Not benchmarked.** No public source gives acknowledgement targets for primary care telehealth in Nigeria; 5 and 10 minutes are the spec's PROPOSED values, held in versioned configuration for the CMO to set.
