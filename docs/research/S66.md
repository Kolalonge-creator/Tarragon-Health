# S66 research: menstrual and reproductive (functions 16.1 to 16.4)

Written 2026-10-07. **Evidence status:** this pass did not browse the live web. Claims are tagged **V** (stated in a source the earlier planning research retrieved, per `docs/design/S66-S70-build-plan.md`) or **U** (background knowledge, unverified, must be checked before it goes into a signed clinical config). Nothing here is a clinical threshold; the CMO signs those (`docs/plans/S66-S70-cmo-signoff-pack.md`, A13 to A15).

## 16.1 Cycle tracking, predictions, symptom and mood logging, PIN lock, discreet notifications (Flo, Clue)
- Best products: calendar plus symptom, mood and flow logging, predictions that widen with irregular history, insight cards, an anonymous mode (**U**). Clue publishes its science and a privacy stance (**U**).
- Complaints: wrong predictions for irregular cycles (**U**); basics behind a paywall (**U**); health data shared with advertising and analytics SDKs. Flo's FTC order and the 2025 Meta jury finding are the cautionary cases (**V**).
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria: phones are shared and read over shoulders, so a reminder that says "your period is expected" is a disclosure; low bandwidth means tracking must work from local state; no ad or analytics SDK may touch this section.
- Tarragon version: one engine for web and mobile (it was two copies), widening confidence windows (already built), a **section PIN** that is optional but on by default and held on the device, recovery by a code to the account's own phone with no server data loss, neutral notification copy ("Your tracker has an update"), a lint that rejects cycle words, and a plain "who has looked at this" screen showing the audit log. Danger signs stay outside the lock.

## 16.2 Conception planning (Mira, Femometer, Flo)
- Best products: fertile-window estimate, ovulation test log, basal temperature charts; Mira and Femometer add hardware hormone or temperature tracking (**U**). Natural Cycles is the only FDA-cleared cycle-based contraceptive (**V**, class II) and needs NAFDAC clearance, so Part C says never present anything here as contraception.
- Complaints: fertile-window screens read as "safe days"; false precision (**U**).
- Nigeria: the no-hardware founder decision (2026-08-02) rules out Mira and Femometer style devices; the existing ovulation test and temperature fields stay but only in planning mode.
- Tarragon version: the fertile window, ovulation date and the temperature and ovulation test fields exist **only** while the patient has switched on "planning a pregnancy" (off by default, only she can switch it on, enforced by a database trigger). The prediction function itself returns no window when the mode is off, so reminders, exports and the insights card cannot show one by accident. Every line that states a window carries "This is not contraception." and the word "estimate"; a test fails if a surface that renders a window lacks the label.

## 16.3 Symptom pattern report for a clinician; neutral contraception education and referral (Clue)
- Best products: exportable summaries of cycle length, period length and symptoms for a visit (**U**). Clinicians want numbers: mean, spread, range, how many cycles fall outside 24 to 38 days, heavy flow days (FIGO ranges are already in the engine, **U** for current thresholds).
- Complaints: reports that conclude something; exports that include data the patient did not mean to share.
- Tarragon version: an audited read (INV-10) for a clinician tied to the patient (INV-12), refusing break-glass, support sessions, care coordinators, admins, sponsors and employers, with every refusal written to the audit log. The report carries lengths, flow and symptom counts and the menopause log; **no notes, temperature or ovulation data and no fertility figure**. Print or save as PDF from the console.
- Contraception education (A13): an alphabetical list (so order cannot read as a ranking) with one factual line per method, no dosing, no "best", emergency contraception described factually, fertility awareness never presented as contraception, one route to the care team. Basis named in the CMO pack: WHO MEC and the Nigerian National Family Planning Guidelines (**U**, each [verify] still to be checked by the CMO). Behind a go-live guard that is off.

## 16.4 Perimenopause and menopause (Flo)
- Best products: symptom log (hot flushes, sleep, mood, vaginal symptoms, cycle changes) and education (**U**). A validated score (Greene scale) exists (**U**) but A15 says no score yet.
- Complaints: apps that push supplements or hormone products; no escalation for bleeding after the menopause.
- Tarragon version: the existing log gets the two missing types (changes in periods, vaginal discomfort), provenance, and an urgent prompt for bleeding after the menopause (the alert to the care team already existed). Education only; **no hormone treatment advice** (the copy says so and a test enforces it); no score.

## Deletion and privacy (decisions B3 and C)
- Patient-entered tracker data is deletable on request after a 14 day grace window (PROPOSED), with a counts-only receipt in the audit log; data a clinician recorded or acted on is sealed, not erased. NHS Records Management Code and HIPAA are the retention references used elsewhere in S39c (**U** for the exact periods; counsel to confirm 8 years).
