# S70a research: devices, wearables and data connections (core half)

Sources: the S70 review in `docs/design/S66-S70-build-plan.md` section 4.5, the S70 to S75 review on branch `docs/s70-s75-decisions`, and the live code. No new web research was done in this session; competitor claims are the plan's (vendor claims unless that plan marked them verified). Reference platforms are studied for behaviour only; nothing is copied.

**18.1 Bluetooth pairing (Withings, DarioHealth).** Best products pair once and sync silently; complaints are silent disconnects and sync lag. Omron and Accu-Chek lock to their own apps, so only standard-GATT models can pair at all. For Nigeria the live risk is the shared phone: one handset, a family of cuffs. Tarragon version: a device belongs to a person, a supporter pairs for the person they manage, and an incoming reading asks whose it is only when more than one person has that cuff. Never run on real hardware; no model is named.

**18.2 Recommended devices (Tarragon decision).** Best practice is a validated-device list (ValidateBP or a published study). Unsuitable here: affiliate links, import duty, and any suggestion that Tarragon sells. Tarragon version: list only what the CMO has marked reviewed, each with its evidence link and a NAFDAC number or authorised distributor, "any other device: type it in" always shown, nothing seeded.

**18.3 Photo reading capture (Nigeria-specific).** Seven-segment OCR is error-prone (one study: 86.5 percent digit accuracy), so a model's guess must never be a reading. Low literacy and low bandwidth argue for on-device recognition and no upload. Tarragon version: photo stays on the phone, every number is an editable box the person ticks, nothing saves until all are ticked, an impossible number is held, a saved one is triaged like a typed one.

**18.5 CGM import (Abbott, Dexcom, Levels).** Streams every 5 minutes; Libre has no official public API; Dexcom data is delayed outside the US. Danger: a stream can flood a queue. Tarragon version: sustained-event rules (decision S70-2) create a rate-limited clinician task; the severe-low safety words show on the phone at once. The live single-reading emergency backstop for a reading under 3.0 is left alone.

**18.6 Single-lead ECG (KardiaMobile).** Vendor APIs need a commercial agreement. Decision S70-3 avoids that: the device's own label comes in through the existing report upload or an iPhone ECG classification read. Tarragon version: label stored verbatim, a routine task due within one working day, the patient sees one fixed sentence, chest pain, fainting or breathlessness goes through the existing red path.

**18.9 Source label and plausibility (Apple Health).** Apple Health labels every sample by source. Wrist PPG SpO2 is less accurate across skin tones. Tarragon version: a source badge on every reading, two plausibility classes (impossible is held, extreme-but-possible is triaged), wrist SpO2 informational only, and cross-source de-duplication that keeps one canonical row and never deletes.

Not built here (S70b or paused): Fitbit to the Google Health API, Withings, Samsung, Kardia, inhaler sensors, the paid CGM programme, the band (D-22), any device shop.
