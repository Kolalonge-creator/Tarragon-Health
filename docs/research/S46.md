# S46 research: engine logic, results and the yearly Health Report (functions 3.11 to 3.16)

Evidence caveat (same as S45 and `docs/research/health-report-study.md`): the competitor points are secondary (vendor pages, press, the repo's own study notes). No real Neko or Function Health report was available, no app was installed and no store reviews were read. Re-verify before using any of it in a clinical or pricing decision.

## 3.11 Pathway suppression (Tarragon decision)
- Nothing we studied suppresses a test because a chronic pathway already owns it; products either sell the whole panel again (Function) or leave it to the clinician. The live rule (`screening_pathway_coverage`, 2026-08-02) is therefore our own.
- Gap found: the rule had no override, so a clinician who genuinely wants the repeat (a medicine change, a doubtful result) had no audited way to say so.
- Tarragon version: keep the coverage table, verify every row suppresses (the proof loops over all of them), add a clinician override with a reason code, a note, an expiry (12 months) and revocation, audited. An override lifts pathway ownership only; it never lifts a terminal serology state or a window period.

## 3.12 Hepatitis B immunity logic (Tarragon decision)
- Public guidance (WHO, CDC): anti-HBs at or above 10 mIU/mL is the usual immunity marker; HBsAg screening matters until a person is known to be immune or chronically infected; HCV antibody screening is repeated by risk, not strictly yearly.
- Conflict recorded: the live rule (2026-08-21) never re-sells HBsAg or HCV Ab once on file; the spec says HIV and HCV stay annual and HBsAg stops after a positive anti-HBs. Founder decided the spec rule wins (2026-10-07).
- Nigeria: anti-HBs is not a panel analyte or an orderable test today (no `anti_hbs` screen type), so immunity can arrive three ways only: a lab item named `anti_hbs` flagged positive, a numeric titre once the CMO confirms the threshold, or a doctor recording it with a stated basis.
- Tarragon version: a versioned rule table (v1 documents the old behaviour, v2 is active), a stored immune state on the existing `patient_serology_status`, never overwriting a chronic HBV record, and the threshold left PROPOSED and unsigned.

## 3.13 Results with plain-language explanation and one next step (Healthily)
- Best products show a short meaning and one action beside the number. Complaint themes: explanations that read as diagnoses, long generic text, and audio or chat that explains a positive HIV or hepatitis result with no human.
- Nigeria: low literacy (short sentences), shared phones (nothing in a notification), low bandwidth (text first). Audio is only through the S32 manifest and no result clips exist.
- Tarragon version: deterministic keyed text per flag (normal, low, high, critical, positive, negative), no model, one "what to do next" line; HIV, HBsAg and HCV Ab get only "your care team will talk this through with you personally". The same list is enforced three times: the server action, the generator and a database trigger on `patient_result_explanations` (INV-04).

## 3.15 Yearly Tarragon Health Report (Neko Health, Function Health, InsideTracker)
- Neko: clinician talk-through and a watchlist tier; critics note incidental findings and cascades. Function: biomarkers by system, in range and out of range, tighter "optimal" ranges that flag normal people. InsideTracker: three zones with a proprietary age. Whoop: healthspan with no clinical benchmark. Withings: a year review only with enough data.
- Unsafe or unsuitable here: any "optimal" band, biological age, percentile of other people, a score that outruns the sensors, a report that delivers a critical or sensitive finding as news, colour-only status, large images over mobile data.
- Tarragon version (all twelve principles in `health-report-study.md`): hidden until a named clinician signs; at most three priorities; three states plus not measured; laboratory or care-team ranges only; compare to the person's own last year; minimum BP reading count; fixed "screening does not rule out disease" wording; sensitive sections excluded from shared copies; A4 black-and-white PDF built from the same text model as the page. Any AI-drafted paragraph sits only on the unsigned draft and is wiped at signature.

## 3.16 Standing disclaimer (Neko Health, Prenuvo)
- Neko and Prenuvo state limits mainly in small print; critics say whole-body screening gives false reassurance. A single shared component shows one sentence on the risk result, the screening calendar, lab results and the report, and the report carries the longer fixed statement.
- The sentence is a placeholder text key until the CMO approves the final wording (OQ-S46-4).
