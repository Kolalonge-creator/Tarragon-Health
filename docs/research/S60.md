# S60 research note: Module 12, symptom checker safety half (functions 12.8 to 12.13)

Sources: the plan `docs/design/S55-S60-build-plan.md` section 4.4 (which already carries the verified and unverified tags for each product), `docs/research/competitor-review-global-2026-10.md` (B2 K Health, B3 Ada, B4 Babylon) and `docs/research/competitor-review-africa-2026-10.md`. No new web fetches were made in this session; every tag below is inherited. V = verified in those documents, U = lead only. Reference platforms are studied for behaviour, not copied.

## 12.8 Red flags first, overriding the engine
- Best products (Infermedica, Healthily): ask a fixed red-flag block before any adaptive question, and a hit ends the interview with emergency guidance. Semigran 2015 across 23 checkers found a caution bias in triage (V), which is the right direction to be wrong in.
- Complaints: a red flag that depends on the engine being up is not a red flag; Babylon was reported reassuring on heart attack and pulmonary embolism (V, one secondary source).
- Unsuitable for Nigeria: any design where emergency guidance needs the network. Low bandwidth and dropped signal are normal, so INV-06 says the red rules evaluate on the device.
- Premium Tarragon version: the floor runs on the device from the bundled copy of the signed rules, before and independently of the engine; an engine error, timeout or missing protocol can only escalate. Finding while reading the live code: the wizard never collected associated symptoms, so no red flag could fire from it. The first screen now carries the safety checklist.

## 12.9 "Possible causes, not a diagnosis"
- Ada and K Health state the limitation in their own help text, and both hand a clinician the diagnosis (K Health: V for the model, U for outcomes). Babylon shows the cost of implying more (V).
- Complaint: the label lives in each screen's own copy, so it drifts.
- Tarragon: one component, one catalogue entry, a test that fails if a result screen stops using it or hard-codes the sentence.

## 12.10 Doctor review within a stated time
- K Health: AI intake then a clinician (V). Unknown to us: the review time they promise (U).
- Risk for Nigeria: promising a time nobody is staffed to meet. The stated time is read from the active signed `escalation_slas` entry and shows nothing at all while that entry does not exist (it does not today; the draft is unsigned).
- Tarragon: review is per-patient (INV-12, a held task), audited (INV-10), completed by a clinician at medical officer level or above, and its fields feed the accuracy audit.

## 12.11 Knowledge base and Nigerian prevalence
- Infermedica: licensed knowledge base, class IIb in the EU (V), a Nigeria deployment case study (V), accuracy figures vendor-claimed (U). Ada: not validated on Nigerian prevalence as far as the review could tell (L).
- Founder decision of 2026-10-07: interface plus in-house adapter only, no possible-causes screen. So prevalence is not a diagnosis input.
- Tarragon: a versioned PROPOSED config of prevalence and season risks (malaria, typhoid, Lassa season, sickle cell) that can only raise an urgency, ships entirely as unsigned drafts that cannot take effect, with provenance and sign-off fields and a proof that it can never lower a category. The seasons, states and wording are the CMO's to supply.

## 12.12 Monthly accuracy audit
- Ada: peer-reviewed and vendor-run comparisons, top-3 about 70%; the AFYA Swahili study is the nearest low-resource validation design (V). Babylon: an unvalidated "beats GPs" claim rested on a small test (V).
- Complaint: accuracy figures used as marketing before independent validation.
- Tarragon: checker category against the reviewing clinician's category (top-suggestion accuracy cannot be measured until a differential exists), by age band, sex and region, Wilson intervals, minimum cell size with complementary suppression, test accounts excluded, first report a baseline, and a database CHECK that no report is ever publishable.

## 12.13 NAFDAC classification
- NAFDAC published a draft Medical Devices and IVD regulation in July 2025 with no software-specific criteria; I found no final text (V for the draft, U for current status).
- Not code. A recorded position: text, classification, counsel, date, who attached it, append-only. The guard attestation for it is refused until a determined position is on record.

## Decisions taken
1. Red-flag floor and degraded behaviour are shared pure code in the engine package, used by web and mobile.
2. Accuracy and degraded-mode values are PROPOSED config for the CMO; nothing is signed by an agent.
3. `pcm` is not built: the repository is English-only by founder decision D-14, which post-dates the brief's mention of Pidgin (recorded as OQ-S60-06).
