# S71 research note: Module 19, part 1 (functions 19.1 to 19.7)

Date 2026-10-07. Method: the S70 to S75 review (`docs/research/S70-S75-review-and-build-plan.md`, which verified Paystack, mPharma and Reliance public pages) plus the shipped code. Items marked (recall) come from memory of public product pages and were not re-fetched in this session (the Paystack docs site returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool). Nothing here is copied from any product; these are behaviours studied, then designed from Tarragon's own rules.

## 19.1 Pay per item (Tarragon decision)
- Best products sell one clear thing per checkout (a visit, a test, a pack) and show what it includes before the pay button (Hims, Babylon, mPharma (recall)). Users complain when a "free" offer reaches a card screen with extras added.
- Unsuitable here: a basket with many lines on a shared low-bandwidth phone. Tarragon keeps one item per order (INV-09: a payment is a checkout for a specific item).
- Decision: add `home_kit` as a catalogue kind; seed consultation, lab panel and home kit placeholders switched OFF with NO price row. No price is invented; the founder prices and switches each on. The membership pivot (2026-10-05) said "no one-off consults": the consultation placeholder is therefore a question for the founder (OQ-400), not a decision. No device kind: CLAUDE.md records the 2026-08-02 decision not to sell hardware. Legacy per-domain checkouts stay (OQ-23).

## 19.2 Card, bank transfer, USSD (Tarragon decision)
- Paystack hosted checkout offers card, bank, USSD, QR, mobile money and "Pay with Transfer" (a one-time account number) (recall; the S70 to S75 review verified card, transfer and USSD). If `channels` is not sent, the Paystack dashboard defaults apply and can change without a code review.
- Complaint pattern: bank transfer and USSD settle minutes late, and apps that say "failed" or spin forever cause double payment. A transfer is not a failure.
- Decision: send `channels` explicitly (`card`, `bank_transfer`, `ussd`) from configuration, show an honest "waiting for your bank" state on web and mobile (the `paid` page already polls; the copy changes), keep the existing 5-minute reconcile sweep as the safety net. The exact accepted channel strings must be proved with the founder's test key (OQ-401); the code and tests use the test double only.

## 19.3 Naira, one price list (built)
- Bigint kobo, NGN only, one price per item, versioned and immutable (S25, S73). Nothing to add.

## 19.4 Pay for a loved one (Nigeria-specific, built)
- Diaspora payers use foreign cards; processors charge more for them (Paystack lists a higher international rate, recall: 3.9 percent + NGN 100 per the 2026-10-06 note in the registry). Users complain when a surprise higher total appears on the card page.
- Decision: show a "paying with a card from outside Nigeria" estimated line before pay for a beneficiary order, from configuration (`commerce.processing_fee_estimate` v3, PROPOSED), labelled as an estimate. Tarragon never keeps the fee.
- Safety gap found: OQ-192. The payer path admitted any `profile_access` grantee. Fixed in this session: a payer is a Care Circle member holding `pay_for_care`, or an older caregiver grant at `manage` level whose permissions include `manage_payments` (the vocabulary S26 caregiver enforcement already uses). The older read paths (profile row, booking requests, vaccination rows) get the same rule.

## 19.5 Sponsor-paid items (Omada Health, Reliance Health)
- Omada and similar employer programmes show the benefit as already covered and never ask the member for a card (recall). Members are confused when a covered service still shows a price or a "pay" button, and employers are frustrated when a budget is silently overspent.
- Unsuitable: a stored balance the member can spend (INV-09) and a sponsor "wallet". A sponsor holds a budget CEILING at Tarragon; the person never holds value.
- Decision: `sponsor_programmes` (sponsor, items, rules, `budget_kobo`), budget reserved atomically per order (row lock), released on cancel, fail or refund, committed on paid. A fully covered order is an order with amount 0 and no payment step; the page says "Covered by your employer" or the sponsor's name. Event `sponsor_entitlement.granted`. Ledger: a sponsor receivable (account 1210) for the sponsor share, for finance to confirm (OQ-403). Sponsor invoicing and collection are not built. Counsel opinion is still needed before live use (OQ-07).

## 19.6 HMO eligibility (Reliance Health)
- Reliance runs its own plans and has no public eligibility API that was found (S70 to S75 review). Nigerian HMOs verify by member number against their own system, often by phone or portal.
- Decision (S71-1): an adapter per HMO behind one interface, a manual operations check as the fallback, and only a keyed hash of the member number stored (HMAC with a Vault pepper, the OQ-223 pattern; fails closed if the pepper is missing). A stub adapter connects to nothing and always answers "unavailable", which routes to manual. Operations confirm by typing the number again; the system hashes it and compares to the stored hash, so the number is never stored and a different number cannot be confirmed. A pass lets the person claim items of a sponsor programme tied to that HMO; a fail shows how to pay normally. No capitation and no HMO billing (I8, decision S71-1).

## 19.7 Itemised checkout (Nigeria-specific)
- The best checkouts name who gets what: a visit fee, a service fee, a processing fee, and the total (Uber and Bolt style fare breakdown; Paystack itself shows its fee). Users distrust a single big number.
- Decision: the checkout lists the clinician or partner fee, Tarragon's service fee, and the processing estimate in plain words; the lines must sum to the total shown; the processing line is labelled an estimate. When a price has no component split (the Membership today), the checkout shows one price line and does not pretend to a split. Tested with a property check that lines sum.
