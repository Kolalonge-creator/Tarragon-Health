# S72 research note: checkout and payments, part 2 (Module 19, functions 19.8 to 19.13)

Method and honesty: this note was written without live browsing of the reference platforms (no network research was run in this session). It records what these products are publicly known to do, as of the author's knowledge, plus what the platform's own code and decisions already say. Treat the reference claims as informed starting points, not fresh citations. The design below follows the requirement and the founder decisions (`docs/DECISIONS.md` S72-1, S72-2, S72-3) first.

## 19.8 Receipts, payment history, refunds and cancellations (Nigeria-specific)
- Best products (Paystack, Flutterwave, Nigerian bank and wallet apps) send a receipt per payment with a reference, the amount, the fee and a download or share option; the better ones show a payment history grouped by month and a plain refund status.
- Users complain that a refund is a black box (no status, no date), that a bank-transfer payment shows as "failed" when it is only late, and that the receipt is only on one device or only by email.
- Unsuitable for Nigeria: a PDF-only receipt on a low-data phone, a refund flow that needs a typed essay, an amount shown without the fee that was charged.
- Tarragon: web already had receipts (`patient_receipts`), a refund dialog and an admin queue. Mobile had receipts but no refund request and no order history. Premium version: the same refund request on the phone, with the refund state in words ("Refund asked for", "Refunded", "Refund not approved"), a refund button only for the person who actually paid (a supporter-paid order has none for the person it is for), and a plain note when a sponsor covered the order (nothing to refund). Text only, so it works on a slow connection (S34).

## 19.9 Health Points discounts at checkout (Personify Health)
- Best products let points lower a price in clear steps, with a cap and an expiry, and show the effect before paying.
- Users complain about points that "disappear", unclear value, points that can only be spent on partner goods, and cash-like point balances that become a liability.
- Unsafe here: turning points into money or vouchers (a stored value, INV-09, and the earlier kobo conversion did exactly that), letting points reduce a clinician's or a partner's fee (the partner must be paid in full).
- Decision S72-1 and the S73 engine: a single-use reduction on Tarragon's own service fee only, tied to one order. S72 removes the leftover kobo conversion (function, table, column) with a count-first, fail-safe migration, and wires the choice into the checkout on web and mobile: the person sees, from the database's own quote, what points would take off and chooses; nothing is automatic.

## 19.10 Instalments (Nigeria-specific)
- Providers (Paystack split payments, buy-now-pay-later partners) exist, with different terms, fees and credit checks.
- Risk: a credit product is a regulated activity, adds cost for the person, and a failed instalment on a care item is a safety problem.
- Decision S72-2: not built. Recorded only. Reopen on an explicit ask.

## 19.11 Community Access Fund (Insight Timer; Nigeria-specific)
- Insight Timer funds free access with member support and publishes the principle; crowdfunding and charity-fund platforms show a running total and who is helped; the good ones publish the rules for who receives help and aggregate impact numbers.
- Users complain about opaque criteria, about not knowing where the money went, and about tiny-number reports that identify people.
- Unsafe/unsuitable here: a donation that sits as a spendable balance (INV-09), a donor choosing a recipient, a report that names or lets someone identify a recipient, and taking money before the legal vehicle is settled (OQ-323).
- Tarragon version: a donation is a pledge, confirmed by operations when a bank transfer arrives (no live Paystack call in this session). It is a restricted gift: general, or tied to a named item. Grants are approved by operations against published reasons, with a written reason, and are realised as an order-bound sponsor entitlement with a ceiling (S71 machinery: reservation, ledger, refund and event paths all apply). The public report is aggregate, never includes test data, and withholds any group below the platform minimum cell (and the totals with it if a subtraction could recover a withheld cell). Whole feature behind a go-live guard that is OFF, with the counsel opinion as a condition.

## 19.12 Entitlements view (Omada Health)
- Best products show what you have in plain counts and dates ("3 coaching sessions left", "your plan runs until ..."), and where it came from.
- Users complain about hidden expiry dates and not knowing whether an employer or they paid.
- Tarragon: the web card existed but was never mounted on a page and printed raw "{{count}}" placeholders (a bug: the i18n helper only fills single braces). Fixed. New wording shared by web and mobile: "3 consultations remaining", "valid for 60 more days", and the source in the person's own words (you bought it, someone who cares about you paid, covered by your sponsor, from the Community Access Fund). The Fund is named but never explained beyond its name, and no sponsor name is shown for it.

## 19.13 Payment requests (Nigeria-specific)
- Common Nigerian practice: someone asks a relative or friend (often abroad) to pay a bill, by phone message or bank-app request; Paystack and Flutterwave offer payment links; bank apps offer "request money".
- Users complain about links that never expire, links anyone can use, and links that show too much about why.
- Unsafe: a link that exposes a condition (INV-07), a guessable code, a link that can be paid for a different item or person, WhatsApp automation (removed, F-02).
- Tarragon: a person creates a request for one item; the code is unguessable, stored only as an HMAC, shown once, shared with the phone's own share sheet. The link page shows a generic item name and the itemised price, nothing about the person. It expires, can be cancelled, is single use, and is rate limited; the database refuses any order that is not that item for that person from a different payer. By founder decision S29c nothing smaller than the full yearly Membership can be paid for someone else, so that is all a request can ask for (OQ-425).
