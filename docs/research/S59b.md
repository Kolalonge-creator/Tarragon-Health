Status of everything below: DRAFT. Nothing here is signed, activated or approved. Sources were found by web search on 2026-10-07; in most cases I read a search summary or a secondary page, not the primary guideline text. Where that matters it is said. Quotes are avoided; everything is paraphrased.

## C4 symptom.risk_tightening (prevalence and season), version 2 of the config

What changed from version 1:
- Typhoid entry dropped: no signed pathway asks `abdominal_pain` (only the v2 adult draft would), so it could never match, and I found no sourced Nigerian typhoid season to cite.
- Sickle cell entry dropped: no signed pathway collects `sickle_cell_disease` history, so it could never match. It belongs in the paediatric and adult v2 pathways once signed (the paediatric draft already asks it).
- Malaria entry kept, now sourced: Nigeria's rainy season runs roughly April to October and malaria transmission peaks within it (MSF material on Nigeria; WHO has recommended seasonal malaria chemoprevention since 2012 for areas with highly seasonal transmission). In the north transmission is concentrated in the rainy season, in the south it can run all year. Months 4 to 10 are therefore a reasonable draft, and the CMO may want a state split.
  Sources: https://www.doctorswithoutborders.org/latest/nigeria-preventing-long-and-deadly-malaria-season-claiming-more-lives-borno ; SMC state coverage summary at https://www.malariaconsortium.org/resources/impact-of-seasonal-malaria-chemoprevention-a-plausibility-evaluation-of-routine-data-from-health-facilities-in-three-implementing-states-in-nigeria
- Lassa entry kept and now names states: press reports of NCDC situation reports name Edo, Ondo, Bauchi and Taraba as the epicentres (about two thirds of confirmed cases) and put the peak in the dry season, December to April. The draft uses months 12, 1, 2, 3, 4 and those four states. Cases are reported elsewhere (for example Ebonyi and Nasarawa in 2026), and Lassa is reported all year, so a state list is a draft cut, not a boundary. A person with no state on file still matches (the safe direction, existing engine rule).
  Sources: https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/Lassa-fever---Nigeria ; NCDC figures as relayed by https://allafrica.com/stories/202601300066.html and https://www.arise.tv/ncdc-lassa-fever-has-claimed-168-lives-across-21-states-in-2025/

Could not verify: the NCDC situation report pages themselves (I relied on press summaries of them); the exact current burden-state list for 2026; any Nigerian typhoid seasonality; the right minimum category for each entry (that is the CMO's clinical call); whether the profile `state` values match these spellings exactly.

## C5 symptom.context_tightening, version 2 of the config

Raise-only, as before. Changes: young child entry dropped (the checker now refuses under 18 until a signed paediatric protocol exists); `abdominal_pain` removed from the pregnancy list (not in any signed pathway); a diastolic entry added; oxygen threshold moved to 91 to match the highest-scoring NEWS2 band.
- Pregnancy with headache and a vision change: NICE NG133 lists severe headache and visual disturbance among features of pre-eclampsia (read through secondary summaries; the guideline text itself was not opened). Emergency as the minimum category is the CMO's call.
- High blood pressure reading with a symptom: the commonly used line for a hypertensive emergency is 180 systolic and/or 120 diastolic with symptoms of organ damage (several secondary medical references). Both numbers are in config, not code. A home reading is not proof of organ damage, so the entry only raises a minimum.
- Low oxygen: NEWS2 SpO2 scale 1 gives its highest score (3) at 91 or below, 2 at 92 to 93. Version 1 used 92. Consumer pulse oximeters are less reliable, so the CMO may prefer a lower category or a repeat reading rule.
Could not verify: WHO and ACOG primary texts for pregnancy cut-offs (no source found for "severity 6" in pregnancy, flagged as the CMO's number); any guideline cut-off for a non-injury headache on an anticoagulant (flagged unverified); the NICE, ESC and NEWS2 primary documents (secondary summaries only).

## C8 merged draft protocol (paediatric plus the adult v2 draft)

The old S59 paediatric draft and the adult v2 draft (fever, abdominal pain, migration 20260911202322) are now ONE draft version created by the reworked S59 paediatric migration: the active signed adult pathways with the chest pain cardiac pattern having no severity floor, the v2 extras, and the three paediatric pathways. Signing replaces the whole active config, so one signature covers everything; the old v2 row stays as a superseded draft.

IMCI cross-check (WHO Integrated Management of Childhood Illness):
- The general danger signs for a child aged 2 months to 5 years are four: cannot drink or breastfeed, vomits everything, convulsions in this illness, lethargic or unconscious. The paediatric drafts use exactly these four (confirmed against several IMCI chart summaries; the WHO booklet PDF was reached only as a search result).
- IMCI treats infants under 2 months as a separate "young infant" group, where fever (axillary 37.5 C or higher) is itself a sign of possible serious bacterial infection needing urgent referral. The draft previously said "under 3 months"; it now says under 2 months (`infant_under_2_months`) to match IMCI's group. Whether the CMO wants a wider band (some national protocols use 3 months for fever in infants) is a clinical decision.
- Chest indrawing and fast breathing are IMCI pneumonia signs, but the age-specific fast breathing cut-offs were not verified, so the draft asks the carer rather than coding a number.
Could not verify: IMCI dehydration signs (sunken eyes, drinks poorly, skin pinch) in the booklet text; severe malnutrition and anaemia signs (not in the draft at all); sickle cell fever guidance; the booklet's own wording. All paediatric content remains NOT independently clinically reviewed, and the checker refuses under 18 until the CMO signs a paediatric version.

## Chest pain, no severity floor

The signed v1 rule `chest_pain.cardiac_pattern` fires for breathlessness, sweating or arm or jaw pain with chest pain only from severity 6. The CMO decided to remove the floor. Draft A (migration 20261007190204) is the active config with exactly that one rule changed. The bundled on-device floor stays equal to the signed v1 (floor 6) until the CMO signs; then the bundled copy and db-seed-fixture.json must change in the same commit. A Jest test (floor-drift.test.ts) proves the draft differs by only that rule and reports drift if a signed protocol drops the floor without the bundled copy; the server also raises a Sentry error on every check where the active pathway's red flags differ from the bundled ones.
