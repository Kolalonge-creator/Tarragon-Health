# S58 research note: Module 11, Rewards and engagement

Sources: the plan `docs/design/S55-S60-build-plan.md` section 4.3 (its **V** verified / **U** unverified tags carry over) and the repository audit. No live web research was run for this note. Treat every **U** as a lead, not a fact. Reference products are studied for behaviour only; nothing here copies their text or design (level names, copy and screens are original).

## 11.1 Earning (Personify Health, Sidekick Health)
- Best: points for logging and for verified actions (screenings, vaccinations), daily caps, challenges, employer pools (**U**). The best independent evidence (an Illinois workplace wellness trial, **V**) shows incentives raise participation but not biometrics or spend, and that healthier people self-select in. Complaints: step gaming, points for calorie or weight targets, notification pressure.
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria: any reward for weight, BMI or waist (invites crash dieting and shame); rewarding a "good" result (invites hiding a bad one); streak anxiety on shared phones where one person logs for another.
- Tarragon: points are rules in data (`reward_rules`), never numbers in triggers. Verified clinical actions (lab done, review attended, screening recorded, programme finished) are marked and shown first; they reward doing the thing, never what it found. A repeated action decays (full, then half, then a quarter) so returns do not flatten into spam. Weight and waist readings earn nothing, a rule that names a body measure is refused by the table, an implausible reading earns nothing, and a daily limit across all rules keeps effort steady.

## 11.2 Redemption (Personify Health; Nigeria-specific)
- Best: points redeem for discounts or partner rewards, often funded by a partner. Complaint: points that act like cash invite fraud and, here, would be stored value (INV-09).
- Tarragon: F1 switched redemption off (OQ-08). S58 builds the checkout discount and keeps it off. Points buy a percentage of one order, never a naira amount (no rate exists), capped by a share of the item price and a kobo cap per order, one per order, the buyer's own points on the buyer's own order, never cash, never for a minor. The founder sets the cap; until then it is "coming soon".

## 11.3 Levels and badges (Sidekick Health, Headspace)
- Best: yearly tiers with earned status carried into the next year (Vitality pattern, **V**), badges for consistency, a grace day. Complaints: streak loss notifications, leaderboards that shame, tier cliffs.
- Tarragon: four original level names (Sprout, Leaf, Branch, Canopy), counted per Lagos calendar year, last year's level carries through this year, the spendable balance never resets, a consistency badge tolerates a quiet day, there is no counter that resets, no notification about a missed day, no leaderboard, no comparison. Adults only for levels.

## 11.4 Employer reward pools (Personify Health)
- Best: employer-funded pools with participation reporting. Risk: an employer seeing who took part in what is health surveillance (spec I9: aggregate only).
- Tarragon: Module 24 is not built. Only an aggregate-only seam exists (counts, groups under a minimum suppressed, test accounts excluded). Per-person caps and funding are design notes in OQ-S58-06.
