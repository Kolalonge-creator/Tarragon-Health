# S45 research: risk, screening calendar, packages (functions 3.1 to 3.10)

Evidence caveat (same as `docs/plans/S41-S45-build-plan.md`): the competitor and guidance points below are secondary (vendor pages, press, background knowledge plus the repo's own research notes). No app was installed and no store reviews were read. Re-verify before using any of it in a clinical or pricing decision. The WHO paper's appendix (the model coefficients) could not be obtained in this session: the open copy at eprints.gla.ac.uk is the main paper only, and the coefficient tables are in the Lancet appendix.

## 3.1 Risk questionnaire (Omada, Healthily)
- Best products keep the questionnaire short, points-based, and end on one plain-language result and one next step. The CDC prediabetes risk test is the model.
- Complaint themes: result looks like a diagnosis; long forms abandoned; one study found Healthily gave unsafe triage in 28.6 percent of cases, so a questionnaire must stay risk stratification, never triage.
- Nigeria: low bandwidth (resumable, grouped questions), shared phones (no result in a notification, INV-07), low literacy (short sentences, audio later via the S32 seam).
- Tarragon version: reuse the live versioned questionnaire; add nothing clinical; make sure the result names the config version (INV-16) and says it is an estimate, not a diagnosis.

## 3.2 Cardiovascular estimate (Nigeria-specific)
- Chosen by founder and the CMO pack: WHO 2019 charts, Western sub-Saharan Africa, lab model when total cholesterol is on file, else non-lab (BMI) model, shown as bands, never a precise percentage.
- Unsafe or unsuitable: non-lab model performs poorly in diabetes; no Nigerian outcome validation; not for ages outside 40 to 74; not used when treatment is already indicated (established CVD, BP 160/100 or above); Nigeria's region mapping is unverified.
- Tarragon version: the engine reads coefficients from signed configuration only. No coefficient is embedded in code. Without verified coefficients the instrument stays off behind a go-live guard and records the reason instead of a score.

## 3.3 Yearly or major-change reassessment (Omada)
- Best products re-prompt on a calendar and after an event (new diagnosis, medicine change). Complaint: nagging.
- Tarragon version: one in-app prompt, nothing in a notification naming a condition (INV-07). Triggers are configuration: 365 days, a new chronic diagnosis, a smoking status change, a reading at or above 160/100.

## 3.4 Age and sex calendar (Healthily; Tarragon decision)
- Healthily shows a generic "checks for your age" list; no product we saw runs a signed, versioned rule set per country.
- Nigeria: the cervical policy (HPV DNA at 35 and 45 in national targets) is unconfirmed by the CMO, so v1 keeps the live catalogue rule (25 to 65, every 3 years) as PROPOSED and records the question.
- Tarragon version: a nightly scheduler computes due items from a signed rule set only; unsigned rules schedule nothing live. Prostate stays "optional" per the catalogue.

## 3.5 Done, not applicable, declined with reasons (CareClinic)
- Trackers let people tick items off; the complaint is that a declined item vanishes and then returns.
- Tarragon version: terminal states with a stored reason code and note; a declined or not applicable item is never re-created by a later run; the patient can reopen it.

## 3.6 to 3.8 Packages (Tarragon decision; Function Health)
- Function sells a membership with 100+ biomarkers; the complaint theme is breadth over evidence and large unexplained panels. The bundled video consult is dropped by the founder (covered by the S25 membership).
- Tarragon version: `screening_packages` is a thin eligibility and safety layer over `panel_bundles` (names provisional, OQ). HPV DNA is a dormant SKU behind a guard that needs a positive-result pathway.

## 3.9 Home collection and kits (Everlywell, LetsGetChecked)
- Complaint themes: reactive HIV or STI results delivered by app or email with no human; slow kit support.
- Tarragon version: no HIV, hepatitis or STI home collection until a human-disclosure path is attested (guard). Results follow S27 (INV-04).

## 3.10 Booking and payment in one flow (Clafiya, Vezeeta)
- Both show price before booking; complaints are hidden fees and rescheduling friction.
- Tarragon version: a read function returns package, partner rate card and eligibility so the S25 checkout shows the price first. The checkout itself is S25's; this session ships the seam only.
