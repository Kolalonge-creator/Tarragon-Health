# S28 research: pharmacy partner and dispensing

Reference platforms (Medisafe, MyTherapy, Yodawy, JD Health, mPharma) were studied for behaviour only; nothing was copied. Their public help pages were not re-read in this session, so every line below is general knowledge to be checked before it is quoted.

- **Sending a prescription to a chosen pharmacy**: e-prescribing services let the patient pick a pharmacy and show a pick-up reference; the pharmacy sees only what was sent to it. Adopted. Unsuitable for Nigeria: relying on a national prescription register that does not exist; our pharmacies are verified one by one against the PCN list (UNVERIFIED process).
- **Collection code**: pick-up codes are common in click-and-collect. A short code is guessable, so ours is 8 characters from a safe alphabet, held where the pharmacy cannot read it, and locked after 5 wrong tries. Not sent in a notification or message.
- **Mark dispensed, partial supply**: pharmacy systems record supplied quantity, batch and expiry and allow part-supply with what is owed. Adopted with a required note. Controlled medicines are out of scope here (OQ-276).
- **Flag a problem (out of stock, query to the prescriber)**: already built in S36h; reused.
- **Price comparison, verified batches, pharmacist chat**: found in the named platforms but deliberately not built (Release 2 or undecided, OQ-273). Unsafe or unsuitable: ranking pharmacies by commission, or showing a price the pharmacy has not confirmed.
- **Nigeria**: no home delivery (Part C.2); patients pay the pharmacy themselves (medicines are not in the Membership); many pharmacies are small and use a phone, so the QR and phone-desk paths stay and share one supply count.
