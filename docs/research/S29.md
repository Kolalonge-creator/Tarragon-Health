# S29 research: Care Circle invites, per-supporter permissions, pay for a loved one, supporter views

Date: 2026-10-06. Public sources only (help centres, support articles, published guidance). "Unverified" means I found no public document confirming it. Omada and Personify publish little about family sharing; Reliance, Hygeia and "MyHealth gift" diaspora plans were not researched in depth (search budget), so those claims are unverified. Adapt patterns, copy nothing.

## 17.1 Invite family or supporters by phone or email, in Nigeria or abroad

- Medisafe "Medfriend": the patient enters name, phone and email, or picks a contact; the invitee gets an email link and the invite stays pending until they approve. Free tier allows one Medfriend (Medisafe tips pages).
- Dexcom: sharer invites by email only, up to 10 followers, and can remove a follower at any time (Dexcom FAQs). Apple Health: invite from a contact, up to five people, the person must accept before seeing anything (Apple Support).
- Personify lets a member invite up to 10 friends or family to the platform; invitees get basic features but no rewards (Personify/Vanderbilt pages). Omada family cover is tied to the same health plan, so there is no free-form invite (unverified beyond FAQs).
- Tarragon should adapt: the patient types a phone (E.164) or email, picks a relationship and ticks permissions; an invite is a one-time link or code, accepted only by signing in, shown with the patient's first name and the exact permissions offered. Email is the default for abroad; phone invite goes by SMS code only if a real sender is available, else show a link the patient shares themselves (SMS sender approval still open, OQ-05).
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria / for a health app:
  - Guessable or reusable invite links, links that stay valid for weeks, links forwarded to the wrong person.
  - Phone-number enumeration: an "is this number on Tarragon?" check lets anyone map who is a patient. Return the same neutral response for known and unknown numbers.
  - SIM churn and SIM swap: Nigerian numbers are often recycled or swapped, so a number alone is a weak identity (Guardian NG, sent.dm guides). A stale invite to an old number could reach a stranger.
  - WhatsApp-only invite flows (WhatsApp is removed from Tarragon, F-02). Invite text must not name a condition or reading.

## 17.2 Per-supporter permissions with expiry and revocation

- Apple Health: the patient chooses topics per person (activity, heart, vitals, labs, cycle tracking) and can change or stop at any time (Apple Support).
- Dexcom: per-follower choice of which alerts they receive, with thresholds and time windows; the sharer can remove a follower instantly (Dexcom FAQs).
- NHS proxy access: the patient consents to named services (appointments, prescriptions, records), each proxy gets their own login, and access is withdrawn at the patient's request (NHS England, GP practice policies). Identity is verified face to face; Tarragon cannot copy that, so use sign-in plus a code confirmed by the patient.
- Tarragon should adapt: the five spec permissions as separate ticks (`adherence_summary`, `weekly_bp_trend`, `appointments`, `red_alerts`, `pay_for_care`), each off by default, an expiry date on every grant, a one-tap revoke by the patient, a visible "who can see what" list, and an audit row on every view.
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria / for a health app:
  - A supporter seeing readings without an explicit tick, or a broad "full access" preset.
  - Coercive control: a relative demanding access or watching a patient who cannot say no. Revoke must be silent (no "you were removed" notice sent that could cause conflict) and the patient must be able to hide the Care Circle screen from a shared phone.
  - Supporters inheriting permission from other supporters or from payment (see 17.3).
  - Health data is sensitive personal data under the NDPA 2023; consent must be specific and provable (cookieyes, securiti summaries; confirm with counsel).

## 17.3 Pay for a loved one at checkout

- Paystack accepts Visa, Mastercard and Verve from anywhere; 3D Secure is mandatory; foreign cards are charged at an international rate of about 3.9 percent (Paystack support, afrotools; confirm on the pricing page).
- Medfriend and Apple sharing have no payment concept; the nearest precedent is employer-sponsored Omada/Personify, where the member sees no price. Diaspora HMO gifting (Reliance, Hygeia) exists but details are unverified.
- Tarragon should adapt: the payer is a signed-in supporter with the `pay_for_care` tick; they pick an item the patient needs, see the naira price and fee warning for foreign cards, and pay. The order stores payer and beneficiary separately, the beneficiary gets the entitlement, the payer gets a receipt.
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria / for a health app:
  - A payer gaining clinical access because they paid. Paying grants no view of records, results or messages.
  - Refund routing: a refund goes to the original payer's card, not to the patient or a wallet (no stored value, INV-09).
  - Diaspora card declines: show a plain "your bank declined, try another card or ask your bank to allow it" message, never a generic failure; keep the order as awaiting payment until Paystack confirms.
  - Paying for an item the patient did not want: the patient can decline an offered item before it is used, with the refund rules stated first.
  - Receipt or email naming the item when it would reveal a condition (for example a diabetes programme); use a neutral item label on payer receipts.

## 17.4 Supporter view on web and app, working internationally

- Dexcom Follow and Apple Health show the follower a read-only view in their own app; Dexcom followers can tune their own notifications within what the sharer allowed (Dexcom FAQs, Apple Support).
- Medisafe's missed-dose alert goes 30 minutes after the dose and does not name the medicine, only that a dose was missed (Medisafe tips). That is the closest model to Tarragon's neutral alert rule.
- Tarragon should adapt: one supporter home showing only permitted tiles, each with "as of" time and a "Shared by [first name], until [date]" line; times shown in the supporter's own timezone with the patient's Lagos time noted; low-data layout; works on email sign-in from any country; no download of raw data.
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria / for a health app:
  - Notification text that names a condition, reading or result (Tarragon invariant: never). Use "[Name] needs a check-in. Open Tarragon."
  - Supporter view cached on a shared or public device after sign-out; use short sessions and no offline copy of patient data.
  - Showing a red alert to a supporter without a permission tick, or with a clinical reading attached.
  - International SMS that fails silently: never make a safety alert depend on one channel; in-app, push and email all fire.

## 17.5 Concierge care coordinator for supporters (dormant)

- Personify offers Personal Care Managers and advocates inside employer programmes (Personify press). Not a consumer concierge.
- Tarragon decision: configured dormant until staffing allows; build only the flag, no UI promise. Unsafe: marketing a concierge that does not exist, or "dedicated coordinator" wording (withdrawn 2026-07-31).

## 17.6 to 17.9 Out of S29 scope (S69)

- 17.6 Group challenges: Strava clubs and challenges and Personify team challenges are the references. S69. Unsafe: group lists that expose a member's health status in a church or workplace.
- 17.7 Group totals and anonymised rankings: Strava privacy controls (Everyone, Followers, Only You, mute activity, hide map areas) and Omada groups (limited profile, weight shared only with the coach) show the pattern. S69. Unsafe: rankings small enough to identify a person, any individual leaderboard.
- 17.8 Clinician-led live audio group sessions: Omada matches small peer groups with a coach (Omada FAQ). S69. Unsafe: recording or transcribing without consent, patients seeing each other's phone numbers.
- 17.9 Moderation, opt-out, notification controls: Strava has block, report to a Trust and Safety team, per-category notification switches and an opt-out of follower notifications (Strava support). S69. Take now only the principle that a supporter can mute alerts and a patient can block an inviter.

## What we take from this

1. Invite is a one-time token: store only a hash, expire in 72 hours or less (value PROPOSED, in versioned config), single use, bound to the invited email or phone.
2. Accepting requires sign-in plus a code sent to the invited address; the patient sees "pending" until then and can cancel it.
3. Per-patient and per-supporter invite rate limits; identical neutral responses for known and unknown numbers or emails, so nothing can be enumerated.
4. Permissions are separate ticks, all off by default; a supporter sees no clinical data unless the matching tick is on, enforced in the API and in RLS, with a test per permission.
5. Every grant has an expiry (PROPOSED default in config, renewable only by the patient); an expired grant behaves as revoked.
6. The patient can revoke at any time in one tap, immediately, with no notice sent to the supporter that names a reason; revocation is audit logged.
7. Red alerts to supporters are neutral (INV-07): no condition, reading or result in any notification, email or lock screen text; the detail stays inside the app behind the tick.
8. Pay for a loved one: payer and beneficiary stored separately; payer gets a receipt with a neutral item label only; payment grants no record access.
9. Refunds return to the original payer through Paystack; no stored balance or wallet.
10. Show naira prices only, warn before payment that foreign cards may cost more, keep the order "awaiting payment" through declines and late confirmations.
11. Supporter view is read-only, time-zone aware, low-data, with short sessions and a "shared by, until" label on every tile; no export.
12. A patient can hide the Care Circle screen and see a list of every view their supporters made; concierge stays a dormant flag; group features wait for S69.
