# S37 research: go-live guards and configuration sign-off

Compiled 2026-10-06 from general public knowledge of how these products and standards work. Nothing here was fetched live in this session; items marked UNVERIFIED are from memory and counsel or the CMO should check them. Adapted, not copied.

## Switching a clinical feature on (Healthily, Infermedica, Commure)

- Symptom-checker vendors (Healthily, Infermedica) describe their clinical content as written and reviewed by doctors, versioned, and released under a clinical safety process, not toggled by engineers alone. UNVERIFIED in detail. Adaptation: the guard is switchable only by a named role and only when conditions read from real records are met, and every switch is attributed.
- NHS Digital's clinical risk standards (DCB0129 for the manufacturer, DCB0160 for the deploying organisation) require a named clinical safety officer to sign before a health IT system goes live. Adaptation: `prescribing_enabled` is switched by the CMO, not by an admin.
- Commure-style integration platforms gate features per tenant behind configuration. Unsafe for us: a general feature-flag tool would let anyone with access flip a clinical feature. We use one purpose-built table with no write path except the evaluating function.

## Kill switch and drift

- Feature-flag practice (LaunchDarkly-style) treats the off direction as an emergency control that must never wait for approval. Adaptation: switching off is open to the founder and the CMO with an optional note.
- A flag left on after its precondition lapses is a known failure. Adaptation: the dashboard shows "on, but a condition is no longer met" and does not switch it off automatically, because an automatic switch-off of a live consultation service would itself be a patient-safety event (OQ-182).

## Sign-off of configuration values (Practo, Vezeeta)

- Marketplace platforms (Practo, Vezeeta) gate which doctors and services are visible behind verification steps recorded by operations staff. UNVERIFIED in detail. Adaptation: a PROPOSED value is "confirmed" only by its owner, with the signer, time and a hash of the exact value, so a later edit visibly voids the confirmation.
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria: a sign-off that silently applies to whatever the value is today. Hence the hash. Also unsuitable: a client-side-only check. Connectivity is intermittent, so the client check fails closed for booking only; the database refuses regardless, and emergency content is never behind any guard.

## Where it must not reach

- No guard may sit in front of red-event paging (INV-05) or offline emergency guidance (INV-06). The seven guards of spec 14 do not, and the wiring touches only consultations and the scribe start check.
