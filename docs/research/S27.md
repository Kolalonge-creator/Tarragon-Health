# S27 research: partner lab results, release rules

Written from what these services publish about how they handle results (help centres, public product descriptions). Nothing here was tested against a live account, and no figure is copied. Adapt, do not copy.

**Result entry by the lab (Helium Health, Reliance Health, Eka Care, Practo labs).** Labs enter values against a fixed analyte list per panel, with the unit and range pre-filled and a PDF attached as the legal report. Free-text ranges drift between labs, so Tarragon fixes the unit and range per panel version and rejects a different unit instead of converting it silently. Unsafe for Nigeria: trusting a flag typed by the lab; the database recomputes the flag from the value and the stored range.

**Release rules (Function Health, Everlywell, LetsGetChecked, InsideTracker).** Direct-to-consumer services release normal results straight to the app and hold abnormal ones for a clinician or a physician review before the person sees them; some call a person on a critical value. Tarragon follows the same split: all-normal auto-releases, anything else waits for a clinician, and a critical value is a higher-priority task than a routine one.

**Sensitive results (Everlywell, LetsGetChecked, Clafiya).** HIV and hepatitis positives are disclosed by a person, with counselling and a linkage plan, never by a screen. Tarragon: a positive on HIV, HBsAg or HCV Ab sets `clinician_disclosure_required`, no audio, no AI explanation, a disclosure task for a senior clinician, and no result text in any notification (INV-04, INV-07).

**Patient upload of an outside result (Healthily, CareClinic, Omada).** Apps let a person add a photo or PDF to their record and mark it as self-reported. Tarragon keeps the patient's own file visible to them, holds it as "added by you, not yet reviewed", and never reads values from it into the record without a clinician.

**Booking, collection and tracking (Vezeeta, Doctolib, Yodawy, mPharma).** A booking reference travels with the order, the partner marks collected, and the patient sees one plain status line. The platform already has specimen tracking and home collection; S27 only adds the partner's "collected" step on the order.

**Notification wording (Neko Health, Prenuvo, Withings).** These tell a person "your results are ready" and nothing about the content. Same here: one neutral template, never a condition, an analyte or a flag.

**Not copied or not suitable.** Fully automatic "explain my result" text for every result is not used (INV-04 and the AI rule); reference ranges are adult only and not adjusted for age or sex until the CMO signs ranges (OQ-176); lab-to-lab API push is a later release (`source = 'api'` exists in the table but nothing writes it).
