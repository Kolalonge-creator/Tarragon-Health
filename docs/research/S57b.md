# S57b research note: sourced redraft of the snoring and sleepiness questionnaire (C12)

Status: DRAFT, UNSIGNED. Nothing here is a clinical decision. The CMO reads, amends or replaces the instrument and confirms it through `public.confirm_sleep_apnoea_screen_config`; the build never calls it.

## What the published tool is (verified)
STOP-Bang (Chung et al.; STOP questionnaire, Anesthesiology 2008;108:812-21, extended with the four "Bang" items) has eight yes/no items, scored 0 to 8, one point per yes:
1. **S**noring: snore loudly, loud enough to be heard through closed doors or a bed partner nudges you.
2. **T**ired: often tired, fatigued or sleepy in the daytime.
3. **O**bserved: anyone has seen you stop breathing, or choke or gasp, in your sleep.
4. **P**ressure: you have, or are treated for, high blood pressure.
5. **B**MI more than 35 kg/m2.
6. **A**ge older than 50.
7. **N**eck: the AASM-hosted form of the tool says neck size or shirt collar "16 inches / 40 cm or larger", measured around the Adam's apple; the original paper used above 40 cm.
8. **G**ender: male.

Risk bands in the general population: 0 to 2 low, 3 to 4 intermediate, 5 to 8 high. A second route to "high" exists: two or more of the four STOP items plus male sex, or plus BMI above 35, or plus a neck of 43 cm (men) or 41 cm (women). A score of 3 or more is the usual cut-off for a sensitive screen. A 2021 systematic review and meta-analysis in the general population and commercial drivers reports, at 3 or more, a sensitivity of about 88 percent and specificity of about 42 percent for moderate to severe sleep apnoea (AHI 15 or more), and about 92 and 38 percent for severe (AHI 30 or more). So a cut-off of 3 refers many people who do not have the condition: a draft referral task is a clinician look, not a diagnosis.

## How the draft uses it
- Same eight items, scored one point each, cut-off 3 of 8 (PROPOSED config `sleep.apnoea_screen`, mirrored in `sleep_apnoea_screen_config` v1, a test fails on drift).
- Wording is the build's own plain wording preserving the meaning of each item (`sleep.screen.item.*`); no sentence is copied.
- **BMI** is not asked as a category the patient is unlikely to know. The patient enters height (cm) and weight (kg); the server computes BMI and scores it only when it is **above** 35 (exactly 35 does not score). Ranges refused: height outside 100 to 230 cm, weight outside 25 to 350 kg.
- **Neck** is a number in cm (20 to 80), measured around the Adam's apple, scoring at **40 cm or more** (the published form's reading; the CMO may choose "above 40"). The patient may choose "not sure".
- **"Not sure"** on any item, or an unmeasured BMI or neck, scores one point (`unsure_points` in the config), the cautious reading, and is counted in `unsure_count`. The CMO can set it to 0.
- The client never scores: it builds the answers and shows the server's reply. A result and a referral task exist only when the instrument is confirmed, the guard `sleep_apnoea_screen_enabled` is open and the cut-off is met.
- Not built: the second "high risk" route (needs sex-specific neck cut-offs), band wording to the patient (the patient sees one of two sentences, never a score), any diagnosis wording.
