# S08y research: NAFDAC verification channels, and the medicine-safety gaps

Status: research note. Public sources only, all read on 2026-10-20. Nothing here is a clinical judgement; it records what NAFDAC itself publishes so the how-to card in the app points at real channels and says nothing more than they do.

## 1. NAFDAC's own ways to check a product (what the card says)

| Channel | What NAFDAC publishes | Source (read 2026-10-20) | Used in the app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greenbook (registered product database) | A searchable register run by NAFDAC at greenbook.nafdac.gov.ng. Search by product name, active ingredient, NAFDAC registration number, applicant name or approval date; filter by category (drugs, vaccines, medical devices, herbals); results show status and approval date. | https://greenbook.nafdac.gov.ng (page text read directly) | Step 1 and a link ("Open the NAFDAC Greenbook"). |
| NAFDAC "Registered Products Database" / "NAFDAC Verify" | The same register is also reached from nafdac.gov.ng under Our Services ("NAFDAC Greenbook (Registered Drug Database)", "Registered Products Database"). Not found on any NAFDAC page read: a current verification mobile app by name, or an SMS short code for checking a registration number (as opposed to a scratch code). Neither is mentioned in the app.

## 2. Rules the how-to card follows

- Saving a NAFDAC number is a note. Nothing in the platform calls NAFDAC, and **Tarragon itself never sends an SMS** to check anything (INV-08): the person uses NAFDAC's own channels.
- The card says first that a saved number "does not tell you whether a pack is genuine. NAFDAC can." It never says genuine, fake, safe or approved (a test scans the card strings).
- The number format is the existing PROPOSED config `medicines.nafdac_number_format`; the database CHECK mirrors the same pattern, and the form refuses anything that is not shaped like a number (so the note cannot hold free text).

## 3. What the other gaps rest on (no new clinical content)

- **Same ingredient under two names.** Deterministic comparison of ingredient names after lower-casing, dropping punctuation, splitting combination names ("X and Y", "X / Y", "X + Y") and dropping a typed strength or dose form ("10mg", "tablets"). It does not know salts or synonyms (amlodipine besylate is not matched to amlodipine) and it does not know doses. A clean result is never "safe".
- **Interaction rules.** Ingredient pairs with a severity and a public source, in a versioned table that starts empty. This build authored no pair. Writing, citing and reviewing the rules is a pharmacist's and the CMO's work; the existing S53 dataset (class-based, 85 rules, unsigned draft) is a separate mechanism and is untouched (see the design note).
- **Brand to generic.** A brand mapping is only as good as its citation. Rows need an https source (for example the NAFDAC Greenbook entry for the product) and a reviewing clinician; none were seeded because none could be cited from this session without inventing it.
