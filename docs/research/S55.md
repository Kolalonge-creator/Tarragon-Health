# S55 research: Health Learning Centre (spec Module 9, functions 9.1 to 9.8)

Date: 2026-10-07. Method: what the reference products are known to do from their public product behaviour, plus the repo's own audit (`docs/research/S51-S55-review-and-build-plan.md`, Module 9). No live app-store screenshots were opened in this session, and no competitor figure is verified; treat the "what they do" lines as design inputs, not citations. Nothing here copies a product's text or design.

## 9.1 Multi-format content (articles, audio, illustrations, video, "when to seek care")
- Best products (Healthily, BabyCenter, Altibbi): short article first, audio or video as an alternative, a clear "when to see a doctor" block on condition pages.
- Complaints: video-only explainers that need data; long pages with the urgent advice buried; audio with no transcript.
- Unsafe or unsuitable for Nigeria: video by default (data cost, shared phones), any generic "see a doctor" line with no local meaning, audio that cannot be read when the phone is silent.
- Tarragon version: text is always present (audio and infographics always carry a text version), audio plays in the app when a native player exists and otherwise opens in the phone's own player, FAQ and infographic renderers, and a dedicated "when to seek care" category. Nothing in that category is written by the build: it stays draft until the CMO signs it.

## 9.2 Courses with lessons under five minutes
- Best products (Noom, Headspace): one short lesson a day, a streak, a reminder.
- Complaints: streak guilt, daily notifications, lessons that are really long reads.
- Unsuitable: daily pressure for people on shared phones and metered data. The founder prefers weekly pacing.
- Tarragon version: weekly pacing stays; Today shows one short lesson ("this week's lesson"), five minutes or less, enforced in the editor and by the database (a module over five minutes is refused). No streaks, no daily push. Progress and `lesson.completed` / `course.completed` events already exist from S33 (reused, not rebuilt).

## 9.3 Search in everyday and local terms
- Best products (Healthily): symptom-and-question led entry, forgiving to everyday words.
- Complaints: search that only matches clinical titles ("hypertension" misses "high blood").
- Unsafe: a model rewriting the query (cost, latency, hallucinated expansions) and any alias that silently broadens to the wrong condition.
- Tarragon version: one search across every category, plain full text plus a clinician-reviewable alias table (BP, sugar, high blood, pressure, "sugar disease"). Only aliases the CMO marked reviewed are used; seeded ones are draft. No model call.

## 9.4 "What can I do next?" at the end of every piece
- Best products: a closing action card (book, track, read next).
- Complaints: generic "see a professional" endings that do nothing.
- Tarragon version: a required field before publish (one sentence plus a kind: care plan goal, booking or another lesson), rendered as the same footer on web and mobile. A link to a lesson is dropped when that lesson is not currently servable.

## 9.5 Myth-busting series (Nigeria-specific)
- No direct reference. Existing marketing articles already cover blood pressure medicine myths, statin myths and diabetes myths.
- Unsafe: restating a herbal-remedy claim as if true, or ridiculing a belief. Needs clinician wording.
- Tarragon version: a series tag and an active (initially empty) programme; an admin tool copies a public marketing article into the in-app library as a draft, never reviewed, never published by the tool. Marketing copies stay public.

## 9.6 Clinical reviewer, sources, review date; offline downloads
- Best products (Healthily; Insight Timer for downloads): named reviewer and review date on the page; downloads for airplane mode.
- Complaints: a review badge that is just branding; downloaded content that never updates.
- Unsafe: an offline copy that keeps showing advice after its review date.
- Tarragon version: the credit is null-gated (reviewer name only when a review record is complete), linked to a verified clinician where known, sources shown, review date and next review shown. Downloads live in the existing on-phone SQLite store; an item is removed when its review date passes, with or without signal, and the Downloads screen re-checks with the server on open. D4: content past its review date is not served anywhere.

## 9.7 Clinician creator programme
- Best products (Insight Timer): open marketplace of teachers.
- Unsuitable: an open marketplace for health content, and any payment or payout logic here.
- Tarragon version: the opposite of open. Verified Nigerian clinicians only (active, credential verified, not suspended, not a care coordinator), an admin or the CMO approves, the piece goes into the existing clinical review, a different verified clinician must review it, the byline is the verified profile, creator series are a Members-only perk, and a creator who stops being verified disappears. No payouts. Ships dormant behind a platform module.

## 9.8 Share by link or email
- Best products: share sheet with a link; public article pages that rank.
- Unsafe: sharing a signed-in lesson (it carries the reader's progress), or a link that keeps working after the content expires.
- Tarragon version: a public page only for an item flagged public, reviewed, in date and not a Members item, rendered on every request so expiry takes effect at once; the share link carries only the content code. The signed-in lesson view stays gated. Email is a plain mailto link, never a server send.
