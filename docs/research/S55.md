# S55 research note: Module 9, Health Learning Centre

Sources: the plan `docs/design/S55-S60-build-plan.md` section 4.1 (which carries its own **V** verified / **U** unverified tags) and the repository audit done for it. No new web research was run for this build, so nothing below is upgraded from **U** to **V**; each line is a design input, not a claim about a competitor. Reference platforms were studied for behaviour only; nothing is copied.

## 9.1 Formats (Healthily, BabyCenter, Altibbi)
- Best: one page template, plain language, an explainer in text plus audio, optional short video. Complaints: long pages on slow data, video-first content that does not load. Nigeria: low bandwidth, shared phones, low literacy.
- Tarragon: text first, audio as an addition through the S32 manifest (`audio_clip_id`), images optional. Audio is wired through the existing audio service, which always shows the text when a recording or the audio engine is missing.

## 9.2 Daily lessons (Noom, Headspace) (**U**)
- Best: a few minutes, one action, one question. Complaints: streak pressure and guilt on a missed day.
- Tarragon: a daily micro-lesson card (under five minutes, one action, one check question, all enforced when a lesson is published), no streak, no score, no comparison. It stays the same all day, shows "done for today" once understood, and moves to the next lesson tomorrow.

## 9.3 Search in everyday words (Healthily; Nigeria-specific)
- Best: symptom and condition search that tolerates lay words. Unsafe: guessing that one word is another condition.
- Tarragon: a versioned synonym table (PROPOSED, CMO) expands "BP", "sugar", "high blood", "belle" to the clinical words; it never says one condition is another. One database search function for web and mobile; the phone searches its downloads with the same table when offline. Searches that find nothing are logged as an anonymous phrase and count (no patient, no organisation, no time of day, identifier-like phrases skipped, retention limit) so the clinical team can see what to write.

## 9.4 "What can I do next?" (Healthily)
- Best: every page ends with a next step. Complaint: generic "see a doctor" endings.
- Tarragon: a required block in the content template: an authored self-care step (publishing needs it), plus fixed "ask your care team" (saves the lesson to the next consultation, visible to the tied clinician, audited), "book a consultation", and a fixed non-editable "when to get urgent help" box that no author can change or remove. No emergency number is printed (numbers are an unconfirmed localisation fact, OQ-F1).

## 9.5 Myth-busting series (Nigeria-specific)
- Content needs clinical authors and herbal-remedy interaction cautions; an agent must not write medical claims.
- Tarragon: the series structure (a programme of kind `series`, inactive) and six clearly marked DRAFT placeholders (titles and "needs a clinical author" only). A placeholder cannot leave draft, cannot be published, and its flag cannot be cleared without a named clinical author and a real body.

## 9.6 Reviewer, sources, review date, offline (Healthily, Insight Timer) (**U**)
- Best: named reviewer, date and sources on every item; offline is a paid feature at some products.
- Tarragon: reviewer, review date, next review date, sources and creator credit shown on every item; publishing refused without them. Offline downloads are free: text (and audio counted against the cap), a size cap (PROPOSED), expired and unpublished items never included, an expired item is never shown offline even with no signal, refreshed on return to the app.

## 9.7 Creator programme (Insight Timer) (**U**)
- Open upload gives unvetted quality; unsuitable for clinical content.
- Tarragon: invite-only; an admin invites an existing clinician login; the clinician submits MDCN number, evidence and an indemnity confirmation; a different admin verifies; credited by name; suspension takes their published items back to review. Every item is still clinically reviewed before publishing. Revenue share is not built (needs a founder decision, OQ-S55-02).

## 9.8 Share by link or email (Nigeria-specific)
- WhatsApp sharing is how health content moves here, but WhatsApp is removed from the product (F-02), so sharing uses the phone's share sheet and a plain `mailto:` (nothing sent on the patient's behalf by us).
- Tarragon: `/learn/<code>` on the public site, articles only, published, clinically reviewed, shareable and in date; anything else is a calm 404. The link holds the content code only.

## Decision: what premium looks like here
A small, safe, honest Learning Centre: every item carries its reviewer, date and sources; nothing past its review date is ever served (server, search, daily card, share link, offline pack); the next step and the urgent-help box cannot be left out; no engagement gimmicks. Content volume and clinical authorship are the real constraint, not code.
