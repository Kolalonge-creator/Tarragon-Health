# S06z research note: what data costs a Nigerian patient, and what "Wi-Fi only" must never do

Scope: the data-cost meter and the Wi-Fi-only choice. No web research was run for this note (no network tool was used); the values below are the founder-confirmable PROPOSED defaults and the reasoning for them, to be checked.

## Why this matters here

Most Nigerian patients are on prepaid mobile data bundles; Wi-Fi is not a given. Data is a visible household cost and mobile data price rises in 2025 made it more so. A health app that quietly spends a patient's bundle is a reason to uninstall it, and a patient who uninstalls stops logging. Showing the cost builds trust; letting the patient defer the bulk of it onto Wi-Fi gives control.

## The price (PROPOSED, needs confirming)

- Default: 80 kobo per MB (N800 per GB), a mid-range prepaid bundle rate. Real plans vary widely (night plans, social bundles, bulk bundles are cheaper per MB; small daily bundles are dearer). So the figure is a labelled estimate and the note tells the patient their phone's own data screen is exact. OQ-S06z-1 asks the founder to confirm or replace the number against current MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile bundle prices. It is config, not code.

## What "Wi-Fi only" must never do (the safety reasoning)

1. **Never delay an urgent entry.** The app's reason for existing is that a dangerous reading reaches the care team. Anything flagged danger, every symptom, a patient's answer after a red alert, and kick and contraction logs are always sent at once.
2. **Never turn a taken dose into a missed dose.** The server marks an unanswered dose missed after 12 hours. A phone that stays on mobile data for days with an unbounded hold would feed the care team false misses and could trigger adherence follow-up for a patient who is doing everything right. So holds are capped at 6 hours.
3. **Never hide that something is waiting.** The banner says what is waiting and why, and offers "Send now on mobile data".
4. **Fail toward sending.** An unreadable setting, an unidentifiable connection, a VPN or tethered link: send.
5. **Never leave a new phone empty.** The first read is not skipped.

## What was deliberately not held

The BLE device-reading queue and the health-sample queue: a glucometer or a wearable can deliver an extreme value the phone cannot classify, and the server's red-flag engine (including the pulse emergency rule) is what judges it. Holding them would put a delay in front of that judgement.

## Open for decision

OQ-S06z-1 (the price), OQ-S06z-2 (the 6 hour cap and the list of urgent kinds are a clinical-safety call for the CMO to see, not only a product setting).
