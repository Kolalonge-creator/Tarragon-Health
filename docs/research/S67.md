# S67 research: pregnancy (module 16, functions 16.5 to 16.8)

Reference platforms are for studying behaviour only. Nothing is copied. Items marked [verify] come from memory or search summaries and must be checked against the source before the CMO signs. No live web checks were made in this session; the lines below rest on the S66-S70 build plan research (`docs/design/S66-S70-build-plan.md` section 4.2) and the CMO sign-off pack.

## 16.5 Week-by-week information and antenatal reminders (BabyCenter, Ovia Health)
- Best products give a short card per week (baby size, what to expect, what to ask), with a calendar of appointments and tests. Ovia ties it to symptom logging. [verify]
- Complaints: heavy advertising (BabyCenter), alarming or contradictory articles, content that does not match the local schedule or the local health system.
- Unsuitable here: long articles on a slow connection, US appointment names (the glucose test or the 20 week scan are not universal), adverts, community threads of unmoderated advice, reminders that name the pregnancy on a lock screen of a shared phone.
- Tarragon version: a small text-first week card (audio slot ready for the S32 manifest), bundled for offline use, written and reviewed by the CMO before any patient sees it (the table shows patients only reviewed rows). The antenatal schedule is generated from versioned config (WHO 2016 eight contacts, replaceable by the national schedule) and reminders are generic ("You have an appointment coming up", INV-07).

## 16.6 Pregnancy blood pressure (Nigeria-specific, no consumer reference)
- No consumer app runs a deterministic pregnancy BP pathway with an on-call page (build plan 4.2). Clinical anchors from the CMO pack: 140/90 raised, 160/110 severe, plus pre-eclampsia danger signs (ACOG PB 222, WHO, NICE NG133 [verify]).
- Unsafe patterns elsewhere: a single "high BP" banner with no action, thresholds copied from adult hypertension, an app that relies on a connection to alert.
- Tarragon version: reuse the shared triage engine (rule set v4 draft: v3 plus the swelling sign and BP-P6 for a fit or loss of consciousness), run on the phone offline with the pregnant flag and dating cached (INV-06), a red result pages on-call (INV-05). Draft only; the CMO signs.

## 16.7 Kick counter, contraction timer, birth-preparedness plan (BabyCenter; Nigeria-specific)
- Kick counters in consumer apps usually count to 10 and chart "average time". Complaints: false reassurance from a "normal" chart, anxiety from over-alerting, apps that suggest waiting and recounting. Guidance (RCOG Green-top 57, Count the Kicks style) says: know your baby's pattern and contact care when it changes; do not use a count to decide to wait [verify].
- Contraction timers usually show frequency and duration and say "go when 5-1-1" with no handling of preterm or fast labour. Many ignore the long-journey case that matters on Nigerian roads.
- Birth plans in consumer apps are free text and often ask for a hospital bag list. Unsuitable: anything that implies a wallet or payment (INV-09); Nigeria needs transport, money to set aside (a note), a blood donor and an escort.
- Tarragon version: the counter is optional, from week 28, builds her own normal after three finished sessions, and the only outputs are keep counting, target reached, or a fixed "contact your care team or go to a health facility today" card (never "wait", never "recount"). The timer says go or keep timing; instant signs and any contraction before 37 weeks are go-now. The birth plan holds place, transport, money as a kobo note, donor, escort and a long-journey flag that moves the timer to 7-1-1. All decisions are on the phone (offline) and synced through the outbox.

## 16.8 Nutrition, safe medicines, offline danger signs (Ovia Health; Nigeria-specific)
- Apps give food lists and "safe or not" medicine lookups. Complaints: contradictory lists, a "safe" answer for a medicine that depends on dose and stage, herbal products ignored.
- Unsafe: any automated "safe medicine" answer, and anything AI-generated in this path (INV-01, CLAUDE.md AI rules).
- Tarragon version: one fixed default line, "Ask your care team before you take any medicine, including herbal ones", plus CMO-reviewed content in a table that patients cannot read until it is reviewed. The danger-sign guide is bundled data in the app, outside any PIN lock, with an audio slot.

## Decision
Build the safety-critical, testable core now (rules, offline facts, session logic, tables with fresh RLS, guard). Do not invent clinical content: content rows ship empty and gated.
