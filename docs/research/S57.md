# S57 research note: Module 10 (2 of 2), meditation and sleep library, sleep tools

Sources: `docs/design/S55-S60-build-plan.md` section 4.2 (two web research passes; V verified by a fetched or searched source, U unverified), `docs/research/S56.md`, the two competitor reviews under `docs/research/`. No product was installed or copied; this session could not re-fetch product pages, so anything tagged U is a lead, not a fact. Nothing below is cited as clinical evidence.

## 10.4 Meditation and mindfulness library (Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer)
- Headspace and Calm sell a large audio catalogue organised as introductory courses, themed series (stress, sleep, focus) and single sessions, with offline downloads on the paid tier (U). Insight Timer is mostly free with a very large library, a timer and creator-led content (U).
- Complaints (U): subscription dark patterns, content that claims clinical effect, and a library too big to choose from. Nigeria: voices and examples that are not local, large audio files on metered data, a faith-neutral tone that does not fit users who want prayer or scripture-based reflection.
- Tarragon version: a small structured library (introductory, stress, grief, work, exams, faith-compatible reflection as structure only), single sessions and a plain timer, every item behind the same publish gate as learning content (named reviewer, review date, expiry hides it). Faith-compatible reflection ships as an empty series with draft placeholders; no spiritual text is invented. No streak, no score, no claim of effect.

## 10.5 Sleep stories, wind-down audio, soundscapes (Calm)
- Calm popularised sleep stories read by a calm voice, plus soundscapes (U). The clear gap for Nigeria is a story in a familiar voice and accent (plan section 4.2).
- Complaints (U): autoplay that keeps a phone awake all night, bright screens, large files.
- Tarragon version: the same library kinds (`sleep_story`, `soundscape`) with a sleep-friendly player (dark, a timer that stops playback), audio through the S32 audio manifest when it exists (seam: `audio_clip_id`), else a reviewed `audio_url`. Real recordings are creator or ops work; the build ships structure and draft placeholders.

## 10.6 Positive psychology and resilience activities (Happify)
- Happify gamified short activities (gratitude, savouring) with points (U). Evidence for these activities is mixed and effect sizes are small (U, not cited).
- Complaints (U): forced positivity, points that shame a low-mood day.
- Tarragon version: short scripted activities held as reviewed static scripts (`exercise` kind, `exercise_type = positive_psychology`), no points, no streak, never presented as treatment. S58 may reward completion by event id only.

## 10.7 Guided breathing, three to five minutes (Calm, Freespira)
- Calm has a paced-breathing bubble; Freespira is a regulated medical device with a sensor and a prescribed programme (U). The spec's Stage 1 breathing exercise BRE-01 is built by S33 (not on this branch).
- Unsafe or unsuitable: breath-holding for people with respiratory or cardiac disease, dizziness, claims to lower blood pressure.
- Tarragon version: a small in-house paced-breathing component (inhale, hold, exhale phases read from the reviewed library item, total length bounded by PROPOSED config to three to five minutes), a text-first alternative for people who cannot follow a moving circle, and a fixed "stop and seek care if you feel faint, in pain or short of breath" note. No medical claim. When S33 merges its BRE-01 component can replace the renderer; the data seam is the library item.

## 10.8 Private journal and scripted self-help exercises (Wysa)
- Wysa offers a journal-like space and a chat that walks through scripted CBT exercises, with a human coach tier (V for the coach tier; U for details). Woebot's consumer app closed in June 2025 (V), the LLM-regulatory lesson.
- Complaints (U): journals that sync to a server by default; exercises that read like a chatbot giving advice.
- Tarragon version: the journal is device-first and encrypted on the phone with a key that never leaves it; sync is off until the person turns it on, and what the server holds is ciphertext it cannot read. No staff, sponsor or Care Circle path exists (not even break-glass). Prompts are reviewed library items. Exercises are reviewed static scripts with steps, never generated text, never called therapy, always with the care-team and crisis note.

## 10.9 Local-language content by Nigerian creators; Wi-Fi downloads (Insight Timer; Nigeria-specific)
- Insight Timer lets creators publish and be discovered (U). No major product serves Nigerian languages well (U).
- Constraint: D-14 (2026-10-06) made the product English only, so the `language` column exists and defaults to English but no non-English UI or content is shipped; creator credit uses S55's verified-creator model once it merges (nullable link now).
- Tarragon version: downloads only on Wi-Fi, with a per-track size cap and a total pack cap as PROPOSED config; expired items are dropped from the pack; a failed refresh never empties it.

## 10.10 Sleep log, phone or wearable data, wind-down planner (Samsung Health, Oura)
- Samsung Health and Oura show a nightly sleep score and stages; Oura-style scores can cause "orthosomnia", anxiety about sleep numbers (U). Big Health's Sleepio uses a short daily diary and weekly feedback with a strong RCT base (U).
- Unsuitable: a single sleep score, sleep-restriction or "spend less time in bed" advice (a clinical intervention that needs a clinician).
- Tarragon version: the existing sleep log gains a short diary (time to fall asleep, night wakings), a weekly view that shows separate plain numbers (nights logged, average sleep, average time in bed, time asleep as a share of time in bed, bedtime range) and the change since last week as a difference, never a combined score, never advice to restrict. The wind-down planner turns the goal bedtime into a wind-down start time with a few optional steps and library items. Wearable sleep minutes remain informational (founder decision recorded in CLAUDE.md).

## 10.11 Snoring and daytime sleepiness questionnaire (Nigeria-specific)
- No reference platform; designed from the requirement. STOP-Bang is a published screening tool for obstructive sleep apnoea (U on the exact items and cut-off, so none is hard-coded).
- Tarragon version: an original-wording eight-item "STOP-Bang-style" DRAFT instrument whose items, points and cut-off live in versioned PROPOSED config awaiting CMO sign-off. A positive result creates a referral task only when the active instrument is signed, the guard is open and the cut-off is met. Unsigned: the answers are saved and nothing else happens, no result is shown, no task. Stored with the same per-patient access as mental-health data (patient, audited staff read, no Care Circle by default).
