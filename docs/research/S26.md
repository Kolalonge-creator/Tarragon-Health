# S26 — Entitlement, Subscription Lifecycle, Refund and Gifting Research

Research date: 2026-10-06. Sources: public help centres, terms of service, published pricing pages, and support articles only.

---

## 1. Omada Health (chronic disease management / digital therapeutics)

- **Entitlements after payment.** Access is employer/health-plan sponsored, never self-pay. Once enrolled through an eligible employer or insurer, the member gets full programme access (lessons, coaching, connected devices, app and web). Billing uses medical claims (activity and outcomes based), covered at 100 % with zero member copay.
- **Renewal / non-renewal.** There is no consumer renewal event. Coverage continues as long as the sponsoring employer or health plan maintains its Omada contract. When that contract or the member's employment ends, access is terminated and the account is closed; there is no way to continue out of pocket.
- **Refunds.** Fees are non-refundable per the Terms of Use. Because the member pays nothing directly, there is no consumer refund surface. Hardware purchased from the separate Omada Store is refundable within 30 days with proof of purchase.
- **Gifting / pay for a loved one.** Not available. Eligibility is tied to the employer or health plan, not transferable.
- **Nigeria suitability.** Entirely US-payer oriented (medical-claims billing model, US health-plan integrations). No relevance as a direct model for NGN consumer billing, but its employer-sponsored entitlement-gating pattern is instructive for the B2B/institutional tier.

## 2. Reliance Health (Nigerian HMO / health platform)

- **Entitlements after payment.** Individual or SMB purchaser selects a plan tier on the website, pays by card (Visa, Verve, Mastercard), and receives confirmation. Coverage begins immediately. Members access care at the hospital tier their plan covers (Tier 4/3/2 depending on plan). Each benefit type has an annual monetary limit; once exhausted, that benefit is unavailable until the next plan year.
- **Renewal / non-renewal.** Plans renew on the anniversary. The website offers monthly, quarterly, or annual billing. There is no published auto-renewal toggle in help docs; the purchaser appears to re-purchase or maintain card-on-file billing. When the plan lapses, access stops until renewed.
- **Refunds.** If a plan offer is not accepted and funds have already been deducted, a full refund is given. A 20 % "healthy member" cashback of the annual premium is available for members with a clean claims record. No partial or pro-rata refund policy is published for mid-term cancellation.
- **Gifting / pay for a loved one.** Explicitly supported. You create an account with your own (payer) details and assign the plan to the beneficiary (parent, spouse, friend). The "Alafia" elderly-care product line is marketed specifically for buying a plan for an older relative. Dependants (no published maximum) can be added to a plan. Family plans allow up to 4 family members at no extra cost on employer plans.
- **Nigeria suitability.** Directly Nigerian. Prices in NGN, local card rails, NHIA-regulated. The closest market peer to Tarragon's own institutional tier. Key design lesson: the payer/beneficiary split is a first-class concept at purchase time, not an afterthought.

## 3. Personify Health (corporate wellness; formerly Virgin Pulse + HealthComp)

- **Entitlements after payment.** Strictly B2B2C. The employer or health plan pays; the employee creates an account and gets access to wellness challenges, coaching, health assessments, care navigation, and a rewards store. Pricing is PEPM/PPPM/PEPY (per-employee or per-participant, billed to the employer).
- **Renewal / non-renewal.** Governed entirely by the employer's contract renewal. Members have no individual renewal decision. When the employer contract ends, membership terminates and unredeemed Rewards Cash is forfeited.
- **Refunds.** No consumer refund exists; the employer holds the contract. Unredeemed incentive balances are lost on account cancellation.
- **Gifting / pay for a loved one.** Not applicable. Access is tied to employment or plan membership, not purchasable for others.
- **Nigeria suitability.** US/UK employer-benefits market only. The PEPM billing model and rewards-store incentive structure are relevant as design input for Tarragon's corporate wellness / B2B tier, but nothing here is directly reusable for individual NGN consumer billing.

## 4. Insight Timer (meditation / wellness app with subscription tiers)

- **Entitlements after payment.** Free tier is generous (40,000+ guided meditations). MemberPlus ($9.99/month or $59.99/year) unlocks courses, offline downloads, and advanced features. Access activates immediately on payment confirmation. A 7-day free trial is offered before the first charge.
- **Renewal / non-renewal.** Auto-renews monthly or annually. The member must cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to prevent renewal. Cancelling stops the next charge but access continues until the paid period expires. Deleting the app does NOT cancel the subscription.
- **Refunds.** All subscription charges are explicitly non-refundable; no pro-rata credits for partial periods. Google Play purchases can request a refund within 48 hours by emailing support. Apple purchases go through Apple's own refund process. Website purchases go through Insight Timer support with no published time window.
- **Gifting / pay for a loved one.** Not available in the app. On the website, the workaround is to create an account with the recipient's email and subscribe using your own card, then contact support to remove the card so the subscription expires naturally after one term without auto-renewing. This is a manual, unsupported flow rather than a product feature.
- **Nigeria suitability.** Payments are USD via Apple/Google/Stripe; no NGN option. The non-refundable, no-pro-rata stance is the simplest possible model but may conflict with Nigerian consumer-protection expectations (the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act gives consumers a right to return/refund within a reasonable period for digital goods not yet consumed). The lack of a real gifting feature is a gap Tarragon should not replicate.

## 5. MyFitnessPal (fitness / nutrition app with premium tiers)

- **Entitlements after payment.** Free tier covers basic food logging and calorie tracking. Premium unlocks meal plans, macros, ad-free experience, and advanced analytics. Access activates immediately on purchase. Promotional/coupon codes can only be applied to accounts not already subscribed.
- **Renewal / non-renewal.** Auto-renews monthly or annually. Cancelling stops the next renewal; premium features remain available until the current paid period ends. No manual-renewal option is offered.
- **Refunds.** All Premium purchases are "final and non-refundable" per terms. In practice, refund routing depends on the purchase channel: Apple subscriptions go through Apple, Google through Google Play, web purchases through MFP support. Non-US residents get a 14-day cooling-off refund right. No partial or pro-rata refunds.
- **Gifting / pay for a loved one.** No official gift subscription feature exists. The community workaround is to send a Visa gift card or PayPal funds to the recipient. App Store / Google Play gift cards do not work because Premium is subscription-based, not a one-time IAP. Promotional codes exist but are not sold as consumer gift products.
- **Nigeria suitability.** USD pricing only, Apple/Google billing. No NGN rail. The 14-day non-US refund window is noteworthy as a precedent for respecting local consumer law in Nigeria. The complete absence of gifting is a clear gap; Tarragon's "pay for a loved one" requirement is a differentiator.

## 6. Calm (mental wellness app with subscriptions and gifting)

- **Entitlements after payment.** Free tier gives limited content. Calm Premium ($14.99/month or $69.99/year) unlocks the full library, Sleep Stories, music, and masterclasses. A Lifetime option exists at a one-time price. Access activates immediately on purchase or gift-code redemption.
- **Renewal / non-renewal.** Monthly and annual plans auto-renew. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the paid period ends. Cancelling does NOT trigger a refund automatically; a separate refund request is required.
- **Refunds.** 30-day refund window for purchases made through calm.com or Google Play. Apple purchases go through Apple support. Lifetime memberships, gift cards, Family Plans, and enterprise plans are explicitly non-refundable. Refunds process in 5-10 business days.
- **Gifting / pay for a loved one.** This is a first-class feature. Calm sells gift cards in 1-year and Lifetime denominations. The purchaser receives a code (emailed immediately or on a scheduled date, or as a printable PDF for Lifetime). The recipient redeems at calm.com/redeem. Gift codes never expire. The code cannot be stacked onto an existing active subscription; the recipient must let their current plan lapse first. Gift cards are non-refundable and non-redeemable for cash.
- **Family plan.** Calm Premium Family ($99.99/year) covers 6 accounts (1 owner + 5 invitees). Members do not need to live in the same household. Each member gets a fully independent, private account. All members must be 18+. Not eligible for Apple Family Sharing or Google Play Family Library; must be purchased directly from Calm.
- **Nigeria suitability.** USD pricing, international card or Apple/Google billing. No NGN option. The gift-code redemption model is the cleanest "pay for a loved one" pattern in this set and maps well to Tarragon's Care Voucher / sponsor model. The "code cannot stack onto an active subscription" constraint is worth noting as a design decision Tarragon should evaluate (Calm chose simplicity over flexibility).

## Summary of patterns relevant to Tarragon

| Concern | Pattern seen | Tarragon implication |
|---|---|---|
| Entitlement activation | Immediate on payment confirmation (all consumer apps) or on employer enrolment (B2B) | Tarragon's membership should activate synchronously on Paystack webhook confirmation, never on a polling delay |
| Auto-renewal default | Every consumer app auto-renews; the opt-out burden is on the member | Nigeria's FCCPA and NDPR may require clearer consent for auto-renewal than a buried ToS clause; Tarragon's non-auto-renewing annual membership (per v5 spec) is the safer default |
| Refund surface | Ranges from "non-refundable, period" (Insight Timer, MFP) to 30-day window (Calm). All route Apple/Google purchases through those platforms' own refund flows | Tarragon owns the full payment relationship via Paystack, so it must implement its own refund/pro-rata policy rather than deferring to a store. A 14-day cooling-off period aligns with consumer-protection norms |
| Gifting / pay for a loved one | Only Calm has a real, first-class gift feature (gift codes, scheduled delivery, redemption flow). Reliance Health supports payer/beneficiary split at purchase. MFP and Insight Timer have no gifting at all | Tarragon's sponsor + Care Voucher model is already more sophisticated than any of these. The Calm gift-code pattern (purchaser buys a code, recipient redeems independently, code never expires) is the closest analogue to a Care Voucher |
| Family / household sharing | Only Calm offers a Family Plan (6 seats, $99.99/year, no household requirement). Reliance supports dependants on a plan | Tarragon's individual-enrolment-only decision (no family plans) is a deliberate narrowing; if it ever reopens, Calm's "independent accounts, shared billing" model is the cleanest reference |
| NGN / Nigeria readiness | Only Reliance Health operates in NGN. All others are USD/international-card only | Tarragon's Paystack-only, NGN-only payment rail is a hard differentiator. None of these platforms' billing integrations are reusable; the design patterns (especially Calm's gift codes and Reliance's payer/beneficiary split) are what transfers |
