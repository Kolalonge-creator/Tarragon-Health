# A reflection of thanks

> DRAFT. Not approved. Do not publish, seed or generate audio until the Chief Medical Officer has signed off.

## Production notes (not spoken)

- Voice note: gentle, grateful, unhurried. Non-denominational. Do not add a closing blessing or a named faith.

## Narration (spoken)

Welcome. This is a short reflection of thanks.
It is open to everyone. You can think of your thanks as going to a person, to life itself, to your faith, or just to the moment. It is up to you.
Sit comfortably. Let your eyes close if that feels okay.
[pause 9s]
Take a slow breath in. [pause 4s] And out. [pause 9s]
Think of one thing you are thankful for today. It can be very small.
Water from a tap. A cool breeze. A message from a friend. A meal.
[pause 22s]
Let yourself really feel it. Where do you notice it in your body?
[pause 18s]
Now think of one person you are thankful for.
What did they do? What do they bring into your life?
If you like, silently say, "Thank you."
[pause 22s]
Now think of something about yourself you can be thankful for.
Maybe you kept going on a hard day. Maybe you were kind to someone. Maybe you came here and gave yourself a few minutes.
[pause 22s]
Breathe in. [pause 4s] And out. [pause 11s]
You can bring this feeling of thanks with you into the rest of your day.
Open your eyes when you are ready.
Thank you for being here.
