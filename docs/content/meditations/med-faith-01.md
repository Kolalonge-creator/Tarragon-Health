# A quiet reflection, in your own words

> DRAFT. Not approved. Do not publish, seed or generate audio until the Chief Medical Officer has signed off.

## Production notes (not spoken)

- Voice note: warm, neutral. Open to people of any faith or none. No religious claims. Prayer is offered as an option only. Do not name a faith, a deity, or a text.

## Narration (spoken)

Welcome. This is a quiet reflection. It is for anyone.
If you have a faith, you are welcome to bring it with you. If you do not, you are equally welcome.
There is nothing you have to believe to do this.
Sit comfortably. Close your eyes, if you like. You can stop at any time.
[pause 9s]
Take a slow breath in. [pause 4s] And out. [pause 9s]
Let your body settle. Feel the weight of it, held by the chair or the bed.
[pause 13s]
Bring to mind something that gives your life meaning or steadiness.
It might be your family. Your community. A belief. A memory. A place you love.
Let it come, in whatever way it comes.
[pause 22s]
If you pray, or if you would like to, this is a good moment.
You can pray silently, in your own words, in your own language.
If you do not pray, you can simply rest here and think kindly about the people you love.
[pause 33s]
Now think of something you are carrying right now. A worry. A decision. A hope.
Hold it gently. You do not have to solve it in this moment.
[pause 22s]
If you like, you can ask for steadiness, or for wisdom, or for peace. In your own words.
Or you can just rest, knowing you have done what you can for today.
[pause 26s]
Breathe in. [pause 4s] And out. [pause 11s]
Think of one person who has been kind to you lately.
You might silently wish them well.
[pause 18s]
Take a last slow breath. [pause 9s]
When you are ready, open your eyes.
You can return to this whenever you need it.
