# Source register

Status of every citation used in the DRAFT scripts, as of 2026-10-07. VERIFIED means the page was fetched and read in this drafting session. PARTLY VERIFIED and WEAK mean the reviewer must open the source before approval. Nothing here is clinical sign-off.

## S1. WHO, Hypertension fact sheet

- URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension
- Status: VERIFIED (fetched): most people with hypertension have no symptoms; very high readings may bring severe headache, chest pain, confusion and need care at once; about 44% of adults with hypertension are unaware. NOTE: the page's salt line reads 'under 2 grams' and appears to mean sodium; scripts use the 5 g salt figure from S2/S3.
- Used by: myth-01, myth-04, myth-05, bp-lesson-01, bp-lesson-08

## S2. WHO, Salt reduction fact sheet

- URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/salt-reduction
- Status: VERIFIED (fetched): adults less than 5 g salt a day (about one teaspoon), equal to under 2000 mg sodium; sodium is high in processed foods and condiments. Stock or seasoning cubes are NOT named on this page.
- Used by: myth-06, bp-lesson-05
