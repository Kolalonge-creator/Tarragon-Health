# Counsel brief: adult-only registration and dependants under 18

Prepared 2026-10-13 for the founder to send. Not sent by the assistant. Primary text of the NDPA 2023 and the NDPC GAID 2025 has not been read by the author; every legal statement below is a question, not a conclusion.

## Facts of the product
- Tarragon Health is a Nigerian chronic-care and preventive-health app. Registration is open to adults (18 and over) only.
- An adult may add their own children (under 18) as dependants on their account, for two purposes only: tracking immunisations and preventive health tracking (growth, screening reminders). A dependant under 18 has no consultations, prescriptions, messages, lab orders or reproductive, mental-health or HIV/STI data.
- The guardian is the only person who can view or change the dependant's data. When the child reaches 18 they register their own account and must agree before any record is handed to them (notice 30 days before the birthday).
- Health data is processed as sensitive personal data. Data is hosted in the EU (eu-west-1).

## Questions
1. Is the guardian's consent sufficient under the NDPA 2023 and GAID 2025 for processing a child's immunisation and preventive-health data for these two purposes? Is any additional verification of parental responsibility required?
2. What age does the law treat as a child for these purposes (we assume under 18), and does any healthcare or vital-interests exception change what consent we must record?
3. What must happen to the child's record at 18: may it stay with the guardian's account until the young person registers and agrees, and for how long? What retention applies if they never register?
4. Do we need a data protection impact assessment for children's data, and must the processing be mentioned in our NDPC annual return?
5. Is a consent receipt (text version, language, channel, time, who consented) adequate evidence of consent for the controller's burden of proof?
6. Cross-border: does hosting in the EU change any of the above for children's data?
7. Is anything in our plan to reject sign-up under 18 (date-of-birth check, calm refusal message) problematic?
