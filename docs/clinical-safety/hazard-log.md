# Hazard log for the four journeys (PROPOSED, unsigned)

Status: **PROPOSED by engineering. Not reviewed or signed by the Chief Medical Officer or a clinical safety officer.** It follows the shape of a
DCB0129-style log (hazard, cause, harm, existing control, how the control is checked, residual risk, owner) as a structure only; nothing here is a
claim of compliance with that standard, and the severity and likelihood columns are left for the clinical owner to fill. Do not quote this file as
a safety case. `packages/shared/src/journeys/hazard-log.test.ts` keeps it in step with the journey definitions: every hazard id a journey step
cites must exist here, and every row here must cite a real journey step that cites it back. Whether a step is hazardous enough to tag is a judgement for the clinical owner; the test cannot make it.

How to read a row: **Control** is what the platform does today. **Check** is the test or proof that exercises it (it may need a real database, see the
PR that added this file). **Gap** is what is still open. Severity and likelihood are the clinical owner's to set.
