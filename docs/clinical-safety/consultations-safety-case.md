Prepared by the assistant on 2026-11-03 for the Chief Medical Officer, Dr Kola Longe, who is also the founder and the DPO and the sole reviewer of this platform (no second reader is required; the residual risk of having no independent check is stated once in HZ-CON-12 and gates nothing).** It becomes v1.0 when the CMO reads it, corrects it, signs the block at the end, signs the matching line on the sign-off hub, and records the attestation `clinical_safety_case_current` on the Clinical operations guard. Nothing here claims compliance with a standard; the shape follows a DCB0129-style hazard log as a structure only. Ratings marked `(s)` are the assistant's suggestions and are the CMO's to change.

Guard this document supports: **`clinical_operations_enabled`** (a master switch, pressed by an admin). It is the only one of its five conditions still unmet on 2026-11-03: the approved hypertension protocol (`htn_rtsl_ng` v1, approved by the CMO from chat on 2026-11-02), the approved blood pressure triage rule set (`bp_care_triage` v8, 2026-10-29) and at least one active senior clinician (2 real) are all read from the data and are met. The same attestation code is also a condition of `prescribing_enabled` (a master); this document does **not** cover prescribing (see HZ-CON-06) and covers the AI scribe only as it runs inside a consultation (`scribe_enabled` is a feature switch, already on, with no condition of its own).

## 1. What the switch opens, and what it does not (read from `go_live_guards` on 2026-11-03)

**Opens:** holding and confirming a consultation slot (`hold_appointment_slot`, `confirm_appointment_booking`), the consultation room (`service_get_encounter_room`, the join action), the older video-visit request path (`video_visit_requests` insert, `accept_video_visit_request`, `select_video_visit_alternate_slot`) and the patient booking screen. The scope is any remote clinician appointment, so a `gp` or `follow_up` video visit cannot go around it. A booking where both people are `is_test` already passes (the S37 pair rule), which is how the flow has been exercised so far.

**Does not open, by design:** the 25 feature switches whose parent is this master (the condition pathways, fasting safety, the therapy programmes, live red-flag prompts, the symptom checker, post-consultation check-ins and the rest) each stay closed until an admin or the CMO opens them one by one from the go-live page. Clinical tasks (live since S16), lab-result consult requests (a live flow) and care pack sales (not built) were never behind it.

**Still closed by their own masters, and this document assumes they stay closed unless the CMO decides otherwise:** `checkout_enabled` (a real patient cannot buy a consultation credit until it is on; its two attested conditions were recorded by the founder on 2026-11-02), `prescribing_enabled` (a doctor cannot sign a prescription or send one to a pharmacy), `on_call_cover_ok` (not wired to consultations).

## 2. What the consultation path does (read from the live definitions, the S21, S37 and S64 design notes and the code on 2026-11-03)

1. **Booking.** A patient with a visit credit (a Membership entitlement or a bought `video_visit_credit`) picks a clinician and a slot from confirmed `availability_blocks`. The hold lasts 10 minutes; the booking is confirmed on payment or on redeeming a credit. Adults only: a trigger refuses anyone under 18 and fails closed when no date of birth is on file. Policy in force is `consultation_policy_config` v2 (active, PROPOSED): cancel window 2 hours, a late cancellation keeps the credit, a clinician cancellation or no-show always returns it, no cash refund (OQ-133), join window 15 minutes before to 60 minutes after, reconnect grace 120 seconds, clinician no-show wait 15 minutes, patient no-show wait 10 minutes, chart access after finishing capped at 72 hours until a signed note exists.
2. **Intake.** The patient may send a structured intake (reason, duration, up to five fixed answers in their own words). It is summarised by plain code, never by a model, and the text is **not** screened for danger; the screen carries the standing "go to the nearest hospital now" line instead (OQ-S64-3). The assigned clinician reads it through an audited function; a draft is never visible to them.
3. **Room and fallback ladder.** Zoom, link based, with the in-app Meeting SDK where the SDK keys exist (they do, in production). Video to audio-only to Zoom's own dial-in phone number; nothing is rung from our side. Recording is off and asserted off in the room row. A waiting room is on, so the clinician admits the patient. One caregiver may be invited per call (`consultation_caregiver_config` v1: one caregiver, invite valid 240 minutes). Only the server writes the final media mode and the join records.
4. **Presence and no-show.** A person's join is recorded when they are handed the way in (link, SDK or dial-in), inside the join window. Real presence from Zoom's webhook is behind `ZOOM_PRESENCE_WEBHOOK`, which is **not set** in production, so the safe default applies: a patient who asked for a way in cannot be marked absent. A patient who joins only by phone is never seen by any webhook (OQ-160, open).
5. **Record.** The clinician writes or signs a note (`clinical_encounter_notes`); an AI-drafted note cannot be signed until the allergy and medicine lines have been confirmed, and any edit clears that confirmation. The patient reads the published summary only, never the clinical note (founder decision 2026-10-01). The AI scribe (`scribe_enabled`, ON) runs only with the patient's own in-app consent for that consultation; switching the scribe off revokes every open consent.
6. **After the visit.** `consultation_follow_ups` exist for the clinician to record a plan. The 48-hour and 7-day check-ins are behind `post_consult_checkins_enabled` (a feature switch, OFF; its configuration v1 was approved by the CMO on 2026-10-27, so opening it is one click on the go-live page). A red reading after the visit goes through the signed triage rule set and the paging path, which this switch never touches (INV-05).

## 5. Decisions recorded 2026-11-03 (CMO, from selectable prompts in chat)

1. **HZ-CON-02 and HZ-CON-11 (first call):** one real consultation is run end to end (book, join from a phone, drop to audio, dial in by phone, finish, sign the note) with a known patient before any other patient is booked. The switch may go on for that call; it is the first visit, not a rehearsal with test accounts. The Zoom dedicated host user, paid plan and Nigerian dial-in number are confirmed on that call.
2. **HZ-CON-06 (prescribing):** paid consultations open while `prescribing_enabled` stays closed. A visit can end in advice, a referral, a downloadable prescription form or a titration proposal under the signed step table, not a prescription sent to a pharmacy. Follow-up: the booking terms card is to say so.
3. **HZ-CON-07 (check-ins):** `post_consult_checkins_enabled` is opened in the same sitting (its configuration v1 is already signed).
4. **HZ-CON-08 (checkout):** `checkout_enabled` is opened in the same sitting so a Free patient can buy a visit credit.
5. **HZ-CON-10 (paging):** both. Email plus the in-console alarm is accepted as the working page path now so consultations can open; both doctors also re-enable push on their devices and one test page is acknowledged, as soon as possible rather than as a blocker. If no push arrives, email keeps working.
6. **Ratings:** the severity and likelihood cells in section 4 are accepted as written and are the CMO's own.

## 6. First-week check (suggested)

After the first real consultation: read `encounters` and `encounter_events` for that visit (joined, mode changes, completed), confirm the note was signed within 72 hours, confirm no notification named a condition, check whether the patient's join was recorded by the webhook or only by the hand-in path, and read `go_live_guard_log` for drift notices. If a visit is left `scheduled` with no join from either side, treat it as a clinician no-show and check the credit was returned. If anything is unexpected, switch `clinical_operations_enabled` off: a call under way is never ejected, only new bookings and joins stop.
