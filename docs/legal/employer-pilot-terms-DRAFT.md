## 1. What is offered
1.1 Tarragon Health Ltd ("Tarragon") offers `[CONFIRM: employer legal name]` ("the Employer") a pilot of **[N] Membership seats** for people the Employer employs. One seat gives one eligible employee one Membership for twelve months from the day they join.
1.2 The Membership is the same Membership Tarragon sells to individuals. It is not insurance, not a health plan and not a substitute for emergency care.
1.3 Individual enrolment only. There are no family or dependant seats. There is no per-head payment for a population (no capitation).

## 2. Price and payment
2.1 Each seat is priced at the Employer's organisation Membership price in force on the day the pilot is created, in Nigerian naira: **NGN `[CONFIRM: per seat]`**. The total is seats multiplied by that price: **NGN `[CONFIRM: total]`**. The price is fixed for this pilot once created.
2.2 Tarragon issues one invoice for the whole block. The Employer pays by bank transfer or, where Tarragon sends one, a Paystack payment link. `[CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL: VAT and withholding tax treatment, invoice wording, and whether the Employer needs a purchase order.]`
2.3 Seats are granted only after Tarragon has confirmed the payment. People who join the Employer roster before then wait and receive their seat when payment is confirmed.
2.4 `[CONFIRM: refund position.]` Proposed: no refund of a paid block. Tarragon may, at its choice, cancel an unpaid block without charge.

## 3. Claiming seats
3.1 Seats are claimed by employees who are on the Employer's roster and who join Tarragon with the work email or phone number the Employer supplied, or with the Employer's join code. Seats are given in the order people join.
3.2 The pilot has a **claim window** that opens when Tarragon confirms payment and closes on `[CONFIRM: end date]` (the offer date is `[CONFIRM: start]`). A seat not claimed by the end of the window lapses. A seat claimed on the last day of the window still runs for the full twelve months from that day. It is not carried over and not refunded. People who join after the window or after every seat is taken do not receive a seat and are told so by their Employer contact.
3.3 If an employee leaves the Employer, their seat ends. The seat is not given to someone else. `[CONFIRM: whether a departed employee keeps the Membership year already paid for. Proposed: the seat ends when the Employer reports the departure.]`
3.4 An employee who already holds their own Membership is not given a seat while it lasts and the Employer is not charged for them in that time.

## 4. What the Employer sees, and never sees
4.1 The Employer receives a report of **counts only**: seats purchased, claimed, active and unclaimed, and how many members used the service (for example had a consultation or logged a reading in the app).
4.2 The report is prepared so that no person can be picked out. Figures for fewer than the platform minimum group size (`[CONFIRM: currently 20 people, the one rule Tarragon applies to every institution report]`) are withheld. Figures are rounded and cover whole weeks. The Employer must not try to work out who is behind a figure, and must not combine the report with other information to do so.
4.3 The Employer never sees any employee's health information, what an employee discussed with their care team, any reading or result, or whether a named person used the service. The Employer's roster shows who has joined, which the Employer already knows because it supplied the roster.
4.4 Tarragon will not ask the Employer for, and the Employer must not ask an employee for, access to an employee's health information as a condition of employment, a benefit or a seat.
4.5 The Employer will not use the Membership or any report to make an employment decision about a named person.

## 5. What Tarragon does not promise
5.1 Tarragon makes no promise of any health outcome, saving, reduction in absence, or return on spend. Membership is a service; the report describes use, nothing more.
5.2 The service depends on employees using it. Tarragon does not guarantee a level of uptake.

## 7. Ending the pilot
7.1 Either party may end the pilot on `[CONFIRM: notice]`. When the pilot ends, members keep the Membership they were given for the term already granted. No new seats are granted.
7.2 After the pilot Tarragon and the Employer review use of the seats together, using the same aggregate report. Any further purchase is a new offer on new terms.
