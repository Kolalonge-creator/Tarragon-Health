# Research data: successor and insolvency clause (DRAFT for counsel)

Status: DRAFT. Not reviewed by counsel. Do not publish, and do not tell members that this rule exists, until a Nigerian lawyer has confirmed the wording and where it sits (OQ-737).

## Purpose
Members agree to research use of their de-identified data on the understanding that it is used only for the approved purpose and never sold. This clause says what happens to that promise if Tarragon Health is sold, merged, restructured or wound up.

## Draft wording

1. **No transfer of research permissions by sale.** If Tarragon Health, or the part of it that holds member data, is sold, merged or transferred, every approved research protocol is suspended on the day of the transfer. No new export is released under any protocol until the data protection officer of the successor has re-confirmed it in writing and the members' consent records have been checked against it.

2. **Same limits bind the successor.** The successor takes the data on the same terms: research only under an approved protocol, only for people who agreed, only de-identified, never sold, licensed or shared with an insurer, health maintenance organisation, employer, pharmaceutical company, advertiser or data broker. A change that weakens any of these needs a fresh, specific, opt-in consent from each member. Silence is not consent.

3. **Withdrawal survives the transfer.** A member who has withdrawn research consent stays withdrawn. The successor must honour the withdrawal list from day one.

4. **Insolvency or wind-up.** If Tarragon Health is wound up, research exports stop. The data protection officer (or the liquidator acting on that officer's written advice) arranges secure deletion or return of member data, and may keep only what the law requires. No research dataset or participant key is sold as an asset.

5. **Recipients of past releases.** Each data-sharing agreement keeps the recipient bound to delete or return released files when the protocol closes, and not to re-identify anyone. These obligations are not released by a transfer of Tarragon Health.

6. **Notice.** Members are told, through the app and by email, before any transfer takes effect, and can see the research list and ask a question or object at `/patient/research`.

## Questions for counsel
- Does the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 require anything beyond this on a transfer of a data controller's business?
- Where should this sit: the privacy notice, the terms, each data-sharing agreement, or all three?
- Can a liquidator be bound by a contractual promise not to sell a dataset, or does it need a statutory basis?
- Is a "no sale as an asset" term enforceable against a purchaser who is not party to the member agreement?
