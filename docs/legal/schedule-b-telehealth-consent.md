## About this document
This is TarragonHealth's Telehealth Consent, Schedule B of our legal documents. It sits alongside our Terms of Service and our Data Processing Consent.

## What this consent covers
This is your consent to receive clinical monitoring, classification and review from TarragonHealth remotely, and to be contacted by phone, message or in the app as part of that care. It is separate from, and in addition to, our Data Processing Consent and our Terms of Service.

Your plan decides how much of this involves a doctor. On Membership and Membership Plus, a doctor reviews your readings and results as part of your care. On the Free plan, the app checks your readings against the same rules and tells you what to do, but no doctor reviews them. The Terms of Service explain the plans.

## Your care team
Your care is provided by doctors registered with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN). Their registration is verified before they can see patients and checked continuously afterwards. A doctor whose registration or indemnity cover lapses is automatically suspended from seeing patients overnight, with no manual step needed. Our doctors review readings under clinical protocols, start and adjust medications, and refer you on when needed. Care is shared across a team, so you may speak to different doctors at different times, and each one can see your record. Our Chief Medical Officer oversees the care team and signs the clinical protocols that everyone works under.

Non-clinical coordinators may also be involved in your care. They follow up if we have not heard from you, and they help with bookings and other practical matters. They can see your progress record, but they cannot read your clinical notes, interpret a result, adjust your medication or give clinical advice. Anything clinical is always handled by a doctor.

Every clinical note you can read shows the treating doctor's name and MDCN number.

## How your results are classified and how we respond
Every reading you log is classified automatically against a clinical protocol suited to its type. Even a normal reading gets a record, so nothing is silently skipped. Separately, an AI model may draft a plain-language summary of your record to help your doctor prepare for your case. It never classifies a result itself, never diagnoses and never closes a case. A summary is always labelled as AI-drafted until a doctor has actually reviewed it.

How quickly we respond depends on how a finding is classified. A routine screening result and a dangerous blood pressure reading carry different urgency, so there is no single flat response time. For Members, as a general guide:

- Routine findings are usually reviewed within days, as part of a batch.
- Findings that need attention but are not urgent are usually reviewed by a doctor within 3 days.
- Urgent findings usually get a phone or video call within hours, not a message alone.
- Emergency findings get immediate contact. If we cannot reach you, we document an attempt to reach your emergency contact.

A finding classified as an emergency cannot be closed on a written note alone. Your doctor must first have a real, live phone or video conversation with you before the case can be marked resolved.

These are standards we hold ourselves to. They are not a guarantee of a particular outcome in every case. How quickly we can reach you also depends on you being reachable.

On the Free plan, no doctor reviews a reading you log, so these times do not apply to readings. They do apply to two things on every plan: an abnormal screening result, and a critical laboratory result. Both are sent to a doctor whichever plan you are on.

## How we communicate with you
Different channels carry different kinds of information, on purpose.

- **The app is your record.** Your ongoing conversation with your care team, your results, your care plan and your consent history live here. The in-app conversation is the main channel for anything clinical.
- **A phone or video call is how clinical judgement is delivered.** If something in your results needs discussing, a doctor calls you by phone or video, and a note of the call is added to your record.
- **Two-way conversation with your care team happens in the app only.** Send a message any time. A real person on your care team reads it and replies there. No automated system turns what you send into an action on the platform.
- **Push notifications and in-app alerts** tell you that something is waiting for you, such as a reminder, a new message or a result to read. The detail is in the app.
- **Email** carries receipts, reminders and copies of your consent records. It does not carry your results or clinical advice.
- **Text messages (SMS)** are used only for phone verification codes. Care conversations happen in the app and by phone or video.

## What remote monitoring can and cannot do
Remote monitoring lets your care team review your readings and respond between visits, without you needing to travel for every check. It has real limits. Your doctor cannot examine you in person, and their assessment rests on the readings and information you provide. If something needs an in-person assessment, your care team will tell you and refer you appropriately, including, where relevant, to one of our partner laboratories, pharmacies or specialists.

## Emergency care
TarragonHealth does not provide emergency care. If you think you or someone with you is having a medical emergency, do not wait for the app, a message or a call from us. Call the emergency number for your area or go to the nearest hospital straight away. Ask someone to go with you if you can. No part of this platform is designed to be used in an emergency.

If you are on the Free plan, no doctor is alerted when you record a reading or symptom that looks dangerous. The app shows you what to do straight away, which is to seek emergency care or go to the nearest hospital. It tells you plainly that no doctor was alerted because you are on the Free plan. If you are a Member, a dangerous reading is seen by our care team as part of your care. That is still not an emergency service. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital first.

## Confidentiality
Your clinical information is kept confidential. It is shared with your care team on a need-to-know basis. Outside the care team it is shared only with your specific consent, including a family member or sponsor you have separately agreed to give view access to, where the law requires disclosure, or where there is a serious threat to your life, health or public health. If an organisation pays for your Membership, it sees only aggregate figures about its whole covered group and never your individual results. See our Data Processing Consent for how we handle your data more broadly.

## Your right to information and to take part in your care
Every message and note tells you who is speaking to you, with their MDCN number where relevant. You can see every clinical action taken on your record, in plain language, at any time.

## Your right to consent, and to refuse
You can decline a specific recommendation. Declining does not end your access to the rest of the platform, unless what you are declining is safety-critical. In that case we will tell you clearly why.

## Withdrawing this consent
This consent is needed for the care we provide, so we cannot keep your account open without it. If you no longer agree, ask us to close your account and we will do so. Closing your account ends remote clinical review from that point on. It does not delete your existing health record, which we keep as set out in our Data Processing Consent. Declining a single recommendation, as described above, is different and does not need you to close your account.
