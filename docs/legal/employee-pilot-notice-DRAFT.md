# Employee Notice: Membership through your employer (DRAFT)

**STATUS: DRAFT. NEEDS NIGERIAN COUNSEL AND DPO REVIEW. NOT FOR SENDING OR SHOWING IN THE APP.** Written 2026-10-16 (docs/design/EMP.md). Plain-language text meant to be shown to an employee before they accept a seat. Counsel must check that it is accurate against the signed employer terms and the privacy notice, and decide whether it is a notice, a consent, or both. `[CONFIRM: ...]` marks open points.

---

**Your employer has offered you a Tarragon Membership.**

**What you get.** A Membership for twelve months, paid for by your employer. You can use it the same way as anyone who bought their own.

**What your employer knows.** Your employer gave us your work email (or phone number) so we can match you to the offer. Your employer knows you were offered a seat and, on its roster, whether you have joined. That is all it learns about you personally.

**What your employer never sees.**
- Anything about your health.
- What you talk about with your care team.
- Your readings, results, medicines or visits.
- Whether you, by name, used the service.

**What your employer does get.** Totals for the whole group, for example how many people joined or how many had a consultation. We hold back a total if it is for too few people, so nobody can be picked out. We round totals and report them by whole week.

**Your choice.** Joining is your choice. Your employer cannot make you join and cannot ask you for your health information. If you do not join, nothing changes at work. You can leave at any time `[CONFIRM: what happens to the rest of the year and to your records on leaving]`.

**If you leave your employer.** Your employer cover ends when your employer tells us you have left. You can then keep your account and choose to buy a Membership yourself, or use the Free plan. Your health records stay yours.

**Your records.** Your health record is held by Tarragon under our privacy notice `[CONFIRM: link]`, the same as anyone else. Your employer is not part of it.

**Questions or complaints.** `[CONFIRM: contact for the data protection officer]`.
