## What TarragonHealth is, and isn't
TarragonHealth is a care coordination platform connecting you with doctors, labs, pharmacies, and specialists across chronic disease management, preventive screening, and care coordination, for individuals and through employer/HMO programmes. We do not own or operate any hospital, clinic, laboratory, or screening facility. We are not an emergency service — see Schedule B. We are not an insurer: nothing on the platform is capitation, claims cover, or any form of risk transfer — this is enforced structurally in our systems, not just stated as policy — and where we bill an organisation for its members' access, that billing is a service fee, never a per-member premium.
