## Eligibility and accounts
You must be at least 18 years old to hold your own account. An adult may enrol a child under 18 as their dependant and manage their care, under our Guardian and dependant data terms. Each person, including a minor, has their own individually-structured account — we do not offer a shared household or family account or a multi-person discount. Separately, you may name a next of kin who can be contacted in an emergency and, with your consent, view (not edit) your record, and — for an adult who wants help managing their own care — you may set up a two-sided arrangement letting another adult manage bookings on your behalf; either of you can end this at any time. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide, including anything you record on a dependant's behalf, and for keeping your login secure.

## What TarragonHealth is, and isn't
TarragonHealth is a digital-first chronic disease, preventive health, and care-coordination platform: we measure your readings, classify them against a clinical protocol, connect you with a doctor when something needs attention, and coordinate labs, pharmacies, and specialist referrals on your behalf. We do not own or operate any hospital, clinic, laboratory, or screening facility — every lab, pharmacy, and specialist you use through the platform is an independent partner. We are not an emergency service — see Schedule B. We are not an insurer: nothing on the platform is capitation, claims cover, or any form of risk transfer, and where we bill an organisation for its members' access, that billing is a service fee, never a per-member premium.

## Your subscription
All plans are priced in Naira; the same plan costs the same amount whichever way you pay, whether by a Nigerian payment method or an international card. We do not operate a separate foreign-currency price list. Your subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing period unless you turn off auto-renewal before the renewal date; turning off auto-renewal doesn't end your access early, it just stops the next charge.

## The Health Wallet
You may hold a Health Wallet balance, funded by you or, with your consent, by a family member or sponsor — including one based abroad. A wallet top-up is spendable within the platform on bookings and orders; it is not a bank account and is not withdrawable as cash. To limit how large an unverified wallet balance can grow, we apply a lower balance ceiling by default, raised once you complete an identity check. ## Held payments for doctor video visits
A paid video visit with a doctor works differently from a subscription charge: your payment is held by us at the time you request the visit, and only goes through once a doctor accepts the request — a visit is never automatic, and depends on doctor availability. If no doctor accepts your request within a set window, or a doctor declines it, your payment is refunded.

## Cancelling and refunds
You can cancel a subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancelling stops it from renewing; you keep full access through the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not provide a partial or pro-rata refund for the unused portion of a period you cancel mid-cycle, except where required by applicable law, where we decide, at our discretion, that a billing error or a service failure attributable to us warrants one, or as described above for a held video-visit payment that is never accepted.
