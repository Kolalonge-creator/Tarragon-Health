Prepared 2026-09-04 against **PRICELIST 2026** (1,309 priced lines, sheet `listPrices (31)`).

Everything below blocks pricing decisions on the Tarragon side. Questions 1 and 2 are the
two that matter most: question 1 decides whether our margin model works at all, and
question 2 could halve the cost of our largest product.

## 2. Bundle contents — six component lists

The price list gives a name and a price for each of these and nothing else. We would like
the component test list for each, so we can tell where buying your bundle beats assembling
the same panel line by line.

| Bundle | Price |
|---|---:|
| Basic Screen | ₦79,900 |
| Blue Wellness Test | ₦86,000 |
| Gold Wellness Test | ₦487,300 |
| Platinum Wellness Test (Female) | ₦494,700 |
| Platinum Wellness Test (Male) | ₦494,700 |
| Menopausal Screen | ₦87,500 |
| Premarital Panel 1 | ₦116,400 |
| Premarital Panel 2 | ₦106,300 |

Two specific sub-questions we care about most:

- **What is the difference between Premarital Panel 1 and Panel 2?** In particular, does
  either include **haemoglobin electrophoresis** (the full genotype, ₦31,400 on your list)
  rather than only a qualitative sickle screen (SickleScan, ₦18,500)? For premarital
  counselling we need to distinguish AA from AS from SS, which a qualitative screen cannot
  do — so this determines whether we can resell your panel as-is.
- **Does Basic Screen (₦79,900) overlap our own core panel?** We currently assemble Full
  Blood Count, U/E + Creatinine, Liver Function & Protein, a three-part lipid profile,
  HbA1c and Urinalysis as separate lines for ₦190,000. If Basic Screen covers most of that,
  we would rather buy the bundle.

Note we are not assuming bundles are always cheaper — your Comprehensive Metabolic Panel at
₦92,000 costs more than buying Liver Function & Protein (₦50,400) and U/E + Creatinine
(₦33,600) separately at ₦84,000. We want the component lists precisely so we can check each
case rather than guess.

## 4. Two specific line items we cannot make work

**Faecal Immunochemical Test (FIT) — ₦161,600.** This is our only route to colorectal
screening. At this cost the patient price lands near ₦220,000, which is not a sellable
preventive test in this market. The alternative on your list, Faecal Occult Blood at
₦15,400, is the older guaiac method that modern colorectal guidelines have superseded, and
we are not willing to substitute it behind the same label. **Is there any movement on the
FIT line, or a different FIT-method product we have missed?**

**Chlamydia / Gonorrhoea.** Your list prices Chlamydia trachomatis DNA at ₦100,000,
Neisseria gonorrhoeae DNA at ₦100,000, and the combined PCR at ₦200,000. At those levels we
cannot offer routine STI screening at all, and have withdrawn the product. **Is a lower-cost
method available** — a pooled or batched NAAT, or an antigen-based screen with PCR
confirmation only on a positive?

## 5. Turnaround and collection logistics

For each of the panels above, please confirm:

- **Turnaround time**, and whether it differs by collection point or state.
- **Home collection** — is it available, in which states, and at what charge? Our patients
  are in Lagos, Abuja and fourteen other states on your coverage list.
- **Result delivery format** — PDF report, structured data, or API. We upload and review
  results in-platform, so a structured feed would remove manual transcription for both of us.
- **Repeat/recollection policy** when a sample is rejected or insufficient — who bears that
  cost.
