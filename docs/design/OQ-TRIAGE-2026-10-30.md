## 6. Stage three: the default-taken entries (2026-10-30)

The reviewers marked 181 entries as blocking none of the nine master switches and not needing a named person. Under G-04 these close as
"default taken". A closing line was added to 170 of them:
`Decision (default taken, G-04, 2026-10-30): no master switch depends on this, and the recommendation or behaviour recorded above stands.`
The line deliberately does not say what the build did. It records that no master depends on the entry, so whatever is described above
stays as it is.

- **9 already had a decision line**, so there was nothing to add.
- **2 were held back by a wording screen** because the entry itself says something must happen before a release or go-live: OQ-158
  (a device test of the mobile care flow) and OQ-174 (Paystack test-mode field names). They stay open.
- **What this does not close:** the 19 entries flagged as blocking a master (section 1, with three downgraded after checking), the entries that need
  the CMO, counsel or you (section 2), and the nine resolved-looking entries that still carry an unanswered point (section 5).

Where stage three turns out to be wrong for an entry, delete its closing line; nothing else in the entry was changed.
