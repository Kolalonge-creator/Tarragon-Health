## 2. Reconciliation with what exists
- Reused: S29 token pattern (`private.circle_hash`, 72 hours, single use), `private.module_enabled`, `private.go_live_guard_on`, `private.emit_domain_event`, `private.log_audit`, `notifications`, `platform_modules`. Not reused on purpose: the floor of 5 in `institutions/suppression.ts`.
- Names that would collide are avoided: `community_cohorts` (not `cohorts`), and every public function carries a `community_` prefix because S38e already owns `my_cohorts`, `join_cohort(text)` and `leave_cohort(uuid)` (found by a live check, not by reading files).
- `wellness_challenges` (solo) is untouched, except its ending nudge (section 8).

## 3. Data model
`community_config` (versioned rules, PROPOSED, mirrored as `community.rules`), `challenge_templates` (7 seeded, all `proposed`, mirrored as `community.challenge_templates`), `community_cohorts` (type church/mosque/union/estate/workplace, state active/closed/frozen, `is_test`), `cohort_members` (role member/moderator, two consents with timestamps and a text version, `muted`, `barred`), `community_preferences` (global off), `cohort_invites` (hash only), `challenges`, `challenge_participation` (private to the member: one count per day, no column that could hold a clinical value), `challenge_totals` (append only, **no member id**, a band not a count), `community_reports` (enumerated reasons), `group_sessions` (dormant). Every table has RLS and explicit grants; invites, reports and group sessions have no policy and no grant (functions only). New functions are `security definer`, `search_path = ''`, revoked from public and anon.

## 4. Safety rules, in the order a person meets them
1. **Closed by default.** Module off, guard off, no template approved. A real patient is refused; a test account passes and can only be in a test cohort (a real and a test account cannot share a cohort).
2. **Names.** 3 to 60 characters and no word starting with a term from the versioned list (condition, weight, pregnancy, mental health words). Checked in the function and again by a trigger on the table.
3. **Join.** A moderator makes a single-use link (only its SHA-256 is stored, 72 hours). Preview and join give the same `{ok:false}` for unknown, used, expired, closed, wrong test flag, already a member, barred, and community off. Joining needs an explicit yes; it shares nothing about health.
4. **Contribute** needs a second explicit yes (`community_set_totals_consent`). Withdrawing, leaving, being removed or turning community off each erase the person's own effort rows; published aggregates stay.
5. **Effort-only rule.** Heart failure, kidney disease, pregnancy, insulin use or an eating-disorder history (conditions, the pregnancy record, active medicines, a positive eating screen) restrict a person to `log_days` and `lessons_completed`. Decided in `private.community_effort_only`, never returned. The only visible effect is `available:false` ("This challenge is not available for you.") and the same `not_available` answer a non-member gets. Sources and gaps: OQ-363.
6. **Totals** (`private.challenge_aggregate`, run hourly by `private.community_run`): contributors are members who consent, have not turned community off and have at least one effort row; fewer than 10 hides the total; if the biggest contributor holds more than 30 percent (leave-one-out) the total is hidden; published figures are rounded to 10 and progress is floored to 5 percent; the group size is a band; a snapshot is readable only 12 hours after it is taken (so one entry is never a visible jump); snapshots are append only. All PROPOSED values live in `community_config`, never in code.
7. **Board** (`community_board`): same template, overlapping dates, only cohorts whose own total is shown, at least 3 of them, anonymous labels, ranked by percent of goal so size does not decide.
8. **Moderation.** Per-cohort mute, global community off, leave anytime (also with the platform switch off), report-and-remove (four reasons, no free text), moderator invite/remove/close, admin freeze/unfreeze and resolve (with a bar from rejoining), platform switch. A moderator cannot remove a moderator; if the only moderator leaves the cohort closes (no stranger is promoted into moderator powers). No free-text posts exist.
9. **Moderators see no health data**: the roster is first names and roles; they read only their own effort row like everyone else; reports show the reason and the reported member, never the reporter.
10. **No WhatsApp.** The moderator shares the link through the phone's own share menu (`navigator.share`, React Native `Share`) or copies it; no WhatsApp button, link or API is built (founder decision, F-02, spec 17 safety rules). **Decision recorded here.**
11. **No money.** Rewards stay the existing non-monetary badges and points (INV-09); this module adds none.

## 5. Events and notices (INV-07)
`challenge.progress` (kind `goal_reached` or `ended`, payload ids only) and `group_session.scheduled` (registered, nothing emits it yet) go through the S10 outbox. `private.community_run` emits and queues the notice only after the snapshot is published. The notice is the template `community_update` (in-app and push, `non_clinical`, empty payload): "Your group has an update". It never names a cohort, metric, figure or condition, skips muted members and anyone with community off, and is sent once per milestone. Covered by the INV-07 lint (`packages/notifications/src/s69-templates.test.ts` plus the full lint, 298 checks) and by the DB proof. A test cohort's events are test events (INV-13). A failing challenge in the run is written to the audit log and counted in the run result; it does not stop the others.

## 6. Screens
Web `/patient/community` (open and off switch, your groups, start a group), `/patient/community/[cohortId]` (your totals consent, challenges with goal bar, group total, cohort board, member list with report, moderator tools, mute and leave), `/patient/community/join/[token]` (consent and join). The link on the Care page appears only when community is open for the person. Mobile: `CommunitySection` inside "Your people", the same flows. Individual values appear in no view; both apps parse replies with closed schemas that drop any extra field (tested with a leaked `yours: 163`).

## 7. Go-live guard (INV-14)
Guard `community_cohorts_enabled` (switch role admin). Conditions: at least one template signed by the Chief Medical Officer (read from the data), a recorded DPIA (attestation), counsel's confirmation of the NDPA 2023 references (attestation), admin confirmation. Open for a real person only when the module is on **and** the guard is on. Proven end to end: the guard cannot be switched on with nothing signed or attested, then can. **Nothing was signed or attested outside the rolled-back proof; the templates stay proposed.**

## 9. DPIA note (B1; the full DPIA is a person's document and is NOT written by this build)
- **Data**: membership of a church, mosque, union, estate or workplace group (inferable belief or employment); first names of fellow members; per-day effort counts (private to the member); aggregate totals. No clinical value is stored or shown. The eligibility rule reads the health record server-side and returns nothing.
- **Purpose and basis**: encouragement through shared effort; consent, twice, explicit, separate and withdrawable. Joining is not required for any care.
- **Risks and controls**: re-identification from small groups (floor 10, leave-one-out, rounding, delay, bands, append-only snapshots); coercion inside a community (consent screens say nothing is shared, moderators see no effort, no member rank exists, a report path exists); employer or sponsor access (RLS gives them nothing; proof checks); exclusion of people for whom effort tracking can harm (effort-only rule, neutral state); children (adults only, dependants refused); retention (effort rows erased on leave, withdraw, removal, off; kept 30 days after a challenge ends, PROPOSED); data subject rights (leave and withdraw are immediate).
- **Residual risk to record**: differencing across two published snapshots when someone withdraws (OQ-361); first names visible to all members; a moderator knows who they invited.
- **Before the guard can be switched on**: a person signs the DPIA (attest `dpia_recorded`), counsel confirms the NDPA 2023 section references [verify] (attest `counsel_ndpa_confirmed`), the CMO signs the templates, and the consent text version `draft-1` is replaced by counsel-approved wording.
