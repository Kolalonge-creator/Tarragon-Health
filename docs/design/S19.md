# S19 Design: red event paging and escalation

Spec 7.9, safety cases 8 and 9. Depends on S18 (rota, merged on this branch) and S12 (`triage.graded`). Invariants touched: INV-05 (a red event never waits for a pull: it pages on-call at once and escalates), INV-07 (neutral wording in push, email and in-app), INV-10 and INV-12 (an open page ties the paged clinician to that patient), INV-13 (`is_test`), INV-16 (the page records the config version). D-12 and OQ-113: push, in-console alarm and email only, no SMS paging. Research: `docs/research/S19.md`.

## As built (2026-10-06): where the build differs from the plan below
The plan below is kept for its reasoning. Where they disagree this section wins.
1. **Chart access follows acknowledgement, not the page.** Being paged ties a clinician to nothing; acknowledging an open page does (INV-12), and `my_active_pages()` returns the patient reference only to the clinician who acknowledged. Found in review: the first draft opened the chart at the moment of the page, which contradicted the on-call screen's own wording and gave a clinician who ignored a page 24 hours of access.
2. **An unacknowledged page is never closed quietly.** Only acknowledged pages age out (`page_access_hours`). An unacknowledged page keeps ringing, and the clinical lead and ops are re-alerted every `lead_repeat_minutes` (5, PROPOSED) until someone acknowledges. Also found in review.
3. **Acknowledging cancels the stale class 1 task** (unless someone already claimed it). A failure to cancel is audited and never undoes the acknowledgement.
4. **The paging config is validated by the database** (two increasing positive minutes, a repeat interval, an access window), because one bad edit would make every comparison null and silently stop the ladder.
5. **The sweep runs every 15 seconds** (pg_cron 1.6, as `process-events` already does), so a 5 minute step is not a minute late.
6. **No one to alert is itself an incident**: if no active chief medical officer or admin matches, `page_notify_leadership` raises `page_no_leadership` instead of succeeding quietly.
7. **Rota rows and pages match on `is_test`**: a test event pages the test rota and alerts test leadership only.
8. **Ops are told but cannot silence a page**: acknowledge is for the clinicians the family was sent to, or the CMO.
9. **Screens** (apps/web): `/clinician/on-call` (acknowledge, open the chart, close with a note), an alarm banner on every clinician page (a repeating tone switched on once, a visible warning when the check itself fails), an open-pages section on the reviewer rota page, and a `/rota` doorway so reviewer notices link correctly for both an admin and the CMO.
10. **Not built here:** Care Circle `red_alerts` notices and the clinical lead's incident review task (S29, S20), SMS (D-12), the real two-connection test of an acknowledgement racing the sweep (the sweep holds a row lock with skip locked and acknowledgement takes the same lock, but the proof is single-session), mobile push registration changes.
11. **Second-pass additions (2026-10-06):** the lead alert repeats at most 12 times (push and in-app only; email goes once) and then stops with a sev1 incident, so a sleeping lead is not looped for ever; a page acknowledged but not closed after 60 minutes tells the lead and ops once (`on_call_unfinished`); the paging config CHECK now treats a missing key as invalid (a null comparison would have passed it; found by the proof); a "make sure alerts reach you" checklist on the on-call page for power, data and battery-saving realities (Tecno and Infinix guidance is vendor-sourced and not tested on a handset). A concurrency proof `s19_page_ack_sweep_race.sh` (12 rounds of an acknowledgement racing the sweep, plus a lock-free control) is written and its SQL checked, and runs for the first time in CI.
12. **Live status:** the migration is NOT applied. It ran inside a rolled-back transaction against production, after the S18 migration, with its proof (86 checks, two sabotage steps) and the S16 (80), S17 (187) and S18 (139) proofs, and nothing was left behind.


## What already exists (extended, not rebuilt)
- S12 emits an urgent `triage.graded` for a red grade; `triage_events` carries `shadow` (graded by an unapproved rule set, OQ-88) and a table check that a red row has a `page_on_call` action. Nothing consumes it for paging yet.
- S16 has the task type `red_event_unacknowledged` (class 1, no lead window, on-call competency) and `private.create_clinical_task`; S16 deliberately creates no task from a `page_on_call` action.
- S18 has `on_call_rota` (primary and backup), `rota_gaps`, eligibility, and the incident pattern. The older clinician alert ladder (`clinician_alerts`, `escalation_slas`) stays for vitals and results; it is a separate path.
- Notifications: `notifications` rows with channel, `priority` (`critical` is never gated by quiet hours), template, payload; the S13 pipeline and INV-07 lint.
- Registry: `paging.escalation_minutes` = [5, 10] (PROPOSED, CMO).

## Data model (one migration)
- `paging_config` (versioned, one active; mirrored as `paging.rules`, with a test that `escalation_minutes` equals the existing registry value): `escalation_minutes [5, 10]`, `page_access_hours 24` (an open page ties the clinician to the chart for at most this long).
- `pages`: `patient_id`, `triage_event_id`, `parent_page_id`, `role` (`primary`, `backup`, `escalation`), `to_clinician_id` (null only for an escalation row, whose alerts go to the clinical lead and ops), `escalation_level` (0 primary, 1 backup, 2 clinical lead and ops), `sent_at`, `acknowledged_at/by`, `closed_at`, `close_note`, timer bookkeeping on the root (`backup_paged_at`, `lead_alerted_at`), `no_cover`, `config_version`, `is_test`. One root per triage event (unique), one child per role. Direct writes refused (guard flag); RLS: a clinician reads pages sent to or acknowledged by them, the CMO and admin read all, patients nothing.
- Events (ids only): `page.sent`, `page.acknowledged`, `page.escalated`.

## Behaviour
- `public.create_red_page(triage_event)` (service role, idempotent per event): refuses a shadow event and anything not red with a `page_on_call` action (two layers with the handler). Finds cover with `private.on_call_now`: the rota row covering now, the primary if eligible, else the backup if eligible. Inserts the root page, sends push, in-app and email together (critical priority, neutral text), emits `page.sent`.
  - **No cover** (no rota row, or nobody on it is eligible): safety case 9. The root is an escalation row at level 2 straight away: the clinical lead and ops are alerted, a class 1 task is created so any on-call clinician can take it, a sev1 incident opens. Nobody is waiting on a timer.
- `private.sweep_pages()` (cron every minute, each root in its own subtransaction, idempotent through the bookkeeping columns): at the first escalation time (5 minutes) with no acknowledgement the backup is paged (a child row) and the class 1 task is created; at the second (10 minutes) the clinical lead and ops are alerted by push, in-app and email and a sev1 incident opens. If there is no distinct eligible backup at 5 minutes the lead alert is sent then, not at 10. Open pages older than `page_access_hours` are closed so chart access ends.
- `public.acknowledge_page(page)`: only a clinician the family was sent to, or the CMO; ops cannot silence a page. Stops the ladder, idempotent. `public.close_page(page, note)`: the acknowledging clinician or the CMO, a written note of at least 10 characters, ends chart access.
- `private.clinician_has_patient_access` gains one clause: an open page sent to or acknowledged by me (INV-12). Nothing else widens.
- Reads: `my_active_pages()` (the clinician's own open pages with elapsed time; the CMO also sees open level 2 rows), `paging_overview()` (CMO and admin: open pages with the timer state, and the last 7 days).

## Safety rules
1. A shadow triage event never pages (OQ-88).
2. A page, notification or event carries no condition, reading, name or result (INV-07); the in-console page shows the patient only through the audited chart read the open page now allows.
3. Escalation cannot be silenced by ops, only by a paged clinician or the CMO acknowledging.
4. An uncovered red event is never dropped and never waits: level 2, a task and an incident at once.
5. A failed step in the sweep is audited and opens an incident and never stops the other pages.
6. Test events page test clinicians only in spirit: `is_test` is carried on the page and its notifications and excluded from metrics.

## Screens
`/clinician/on-call`: open pages with an Acknowledge button, a written-note close form, and an in-console alarm (a banner and a repeating tone the clinician switches on once, because browsers block autoplay) shown on every clinician page while a page waits. The reviewer pages (`/admin/rota`, `/clinician/team-rota`) gain an open-pages section.

## Not built (belongs elsewhere)
Care Circle `red_alerts` notices and the clinical lead's incident review task (S29, S20), the 5 minute reminder re-page to the same person, mobile push registration changes, SMS.
