## 2. Risk stratification (22.3)
- **Deterministic points, no model.** Two families kept apart: *deterioration* (7-day average well above or above the person's own target from at least 3 readings; rising against the week before; a red or amber event in 30 days; last snapshot not under target; low adherence) and *dropout* (no reading for 5 or 10 days; far fewer readings than the fortnight before; no reading ever since joining; low adherence). Level is high at 60, medium at 30, from the larger family. Every weight is PROPOSED in `risk_config` (CMO), mirrored as `risk.stratification`.
- **Never spend, orders, visits or payments** (Obermeyer 2019: cost as a proxy for need under-selects people who spend less). The proof scans the scoring function for those tables.
- **Explained**: every score stores its reasons (keys and points, no free text). Staff see plain English for each key.
- **Only for the tied clinician (INV-12)**: `clinician_risk_worklist` returns only patients the caller is tied to, never a test account, ordered by effective tier then size of score; each patient shown is written to `audit_log` (INV-10). A clinician can override the tier with a written reason and a bounded expiry (30 days max); the computed score is never changed and the override is recorded. A refused override is returned as a status, not raised, so its audit row is kept.
- **Never shown to the patient, never a gate.** `risk_scores` and `risk_overrides` have no policy and no grant; nothing else reads them. The screen says: not a diagnosis, never a reason to withhold care, low never means no contact.
- **Fairness**: `risk_distribution_report` (admin or CMO) shows the tier mix by sex, age band and state, with small groups withheld and, when exactly one is, the next smallest too.
- **Daily job** (`risk-scores`, 02:20 UTC): current members only (an ended Membership stops the score, so nobody who left is chased as "slipping away"), one score per person per day, append-only, oldest score first so nobody is starved; a failure opens one incident.

## 3. Monthly report (22.5)
- One stored, write-once report per person and calendar month (Lagos), generated from the 3rd of the next month (grace for late-syncing readings). For people who have joined or logged a reading in that month; six months are back-filled. A report is only written if every input can be read: if adherence cannot, nothing is written, one incident is opened and the job retries tomorrow, rather than storing "nothing to show" for good.
- Contents: readings and days logged; the average and where it sits against the person's own target (or the starting 140/90, said to be a suggestion); a week-by-week split; a direction word against last month; adherence as a separate sentence. Under the minimum readings it says "not enough readings" and shows no average, week average or direction. No comparison with anyone, no ranking, no risk, no care-team activity counts (that would reveal that a task exists; OQ-251).
- Read by the patient only (RLS own rows; `my_monthly_reports`). Strings come from `packages/i18n` (en, pcm; Pidgin for native review).

## 4. Screens
`/patient/progress` ("Your month"), `/clinician/risk-worklist` (with the override form), `/admin/risk-fairness`; nav entries and search entries for the two staff pages.

## 5. Safety rules
No language model anywhere; nothing here writes to the patient record or changes treatment; notifications are not sent (a "your month is ready" notice would need its own INV-07 reviewed template, a follow-up); test accounts never counted in the fairness report; versions recorded (INV-16).

## 7. S38d: sharing, notice, mobile, export (2026-10-07, after S25, S26 and S29 merged)
- **Care Circle** (closes OQ-252): `private.circle_view_blocks` is S29's function renamed to `circle_view_blocks_core` and wrapped, so S29's body is not copied. The wrapper adds a `monthly` block built from the patient's own stored reports (latest three), parts present only with the matching tick, no target numbers, week split or counts. The supporter's page, the patient's "see what they see" preview and the mobile views all read the one function. The proof fails if a later migration redefines the wrapper without the block.
- **Notice**: `monthly_report_ready`, in-app and push, routine priority (quiet hours and the daily cap apply), empty payload, only for the month that just closed. Fixed neutral copy in the sender, the in-app list and the lint test (INV-07). The deployed `send-pending-notifications` function must be redeployed from main-dev for the push and email to render; until then the in-app notice works.
- **Mobile**: a "Your month" section (own reports only) and the monthly block in the Care Circle blocks, both through the shared parser in `packages/i18n` (also used by the web, so the two cannot describe a month differently).
- **Export** (22.9, company level): `/admin/outcomes/export` downloads the existing aggregate report as CSV. The access is logged before the file is built (fail closed). Withheld figures are written as "withheld"; spreadsheet formulas are neutralised.
- **Not built, with reasons**: sponsor dashboards (OQ-254: no sponsor, cohort or sponsor consent exists) and the accuracy dashboard (OQ-255: no clinician-agreement field to measure against).

## 8. S38e: sponsor cohorts and triage agreement (2026-10-07)
- **Sponsors**: a sponsor is an existing organisation of type hmo, corporate or ngo. `sponsor_cohorts` are codes a sponsor hands out; `profile_cohorts` is who joined; sharing is a separate per-programme tick recorded in `patient_consents` (type `sponsor_reporting`), withdrawn on untick or leaving (and the global consent is withdrawn only when no other programme still uses it). The consent text is a DRAFT (not current) until counsel approves it, so nothing is shared until then. Wrong, expired, closed and full codes all get the same answer; ten bad tries stop guessing for an hour.
- **Sponsor report**: aggregate only; counts only members who agreed; test accounts out; the smaller of the platform floor and the sponsor's own `min_cohort_size` is used; a category of 1 to floor-1 withholds the whole figure; "how many agreed" is withheld when the people who did not agree would be a small group. Blood pressure control, adherence and engagement are separate. Admin or CMO only; each view and each CSV is audited.
- **Triage agreement**: `triage_reviews` (append-only) records right / should have been higher / should have been lower for a task the caller completed, from a grade of an approved rule set or a draft one (kept apart). Impossible pairs are refused. The capture is the platform switch `triage_agreement_capture`, off. `triage_accuracy_report` shows coverage first, agreement overall, by grade and by sex, age and state with small and complementary suppression.
- **Screens**: `/patient/programmes`, `/admin/sponsors` (+ per-programme figures and CSV), `/clinician/triage-review`, `/admin/triage-accuracy`. All verified in a browser against a local database with a patient, admin, clinician and supporter.
- **Found while building**: two consent changes inside one transaction share a `created_at`, so "latest wins" could pick the wrong row; the consent rows are stamped with the real clock. Fix: the S02 `patient_consent_state` view has the same tie-break and is left alone.
