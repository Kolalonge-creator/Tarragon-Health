## Rules held
- **Hide-stale** changes S36g's "never hides" for patient search only (OQ-S65-1). Nothing deleted or suspended.
- **Q18**: no emergency number anywhere. Pack, card and modal show facilities only; a table check, a TS scan and a test fail on 112, 767, 199, 911 or a phone-like run. All 37 states are "none listed" until a person verifies a hospital. No facility is fabricated.
- **Q17**: first line is always "Go to the nearest hospital now."; aspirin 162 to 324 mg only for chest pain, alert and not allergic; never for stroke; hypoglycaemia 15 g and 15 minutes, nothing by mouth when unconscious; seizure over 5 minutes; pregnancy 160/110. The pack is DRAFT: the app shows step cards only once `signed` is set by the CMO.
- **INV-06**: pulse, SpO2 and temperature are classified on the phone from the same versioned thresholds; a parity test reads the SQL classifiers and compares every value.
- **INV-07**: reminder and tap-alert texts name no facility, time, location, name or condition; lint tests pass. The tap alert's location is read after sign-in, audited, deleted after 24 hours (OQ-S65-3).
- **Q20**: patient-initiated only, consent explicit and revocable, no location on any red page, never replaces the in-app route (the alert is a row first; a failed recipient raises an incident), guard OFF.
- **Q22/Q23**: no rating of clinical judgement; dietitian, pharmacist and specialist prices are per item and shown only where known.
- **15.16**: `directionsHref` and `telHref` only; no map SDK, no tracking, location never stored.
- **Q19**: licence line renders nothing unless a checked licence exists.
