# Design principles audit, 2026-10-22

Founder principles audited: (1) Home shows one next action. (2) Every number comes with a word, a colour and an icon. (3) A doctor trust cue sits on every clinical item. (4) The naira price shows before commitment. (5) Logging works offline. (6) Shared phones, no health details on the lock screen. (7) Screens stay light for low-end Android. (8) Emergency screens are short and direct, everything else is warm.

Method: code reading of `apps/web` patient surfaces and `apps/mobile` (greps and file reads, cited below). **I did not run the web app or take screenshots**: every patient surface needs a signed-in session against Supabase, which this isolated worktree does not have. Nothing below claims to have been seen on screen. The iOS items from the coordinator's second brief were checked by grep only; nothing was run on a simulator or device.

Competitor evidence (browser and App Store passes, supplied by the founder's coordinator) is used only to shape the fixes: Hello Heart and Oura show a number with a colour zone and one plain word; Teladoc, Ro and One Medical show the price up front; Omada shows tappable suggested questions; Apple Health (3.0 stars) shows density hurts.
