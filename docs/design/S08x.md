## 2. As-needed limits (decision C7 part 1)

**Rule** (`packages/medicines/prn.ts`, SQL twin `private.prn_exceedances`): counting only other taken as-needed doses at an earlier-or-equal time, a dose is over when one lies within the **shortest gap**, or it is more than the **daily most** for its Lagos day. Order independent, so a dose logged offline and synced late is judged exactly like one logged in order. Both implementations run the same ten cases (`prn.fixtures.ts`; the database proof repeats them).

**Warn, never block.** A dose is always recorded. The phone shows "This is sooner than your care plan says. Check with your care team before taking another" (verbatim, `medicines.prn.over_limit_notice`). Nothing suggests a double dose after a miss (a test scans the strings). The server writes a flag; it never edits or rejects a log, and the trigger swallows its own failure with a warning so a flag problem can never lose a dose (proved with a constraint that rejects the flag).

**Data.**
- `prn_limit_rules`: governed, versioned, CMO-signed (`sign_prn_limit_rules`, active CMO only). v1 ships **unsigned and inactive**. It holds the bounds a clinician may enter (gap 15 to 1440 minutes, daily most 1 to 24) and the repeat rule (3 flagged doses in 7 days). PROPOSED values live in `medicines.prn_limits` (packages/shared proposed-config) and a mirror test fails on drift. Listed in `GOVERNED_CONFIG_TABLES`, the sign-off queue, the hub's `CONFIG_PANELS`, the supersede guard, the CMO and admin navigation.
- `medication_prn_limits`: one optional row per medicine, **no defaults**. Written only by `set_medication_prn_limits` (prescribing authority, tied to the patient, signed rule present, active as-needed medicine, a reason, bounds checked, audited). The patient reads, never writes.
- `medication_prn_flags`: written only by the trigger. Care-team signal: when one medicine collects 3 flagged doses in 7 days, one `medication_prn_limit_repeated` event per medicine per week goes into `clinical_rule_events` (no new alarm channel, no medicine name in the payload).
- `public.medication_prn_overview`: limits and 7-day flag counts; self, acting guardian, caregiver with the medicines grant, tied staff with a reason (audited). A failed or refused read shows "not available", never "no limits".
- Go-live guard `prn_limits`, **born off**. While off, no flag is written (test accounts pass, so it can be rehearsed) and the phone shows no notice when the switch is explicitly closed. If the switch cannot be read and nothing is cached, the phone shows the notice (a missing warning is the worse mistake).

**Order of dependencies.** Signing the rule is necessary but does not turn the check on. Sequence for the CMO: pharmacist reviews bounds, CMO signs `prn_limit_rules` v1 in the hub, a clinician sets limits per medicine, the CMO flips the `prn_limits` guard.

## 3. The patient's adherence report (decision C6 display half)

- `/patient/medicine-report` (on screen) and `/api/patient/medicine-report/pdf` (react-pdf, the Health Passport setup). Free, not paywalled. The phone opens the page in the browser; the PDF, once saved, is an ordinary file that reads offline.
- Weekly rows, newest first, 8 completed weeks (`medicines.adherence_report`, PROPOSED): "Doses marked taken" as a percentage only when at least 3 doses were due; **skipped shown separately** from no record and from "could not get it" and counted as not taken; late counts as taken. Never a grade, a band, a target or a colour; as-needed medicines left out. All strings from `@tarragon/i18n` (`medicines.report.*`), English only, no em dashes.
- A failed read gives a 503 and no document, never a report of zeros; dose rows are paged (the API caps one response at 1000 rows, and a silent cut would turn answered doses into "no record").
- **Drift test.** The same week (daily 08:00 medicine, 8 to 14 October 2026; taken 8 to 11, skipped 12, late 13, could not get it 14 = 7 due, 5 marked taken, 1 late, 1 skipped, 0 no record, 1 could not get, 71 percent) is asserted in `adherence-report-data.test.ts` (TypeScript) and in section 9b of `packages/db/tests/s08x_prn_limits.sql` (`private.weekly_adherence`).

## 4. Android reminder reliability (closes OQ-73 in code; not verified on a device)

- `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` (never `USE_EXACT_ALARM`) and `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` declared in `app.json`. A small local Expo module (`apps/mobile/modules/exact-alarms`, Kotlin) answers `canScheduleExactAlarms` and opens the system screen. Through `requireOptionalNativeModule` an older binary, iOS and tests answer "cannot tell": no warning and no button rather than a wrong one.
- In context on the Medications screen: plain explanation, "Allow exact timing", and when off a visible "Reminders may be a few minutes late on this phone. Your list on this screen is always right." The health check now feeds the real answer into the existing `exact_alarms_off` issue.
- "Keep reminders working": steps chosen by maker (`keepRemindersGroup` in the package: Tecno/Infinix/itel, Samsung, other aggressive makers, generic), "Open battery settings" (the battery-restriction list, not the restricted ignore-optimisation request) and "Open app settings"; every open falls back and returns false instead of throwing.
- Boot: `expo-notifications` re-registers its scheduled notifications after a reboot; the app also rebuilds its plan on launch and foreground (S08). The permission change and the module need a new native build.
- **`runtimeVersion` is `0.1.0-native6`, set by S06x (#1080) before this merged.** S08x adds a manifest permission and a native module to that same build and does not bump again; the one `eas build` for native6 must include both. The OTA publisher will not push this change; existing phones need that build.
- Unverified (no device, no emulator): the exact-alarm permission flow on Android 12 to 15, the boot reschedule, delivery timing in Doze, and every maker setting. The Kotlin module has not been compiled here. This shares the device-lab run with S06x-c.

## 5a. Known limits (found in review, recorded)

- **Nothing consumes the new signal yet.** `medication_prn_limit_repeated` lands in `clinical_rule_events`, but no clinical rule, task or notification is attached to it (S12/S16 own that). Until then the care team sees the count on the patient's chart panel ("N doses over the limit in 7 days"). The CMO should know this before signing.
- **`public.amend_medication` does not copy `schedule_spec`** (verified on the live definition). Amending an as-needed prescription turns the new row into a plain list, so it is no longer as-needed. A trigger (`carry_prn_limits_on_amend`) carries limits to an amended row while it is still as-needed, which only matters once amend keeps the schedule; the missing copy is a separate pre-existing gap, handed off as its own task.
- The report follows the medicines the patient takes **now** (same as the live weekly figure): a medicine since stopped or changed is absent from earlier weeks. Disclosed in the footnote.
- A flag write that fails is kept as an audit row (`medication_prn_limits.flag_write_failed`) and `private.reevaluate_prn_flags(medicine)` repairs it; there is no scheduled sweep.
- Granting exact alarms re-makes the held reminders (`rescheduleAllDoseReminders`), because reminders already scheduled keep the inexact mode they were created in.
