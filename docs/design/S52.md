## Behaviour
- **7.8 Crisis and emergency.** An emergency button is on every assistant screen (web chat, mobile coach, including the not-open and no-access states). It shows bundled guidance at once with no network (INV-06), then adds the nearest hospitals (active hospital `facilities` in the patient's state, own city first, verified first; never ranked by price or partnership) and the patient's own saved emergency contact. The same addendum is appended to every automatic emergency reply. Self-harm wording gets its own, calmer copy (PROPOSED, awaiting CMO sign-off, OQ-293), the same escalation as every emergency, and an on-call page (INV-05): one class 1 task under `crisis:<patient>`, the neutral `on_call_page` notice to the clinician on call or `on_call_escalation` to the clinical lead and ops with an incident when nobody is on call, once per conversation per six hours, failures audited and never blocking the copy. No phone number is in code or invented: Nigeria has no usable crisis helpline, so numbers are configuration (`assistant.emergency.phoneNumbers`, empty).
- **7.9 Transparency.** Sources shown (S51), a limits panel on both platforms from one bundled text, report-an-answer on mobile and web (the mobile route is not behind the guard). Every report feeds the monthly sample.
- **7.11 Clarifying questions.** A graph node after every safety screen asks at most two short questions for a vague one-liner, never twice in a row and never to an answer to its own question; deterministic and evaluated with fixed labelled cases (`clarify.eval.test.ts`).
- **7.12 Memory.** Off by default twice: the AI-020 kill switch (disabled, and the governance trigger refuses enabling it until criteria are met) and the patient's own consent record, written first. Goals and preferences only: a trigger refuses condition, reading, result and medicine words (the INV-07 list), length and a cap. Patient-only RLS (staff, caregivers and the service role have no policy). The AI reads it only through `assistant_memory_for_prompt()`. View, edit, delete, delete all, export (audited without the text), revoke.
- **7.10 Silence.** Programme members (active care pack or enrolled chronic programme) the assistant is open to, silent for `silence_days`, write one `assistant.silence_detected` event (a signal, never a treatment change, no model); after `reengage_after_days` one generic keyed note, at most one per cooldown. Thresholds are PROPOSED config mirrored by `assistant_config`.
- **7.13 Monthly review.** A monthly cron draws every reported conversation plus a random sample (ids only). The Chief Medical Officer opens `/clinician/assistant-review` (apps/web, not the console, OQ-543), opens a conversation through `assistant_review_read` (a reason, an audit row, INV-10) and records a verdict through `assistant_review_record` (needs a prior read, unsafe needs a note and opens an incident, final). The conversation read policy is untouched.

## Safety rules kept
Red-flag, sensitive-result and dose screens run before the model; no AI output enters the record; every notification is a keyed generic template (INV-07); nothing here depends on a send succeeding; every new table has RLS and explicit grants; the sampler, silence job and page are service-role only.

## Go-live
`assistant_enabled` stays off and needs reviewed knowledge rows, a reviewed red-flag evaluation run and the CMO. The CMO must also sign the emergency and self-harm wording, set the silence and review thresholds, and decide whether to enable AI-020 (and complete its governance criteria).
