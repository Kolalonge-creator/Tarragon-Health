# S39c: audited record access (NHS model)

Founder direction 2026-10-07 (OQ-278): a doctor can search for any patient they want to manage, and every access is audited, so we know who looked at which record. No reason to type. The patient is not told.

## 1. What exists
S39b ties 128 tables to a care relationship, break-glass or a support view. That alone shuts a doctor out of a patient who is not on their list. S05 and S22e audit reads through functions (about 10 tables). `audit_log` and `private.log_audit` hold generic events.

## 2. Design
- `staff_record_opens` is both the log and the grant: one row per opening (staff, patient, organisation, opened_at, expires_at, basis tied or open, after_hours). No foreign keys, so the log outlives an account. Append-only: no insert, update or delete for any role, a trigger blocks update, delete and truncate even for the owner.
- `public.open_patient_record(patient)` is the only way in. It checks the caller is an active clinician in the patient's organisation, writes the row, writes `audit_log` (`record_open`) and returns the window. A second call inside a live window is the same visit and writes nothing. It takes no reason.
- `private.staff_may_read` now also accepts `private.staff_has_open`: a live opening for this clinician and patient, in the same organisation, never for `reproductive_health`. So an untied clinician cannot read a tied table without a logged opening, enforced by RLS on all 128 tables.
- Who cannot: a care coordinator (logistics only), an admin (support view-as is the admin path), a patient, a clinician of another organisation, anon.
- Review: `access_review_report(days)` per clinician (openings, untied openings, after-hours, distinct patients), and direct read of the log, for the CMO and admins only. One security incident per clinician when untied openings in an hour reach the limit; it never turns an opening into an error.
- Config (PROPOSED, `security.rules` v2, registry mirror and drift test): window 8 hours, alert at 20 untied openings an hour, after hours 22:00 to 06:00 Lagos, and the retention periods. Retention is config only; `real_data_auto_delete` is false.

## 3. Safety rules kept
INV-10 (read audited), INV-12 (tied or an audited way in), reproductive health never through an open or break-glass path, care coordinators never read clinical tables, `private.is_org_staff` untouched, kill switch `tied_staff_reads` still restores the old read.

## 4. Limits (open questions)
OQ-281 patient right to the access log (counsel). OQ-282 the log is per opening, not per table read, because a GET runs read-only. OQ-283 retention periods are proposals. Staff writes remain organisation-wide (OQ-279). A staff patient search page that opens through the same call is the next UI piece; today the chart page opens on load.

## 5. Proof
`packages/db/tests/s39c_audited_record_access.sql`, 33 checks and two sabotages (a window check that always passes; an opening with its log insert removed). Both flip.
