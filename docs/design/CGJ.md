# CGJ design note: a caregiver joins a consultation call, invited by the patient, one call at a time

Founder decision (settled): the patient invites per call. Only the patient (or a proxy who already acts for them) can invite. Only a Care Circle member who holds the new `attend_consultation` permission can be invited. The clinician sees who is present and can remove them, for good, for that call. Audio only is allowed. Nothing is recorded. Scribe consent stays the patient's own answer. A standing "may join any call" permission is NOT built. Invariants in play: INV-07, INV-10, INV-11, INV-12, INV-16. Builds on S21 (encounters, rooms, events, Meeting SDK, participant keys, OQ-160) and S29 (Care Circle).

## 1. Reused, extended, new
- Reused as is: `encounters`, `encounter_events` (append-only), `private.log_encounter_event`, `private.encounter_is_on`, `service_get_encounter_room`, `ensureRoom`, the `VideoProvider` join token, `useInAppCall` and `ConsultationRoom` (the SAME call code runs for the caregiver), the participant-key idea (OQ-160), `care_circle_members`, `private.circle_paused`, the circle's neutral notice helper `private.circle_notify`.
- Extended: the Care Circle permission list gains `attend_consultation` (`private.circle_permissions_valid`, web and mobile parsers, copy). `encounter_events` gains the actor role `caregiver` and seven kinds (below). `VideoRole` gains `caregiver` (the display label, never a name). `participantKey` gains a caregiver key bound to one pass.
- New: table `encounter_caregiver_passes` (one row per invite, per call), table `consultation_caregiver_config` (versioned, PROPOSED), patient/proxy invite and cancel functions, caregiver accept/decline and view functions, a clinician remove function, server-only functions for the credential and presence, a caregiver room page, and the invite panel in the patient room.

## 2. Data model (migration `cgj_caregiver_in_consultation`)
- `encounter_caregiver_passes`: `encounter_id`, `patient_id`, `caregiver_id`, `circle_member_id`, `relationship` (the circle's label, copied so the clinician sees "Daughter" and nothing else), `state` (`invited`, `accepted`, `declined`, `cancelled`, `removed`), `expires_at`, `invited_by`, `accepted_at`, `removed_at`, `removed_by`, `config_version` (INV-16), `is_test`. One live invite per person per call (partial unique index), and a removed person cannot be re-invited to that call (checked in the invite function). RLS on; only the patient reads their own rows, and only a column list that leaves out who invited and who removed; the caregiver, a proxy and the clinician read nothing from the table and go through `consultation_caregivers()` / `my_consultation_invites()`; no direct write; explicit column `grant select` to `authenticated`.
- `consultation_caregiver_config`: `inviteExpiryMinutes` 240, `maxCaregiversPerCall` 1, `minAgeYears` 18, `requireDateOfBirth` false, mirrored in `packages/shared` as `consultations.caregiver_join` (a drift test compares the two; a repo scan keeps the numbers out of code).
- Events (ids only: `pass_id`, `mode`, `reason_code`; never a name or reading): `caregiver_invited`, `caregiver_invite_cancelled`, `caregiver_accepted`, `caregiver_declined`, `caregiver_joined`, `caregiver_left`, `caregiver_removed`. Written only by definer functions; the general recorders still refuse them. They use their own kinds so the patient/clinician `joined` logic (start, no-show) cannot be moved by a caregiver.

## 3. Who can do what
- Invite (`invite_caregiver_to_consultation(encounter, member)`): the patient, or `private.can_act_for(patient, 'book_appointments')` (the proxy rule `encounter_visible` already uses). The member must belong to THIS patient, be active, unexpired, not paused, hold `attend_consultation`, be an adult (config `minAgeYears`; a known age under it is refused; an unknown age is allowed because a supporter-only account is never asked for a date of birth, unless config `requireDateOfBirth` is flipped on; a dependant's account is always refused, OQ-CGJ-4) and not be the clinician. The encounter must be live and still inside its join window. Capped by `maxCaregiversPerCall` (counted under a lock on the consultation row, and only invites still in force count). An invite must be answered within `inviteExpiryMinutes` (and before the join window closes); once accepted it lasts for the call, up to the close of the window, and a call in progress keeps its guest past it. Expiry is the earlier of the configured minutes and the end of the join window. All refusals are one generic error to a stranger.
- Accept/decline: only the invited caregiver, only while the pass is live.
- Remove: only the encounter's assigned clinician. Final for the call: state `removed`, event written, credential refused from then on.
- Scribe: untouched. `open_scribe_prompt`/`record_scribe_consent` check `auth.uid() = patient`, so a caregiver, a proxy of the caregiver and the clinician cannot answer. The caregiver's page says an AI note-taker may be on if the patient allowed it (OQ-CGJ-3).
- Chart: no new read right. The caregiver gets NO policy on `encounters`, `encounter_events`, notes, results or summaries; they read only what two definer functions return (when, status, their own pass, "join" state) and nothing from the circle view changes. `reproductive_health` is not touched. `private.can_read_clinical` is not called (no overload risk).

## 4. The join credential (identity)
A caregiver never receives the patient's or clinician's link. `service_get_caregiver_pass(encounter, user)` (service role only; the signed-in id is checked in the server action exactly as `lookup` does for the two existing roles) answers a pass only when: the user is the invited caregiver, state `accepted`, not expired, the encounter is live and joinable, the circle member is still active/unexpired/not paused and still holds the permission. `prepareSdkJoin` then mints a Meeting SDK signature (role 0, never a host key), the room passcode, and a caregiver key `g` + 34 hex of HMAC(secret, `encounter:caregiver:pass_id`), so presence can name the pass and a key from another pass or call verifies as nothing. Display label is `caregiver`. There is NO link fallback and no dial-in for a caregiver: a link or a phone join carries no key, cannot be attributed and cannot be revoked. If the in-app call is unavailable the page says so (OQ-CGJ-1). The waiting room stays on, so the clinician admits the person.

## 5. The clinician
`consultation_caregivers(encounter)` (patient, proxy or assigned clinician) lists passes with the relationship label, state and whether they are in the call now. The clinician room shows "Caregiver present: Daughter" (or "invited, not yet in") and a Remove button. Removal revokes the pass, blocks rejoin and is audited. **Limit, stated plainly:** Zoom's REST API has no remove-participant call we can add to `VideoProvider`, so someone already inside the call is removed by the clinician (host) in Zoom's own participant list; the room tells them to. The platform guarantees no new credential, no rejoin from the app and an audit trail (OQ-CGJ-2).

## 6. Surfaces, copy, notifications
Patient room: "Invite someone from your Care Circle" (members with the permission), status and Cancel. Caregiver: Care Circle supporter page shows "Invited to join a call on <date time>" with Accept / Decline / Join, and a room at `/patient/supporting/consultation/[encounterId]` (CSP widened for that one path like the others). Mobile: the same functions over the signed-in session (invite, list, accept, decline, remove); the join itself opens the web room because the Expo app has no SDK (OQ-158). Notice to the caregiver: in-app + push, template `circle_consultation_invite`, empty payload, fixed text "You have been invited to join a call." (INV-07). Nothing depends on a send. Strings in `packages/i18n`, English only, no em dashes, "your care team".

## 7. Proof
DB proof `cgj_caregiver_in_consultation.sql` (registered in `ci.manifest`, sabotage flips): invite only a permitted member; non-member, revoked, expired member, paused circle, no permission, another patient's circle, ended encounter, other patient's encounter, under-18, the clinician themself, a stranger as inviter all refused; cap; accept only by the invitee; expired invite and removed pass refused for the credential; removal by the clinician only and blocks rejoin and re-invite; the caregiver cannot write or withdraw scribe consent and reads no encounter/event/note row; events append-only and ids only; no `anon` or `authenticated` execute on the server-only functions. Jest: room/credential (mock provider), presence mapping, participant key, UI, config mirror, templates.

Not verified here: nothing ran against a live Zoom account, a browser call or a device (see BUILD-PROGRESS). Open questions: OQ-CGJ-1 to OQ-CGJ-5 in `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
