# OUTCOMES: the honest outcomes dashboard (do Health Points and engagement change outcomes, or do healthier people just take part more?)

Branch `feat/outcomes-dashboard`. One migration (`20261022094717_outcomes_dashboard.sql`), one DB proof (`packages/db/tests/outcomes_dashboard.sql`, registered in `ci.manifest`), nothing applied to production, nothing signed, no guard touched. Progress text for `docs/BUILD-PROGRESS.md` is the "Built" and "Apply" sections below (the parent merges it).

## Who, and what it refuses
For the founder / super admin and the Chief Medical Officer only (`private.is_admin() or private.credential_is_cmo()`, checked inside every public function; the reports table has **no grant to any user role**, so even an admin cannot read it except through the audited `read_outcomes_dashboard`). Not for employers, sponsors, HMOs, insurers, analysts, finance, care coordinators or doctors (all refused 42501, proven). The page and every report say they are internal and that nothing may be published: `publishable` and `internal_only` are CHECK constraints, and no sentence in the report or the page is a claim (a causal-wording test runs on the stored report in the DB and on the strings in Jest).

## Three panels, never one score
1. **Engagement (what we reward)**, one calendar month, by joining cohort: people active, active logging days, lessons, medicine check-ins, screenings, milestones (badges, goals, programmes finished), Health Points issued and earners. Sources: `vitals_readings` (person-logged, not weight or waist), `nutrition_log_entries`, `medication_adherence_checkins`, `health_education_progress`, `screening_results`, `wellness_points_ledger`, `patient_wellness_badges`.
2. **Clinical outcomes (what we care about)**, people with hypertension or diabetes in the programme, cumulative to the data cutoff: blood pressure control at day 90 and 180 (strict, counting people who stopped logging as not controlled, beside the rate among those measured, Wilson intervals, the same people's first week beside it), mean change in the top number, adherence kept apart, HbA1c change and goal (S75 snapshots), time to the first review of an abnormal screening result (first acknowledgement of the alert raised for it), escalations by state. Definitions are not new: they read `outcome_snapshots` (S38: 7-day average against the person's own target, else 140/90, at least 3 readings) and `outcome_measure_snapshots` (S75).
3. **Comparison with caveats**: high / in between / low engagement, and points earners / non-earners, among people measured at both ends. The **pre-period is the same people's day-0 snapshot**. The estimate is a difference in differences with a 95 percent interval: the change in group A minus the change in group B, each change taken per person (control: the 0/1 change in "controlled"; systolic: day 90 minus day 0; HbA1c: the stored change). The interval is the normal approximation from the groups' standard deviations (`private.outcomes_did`; checked in the migration self-test and against an independent recomputation in the proof). The page states, always, that engaged people differ from others (self-selection) and that a difference is an association, not proof.

**Engagement for drawing the groups excludes blood pressure readings** (config `exclude_blood_pressure_from_engagement`), because a reading is how control is measured and "controlled" needs 3 readings: counting it builds the answer into the question. Proven with a sabotage (letting it count moves the whole low group out of "low").

## Safety and privacy rules, and where each is enforced
- **Aggregates only.** No public function returns a row or id; the per-person working sets (`outcomes_dash_due`, `outcomes_dash_people`, `outcomes_dash_programme`) are private with no grant (proven: not executable by an admin). I8/I9 style: no drill-down exists here at all.
- **Small groups** go only through `private.min_cell_floor / suppress_cell / suppress_groups / suppress_difference` (S75a; the S75a static scan style is repeated for these functions). Floor 20 (30 for a cut by two attributes) and, for a comparison group, the greater of that and `min_per_group` (30). One withheld group takes its smallest neighbour with it, then no estimate is computed from a withheld group.
- **Differencing across stored reports** is blocked by the held-back rule: if fewer people than the floor have newly become due since the last report (any of htn/dm at 90/180, people with an abnormal result, people with an escalation), the outcome cutoff stays at the last report's cutoff and the report says `held_back`. Residual: sub-cells (for example controlled among the newly due) are not guarded one by one; see OQ-OUT-08.
- **Excluded everywhere**: test accounts (INV-13, snapshot flag and profile flag), dependants, people with no date of birth, under-18s at joining. **Never read**: mental health, PHQ-9 / GAD-7, cycle and pregnancy tables (static scan in the proof), screening kinds marked `sensitive` in `screen_types` or matching the config patterns (reproductive, sexual health, HIV / hepatitis, mental health) or with no type; points from rules that can touch those areas (`screening_done`, `lab_done`, `review_attended`) are left out of every points figure.
- **Loud, not silent**: the monthly job (pg_cron `45 3 3 * *` UTC, previous Lagos month) catches each organisation's failure, writes `audit_log` `outcomes_dashboard.error` and opens one incident (`outcomes-dashboard-failing`); the page says "could not be read" rather than showing an empty dashboard; every read and export is in `audit_log`.
- **Permanent**: one immutable report per organisation and month (second run returns the same one); the first report with data is the baseline; an empty organisation is recorded but is never a baseline.

## Apply to production checklist (S60 style)
Nothing is applied. Live must be read-only checked first: `max(version)` against `20261022094717`; `list_migrations` for anything recent you do not recognise; that `public.outcome_snapshots`, `public.outcome_measure_snapshots`, `private.min_cell_floor`, `private.suppress_groups`, `private.causal_wording_hits`, `private.open_job_incident`, `screening_results.screen_type_code`, `screen_types.sensitive`, `wellness_points_ledger.rule_code` and `.kind` exist; that the S38 daily snapshot job is running (without it every cohort is empty). Then:
1. `supabase db reset` locally and run `outcomes_dashboard.sql` plus `s75a_min_cell_every_surface.sql` (the DB proof in this branch was only run against a stubbed scratch Postgres, NOT against the real schema; it must pass on a real replay before merge).
2. Apply the migration with the version pinned to the filename's timestamp (never bare `apply_migration`).
3. Merge; the page works against the new functions. (Page first would only show "could not be read".)
4. As a super admin: `run_outcomes_dashboard_now` for the last finished month; check it is the baseline; open both pages; try the CSV.
5. `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts` was spliced by hand (two tables, three RPCs); do not regenerate wholesale (production types carry other branches' schema).
6. No guard exists or is flipped. The CMO should review the PROPOSED thresholds (OQ-OUT-01) before anyone acts on a comparison.
