## Who, and what it refuses
For the founder / super admin and the Chief Medical Officer only (`private.is_admin() or private.credential_is_cmo()`, checked inside every public function; the reports table has **no grant to any user role**, so even an admin cannot read it except through the audited `read_outcomes_dashboard`). Not for employers, sponsors, HMOs, insurers, analysts, finance, care coordinators or doctors (all refused 42501, proven). The page and every report say they are internal and that nothing may be published: `publishable` and `internal_only` are CHECK constraints, and no sentence in the report or the page is a claim (a causal-wording test runs on the stored report in the DB and on the strings in Jest).

## Three panels, never one score
1. **Engagement (what we reward)**, one calendar month, by joining cohort: people active, active logging days, lessons, medicine check-ins, screenings, milestones (badges, goals, programmes finished), Health Points issued and earners. Sources: `vitals_readings` (person-logged, not weight or waist), `nutrition_log_entries`, `medication_adherence_checkins`, `health_education_progress`, `screening_results`, `wellness_points_ledger`, `patient_wellness_badges`.
2. **Clinical outcomes (what we care about)**, people with hypertension or diabetes in the programme, cumulative to the data cutoff: blood pressure control at day 90 and 180 (strict, counting people who stopped logging as not controlled, beside the rate among those measured, Wilson intervals, the same people's first week beside it), mean change in the top number, adherence kept apart, HbA1c change and goal (S75 snapshots), time to the first review of an abnormal screening result (first acknowledgement of the alert raised for it), escalations by state. Definitions are not new: they read `outcome_snapshots` (S38: 7-day average against the person's own target, else 140/90, at least 3 readings) and `outcome_measure_snapshots` (S75).
3. **Comparison with caveats**: high / in between / low engagement, and points earners / non-earners, among people measured at both ends. The **pre-period is the same people's day-0 snapshot**. The estimate is a difference in differences with a 95 percent interval: the change in group A minus the change in group B, each change taken per person (control: the 0/1 change in "controlled"; systolic: day 90 minus day 0; HbA1c: the stored change). The interval is the normal approximation from the groups' standard deviations (`private.outcomes_did`; checked in the migration self-test and against an independent recomputation in the proof). The page states, always, that engaged people differ from others (self-selection) and that a difference is an association, not proof.

**Engagement for drawing the groups excludes blood pressure readings, medicine check-ins, and the points those earn.** A reading is how control is measured and "controlled" needs 3 readings; check-ins overlap the adherence figure. The comparison's points rules (`comparison.points_rules_included`) are meals, lifestyle tasks and goals, challenges and classes, not `vitals_logged` (which fires for blood pressure) nor `adherence_checkin_completed`. Active days use an allow-list of measurement vital types (`engagement.vital_types_allowed`: no mood, sleep, water, weight, waist). Each is proven with a sabotage.

**Survivorship.** Beside each blood pressure estimate there is a worst-case estimate counting everyone due and treating "no usable reading" as not controlled (`control_strict`). The not-measured share is shown for blood pressure groups and for HbA1c groups and the HbA1c block, but is itself withheld when the unmeasured count is a small category (so a worst-case estimate is the way to see it without disclosing a few people). The comparison design still looks at change, not at who started healthier: regression to the mean and the overlap of the activity window (first 90 days) with the outcome window (days 84 to 90) are named on the page.

## Safety and privacy rules, and where each is enforced
- **Aggregates only.** No public function returns a row or id; the per-person working sets (`outcomes_dash_due`, `outcomes_dash_people`, `outcomes_dash_programme`) are private with no grant (proven: not executable by an admin). I8/I9 style: no drill-down exists here at all.
- **Small groups** go only through `private.min_cell_floor / suppress_cell / suppress_groups / suppress_difference` (S75a; the S75a static scan style is repeated for these functions). Floor 20 (30 for a cut by two attributes) and, for a comparison group, the greater of that and `min_per_group` (30). One withheld group takes its smallest neighbour with it, then no estimate is computed from a withheld group.
- **Differencing across stored reports** is blocked two ways. (1) Reports are made in order: a report for a month before an existing one is refused (22023, `outcomes_dash_order_ok`), so a back-filled or out-of-order pair cannot be subtracted. (2) The held-back rule covers EVERY denominator and group size behind a shown figure (`outcomes_dash_counts`: cohort totals and their controlled / uncontrolled / unmeasured splits at day 0 and day 90 and 180, people measured at both ends, adherence and HbA1c counts, every comparison group's due, paired, controlled-before, controlled-after and HbA1c counts, people with an abnormal result and its reviewed count, escalations by status). If any of them has grown since the last stored report by 1 to floor minus 1 people, the whole report is held back and its outcome cutoff stays at the last report's. A figure that shrank cannot come from newly due people and does not hold a report back (otherwise it would be held for ever). The first report has nothing to subtract from and is never held. Held-back reports will be common in a small pilot (OQ-OUT-09, OQ-OUT-11).
- **Excluded everywhere**: test accounts (INV-13, snapshot flag and profile flag), dependants, people with no date of birth, under-18s at joining. **Never read**: mental health, PHQ-9 / GAD-7, cycle and pregnancy tables (static scan in the proof), screening kinds marked `sensitive` in `screen_types` or matching the config patterns or with no type; mood readings (not on the vital-type allow-list); lessons and badges whose code, title or criteria match `protected_topic_patterns`; escalations unless tied to an alert of an allowed kind (abnormal result, abnormal monitoring, deterioration, medication safety; never a symptom escalation) that is not itself from a sensitive screening (an escalation with no alert has no known topic and is left out). Points from rules that can touch those areas (`screening_done`, `lab_done`, `review_attended`, and lessons and courses, whose topic the ledger does not record) are left out of every points figure. Escalation status is its current state for escalations raised up to the cutoff, not its state on the cutoff date.
- **Corrected records.** Every definer function reads vitals through `private.vitals_readings_current` (superseded and corrected readings are not counted), never `public.vitals_readings` directly (the S05b-c rule H2; the proof scans for it, with a sabotage). Lessons are also left out by library category (`engagement.protected_lesson_categories`: mental health, women's health, men's health, cancer screening) as well as by title and code pattern.
- **Loud, not silent**: the monthly job (pg_cron `45 3 3 * *` UTC, previous Lagos month) catches each organisation's failure, writes `audit_log` `outcomes_dashboard.error` and opens one incident (`outcomes-dashboard-failing`); the page says "could not be read" rather than showing an empty dashboard; every read and export is in `audit_log`.
- **Permanent**: one immutable report per organisation and month (second run returns the same one); the first report with data is the baseline; an empty organisation is recorded but is never a baseline.

## Founder view (decision on OQ-OUT-09 and OQ-OUT-11)
A small pilot would be blind under the held-back rule, so the super admin has a separate read, `read_outcomes_founder_unfrozen(reason)`, page `/admin/outcomes/engagement/founder`. It computes the Panel 2 and Panel 3 figures to today, lifting only the held-back freeze: minimum cell, complementary suppression and every protected-data exclusion still apply (proven). Rules: only `private.is_admin()` (the super-admin account; this platform has no separate founder role, and a delegated permission holder, the CMO and every other role are refused 42501); off by default (`outcomes_dashboard_config.founder_unfrozen_view.enabled`, mirrored as `outcomes.founder_unfrozen_view`; turning it on is a new config version); a reason of 10 to 500 characters is required and written to `audit_log` before the figures are built; it writes no report, has no CSV or export route and no CMO twin (tests check the files); a red non-dismissible banner is always shown: "Founder view. Small groups. Not for sharing, not for the CMO or any partner. Individuals could be identifiable from differences between versions." Reading it repeatedly over time lets the founder difference the figures, which is the risk the banner names and the reason the switch is off by default. The risk is stated plainly in the box under the banner: the small-group rule is applied to each view on its own, so two views on different days, or a view beside a stored monthly report (cumulative figures cut at today), can be subtracted to point at individuals; the design is kept and the founder is told not to compare versions. The audit row records the reason, the data cutoff and how many cells were shown and how many were held back, never the figures.

## A failed month cannot be backfilled (by design)
Reports are made in order (`outcomes_dash_order_ok`). If the monthly job fails for an organisation and the next month's report is then made, the failed month can never be made: filling the gap afterwards would give two cumulative reports that can be subtracted to expose a small newly-due group. Ops remedy: the failure opens the incident `outcomes-dashboard-failing` (and an `outcomes_dashboard.error` audit row); fix the cause and re-run `private.run_outcomes_dashboard_monthly()` (or a super admin runs the month on the dashboard) BEFORE the next scheduled run on the 3rd of the next month. After that the gap is permanent. The incident text says so.

## Apply to production checklist (S60 style)
Nothing is applied. Live must be read-only checked first: `max(version)` against `20261022162115`; `list_migrations` for anything recent you do not recognise; that `public.outcome_snapshots`, `public.outcome_measure_snapshots`, `private.min_cell_floor`, `private.suppress_groups`, `private.causal_wording_hits`, `private.open_job_incident`, `screening_results.screen_type_code`, `screen_types.sensitive`, `wellness_points_ledger.rule_code` and `.kind` exist; that the S38 daily snapshot job is running (without it every cohort is empty). Then:
1. `supabase db reset` locally and run `outcomes_dashboard.sql` plus `s75a_min_cell_every_surface.sql` (the DB proof in this branch was only run against a stubbed scratch Postgres, NOT against the real schema; it must pass on a real replay before merge).
2. Apply the migration with the version pinned to the filename's timestamp (never bare `apply_migration`).
3. Merge; the page works against the new functions. (Page first would only show "could not be read".)
4. As a super admin: `run_outcomes_dashboard_now` for the last finished month; check it is the baseline; open both pages; try the CSV.
5. `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts` was spliced by hand (two tables, three RPCs); do not regenerate wholesale (production types carry other branches' schema).
6. No guard exists or is flipped. The CMO should review the PROPOSED thresholds (OQ-OUT-01) before anyone acts on a comparison.
