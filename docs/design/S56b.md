## Reused, not rebuilt
`mental_health_handoffs` (extended, not duplicated), `read_patient_mental_health_audited` (the only way staff read a summary), `request_mental_health_handoff` (the calm tool's "tell my care team"), `private.can_staff_read_mental_health` / `audit_mental_health_read`, `clinician_has_patient_access` (the tie), `private.create_clinical_task` (types `admin_clinical` and `symptom_review`), the S37 guards and attestations, the PCC pattern for a versioned configuration signed by the CMO, `SharedPhoneGate`, the crisis card, the S57 paced-breathing timeline, `mergeMoodBpSleep`.

## Data model
1. `mental_health_handoffs` gains `kind` (`screen` | `doctor_summary` | `talk_request`), `seen_by`, `seen_at`. `mark_mental_health_handoff_seen` (tied clinician only, audited, a refusal returns NULL so its audit row survives).
2. `share_wellbeing_summary_with_care_team` and `preview_wellbeing_summary`: one builder (`private.build_wellbeing_summary`) that reads only the patient's own scores, check-ins and sleep diary. Raw totals, the band the signed instrument already stored, the raw change, weekly means, sleep highlights, the patient's own note. It cannot reach the journal, check-in notes or tags, or any questionnaire item answer (proved, with a sabotage that adds a note). Nothing is pre-chosen; the preview writes nothing; the stored summary equals the previewed one; a repeat within ten minutes returns the same hand-off. Windows are PROPOSED (`private.wellbeing_share_cfg`, mirrored as `wellbeing.share_summary`).
3. `mental_health_worklist_config` (versioned, born UNSIGNED and INACTIVE, never edited or deleted, `sign_mental_health_worklist_config` CMO only), `mental_health_worklist_actions` (no policy and no grant: only the definer functions touch it; registered in the data registry and the minor guard).
4. `wellbeing_checkin_preferences.mood_insights_hidden` and `set_mood_insights_hidden` (a patient preference nothing else reads).
5. Four guards, all born off: `mental_health_worklist_enabled`, `wellbeing_summary_share_enabled`, `panic_tool_enabled`, `mood_insights_enabled`, with their conditions patched into `private.go_live_conditions` and the attestable list. The two wording guards bind the attestation to a fingerprint of the exact `panic.*` / `insights.*` strings (`private.s56b_*_fingerprint`, pinned by `packages/i18n/src/s56b-wording-pin.test.ts`), so one changed word closes the feature until the CMO attests the new text. `public.wellbeing_features_open()` answers "which are open for me" in one call (test accounts pass the wording gate, real people do not).

## The worklist (deterministic, no model)
Rules (PROPOSED v1, in the CMO's hands): a PHQ-9 or GAD-7 that rose by a set amount between the two latest screens; a screen overdue against the signed schedule (the rule is OFF while `mental_health_screening_cadences` is unsigned, and the screen says so); a sleep diary with most nights short or poor; regular check-ins that stopped; a hand-off, summary or request to talk nobody has marked seen. Candidates are patients who used the mental wellbeing area at all. Rows are filtered to patients the caller is TIED to (`clinician_has_patient_access`, not break-glass, never `is_org_staff`), only for a clinician who is not a care coordinator; each patient served is an `audit_log` row (section `worklist`); the count is a bare number that is 0 for everyone else. Until a version is signed and the guard is on, the list returns `not_signed` or `off` and the page says so calmly (not an all-clear). The page always states what it does not detect: it is not a risk predictor and silence is not safety. "Make a follow-up task" calls `create_clinical_task` (`symptom_review`) and records an action; the patient stays on the list, marked, for the snooze days.

## Events
`wellbeing.summary_shared` (`handoff_id`) and `mh_worklist.follow_up_created` (`action_id`), ids only, through `emit_domain_event`; a failed emit is audited and never blocks.

## Screens
Patient web: wellbeing home and check-in (calm button), `ShareSummaryCard`, `MoodPatterns` (a server component: the counts are worked out on the server from the patient's own session and only sentences reach the browser; own profile only). Phone: the same three on the wellbeing screen; patterns are worked out on the phone; the feature answer is kept per person for offline. Clinician: `/clinician/mental-health-worklist` (nav, count badge, admin search entry), the shared-summary panel on the chart, the sign-off hub block (rules to sign, cover attestations, the exact wording with its fingerprint, the accessibility attestation).

## Safety rules
The calm tool needs no network and no account once open, logs nothing, and shows the way out to the crisis card ("I am in danger or thinking of harming myself") at every step; the crisis card is not behind any guard. Words only mode and reduced motion remove the moving circle. Patterns use only four fixed sentence templates that describe the same days and never a cause (a test bans causal words); they never feed a score, task or alert, are never shown to clinicians, Care Circle, sponsors or employers, send no push, and can be hidden. Admin view-as, break-glass and the sponsor surfaces are untouched and see none of this.

## Review round 1 (2026-10-22): what changed
1. A follow-up from the list marks every hand-off still waiting for that patient as seen by the acting clinician (audited), and the chart panel now lists questionnaire hand-offs too with Mark as seen, so no hand-off kind can pin a patient at the top forever.
2. The rules run only for patients who used the wellbeing area and are tied to the caller (the tie is applied first, then the windows); the menu count is kept 10 minutes per clinician in `mental_health_worklist_count_cache` (a bare number, no grant, `count_cache_minutes` in the signed rules).
3. Task keys are random (`mhw:`, `mhs:`); the follow-up is an `admin_clinical` task (the hand-off's own type, not a plan-gated doctor-time review). No new task type was needed.
4. Patients are told what the list uses (`wnotice.*`) and can opt out of the two activity rules (check-in silence, sleep diary) with `set_worklist_activity_opt_out`; scores and anything they send are unaffected. The notice is under a fingerprint attestation and shows only once the list is on.
5. Wording: calm tool opening line no longer assures safety; "sent" lines promise nothing; "Share with my care team" everywhere; `wsum.*`, `wnotice.*`, the tag labels and the weekday names are now under CMO fingerprint attestations (four fingerprints; the share guard gained a wording condition).
6. Phone: a refusal from the server closes every feature, a saved answer is used only when the server could not be reached, saved "open" for share and patterns lasts `offline_trust_days` (30), the calm tool keeps its saved "open" (documented exception).
7. The preview returns a hash; the send must carry it or is refused with `summary_changed` and a calm "look again".
8. Migrations re-stamped to `20261022104130` and `20261022104212` after the newest main-dev migration; rebased.
9. `seen_by` is no longer readable through the table (column grants); a repeat follow-up the same day by the same clinician returns the same task and writes no second action row.
