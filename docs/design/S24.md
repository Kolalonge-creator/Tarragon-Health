# S24 Design: prescriptions, referrals, care plan changes and titration proposals

Spec 4.5, 6.3, safety case 10; invariants INV-02 (signature), INV-01 (no model), INV-07 (neutral notices), INV-10 (audited reads), INV-11, INV-12, INV-15, INV-16. Research: `docs/research/S24.md`. Founder decision OQ-11 (2026-09-30): signature CHECK tables, `medications` becomes a projection, patient column allow-list tightened.

## 1. What exists (extended, not rebuilt)
Live, checked 2026-10-06: `prescriptions` (S05; 0 rows; state machine, `signed_before_send` CHECK, frozen items, stamp trigger), `medications` (4 clinician rows; tie-gated `prescribe_medication`, `amend_medication`, `confirm_medication_refill`; patient allow-list trigger already built in S05f), the whole prescription PDF chain (Rx number, verification code, public token, QR, supply record, phone desk, reprint), `specialist_referrals` (0 rows; `signed_before_send` CHECK, create gate, outcome and closure rules, referral letter PDF), `care_plans` (0 rows; staff UPDATE allowed by RLS with no signature, `care_plan_versions` snapshots), `condition_protocols` (WHO reference text, not a decision table), `triage_rule_sets` (S11 pattern for versioned, CMO-approved, immutable config). **Not present:** `care_plan_changes`, a titration step table, any titration code, `patient_tasks` in the live database.

## 2. Data model (one migration)
- **`prescriptions`** gains `amendment_reason`, `supersedes_prescription_id`, `safety_checks jsonb` (what ran, what it found, any override and its reason), `source_change_id`. `prescribe_medication` and `amend_medication` keep their callers: each now writes a signed `prescriptions` row (stamped by the acting prescriber) and projects it into `medications` (`medications.prescription_id`). A new BEFORE trigger on `medications` refuses, for any real user session, a clinician-source insert without a signed prescription of the same patient and any change to drug, dose, frequency, route, quantity, duration or repeats on an existing clinician row. Owner, migration and seed sessions are not user sessions and are unaffected.
- **`protocols`** (spec 4.5): `code`, `version`, `status` draft | approved | retired, `definition jsonb` (the step table), approver and time; one approved per code; immutable once approved (same guard as `triage_rule_sets`); no API write path; read by admin and the active CMO, plus `get_approved_protocol(code)`. No row is seeded. The placeholder step table exists only as a test fixture marked draft (spec 6.3).
- **`care_plan_changes`**: `patient_id`, `care_plan_id` (nullable: a medicine change needs no plan, and there are no plans yet), `kind` medication | target | reading_schedule, `proposed_by` engine | clinician, `proposal jsonb`, `before jsonb`, `rationale`, `protocol_id` plus `protocol_version` and `engine_inputs jsonb` (INV-16), `state` proposed | signed | rejected | confirmed | declined | expired, `signed_by` and `signed_at`, rejection, patient confirmation and decline stamps, `applied_at`, `applied_prescription_id`, `safety_checks`, `is_test`. CHECKs: any state past proposed or rejected has `signed_by` and `signed_at`; confirmed has `applied_at`; an engine row has a protocol and inputs. **No UPDATE or INSERT for anyone through the API; every transition is a definer function.** An unsigned change therefore cannot be saved as signed, and cannot touch the record.
- `care_plans` gains `reading_schedule jsonb`; a trigger refuses a user-session change to `target_ranges` or `reading_schedule` unless it comes through an applied change (INV-02 for plan targets).
- **Config** `care_change.behaviour` v1 (PROPOSED, CMO): `confirmWindowDays` 7, `minReadings` and every stop threshold live in the protocol definition, not here. Registry entry plus a mirror test.

## 3. Rules (the safe path)
1. **Propose.** Tied prescriber only (INV-12). A clinician proposal is typed by a person. An engine proposal comes from `proposeTitration` (pure, in `packages/clinical`, no clock, no I/O, no model) run by a server action under the clinician's own session on inputs read through the audited chart read; the row records protocol version and the exact inputs. Only an `approved` protocol may be used for a real patient; a draft protocol works only for `is_test` patients.
2. **Stop conditions are visible.** The evaluator returns either a proposal or `no_proposal` with reasons (pregnancy or unknown pregnancy status, open red or amber triage, too few or unreliable readings, low adherence, a change inside the review window, unknown inputs, no matching step). The clinician sees the reason; nothing is skipped silently. The evaluator holds no drug name, dose or threshold: all come from the protocol definition.
3. **Draft is clinician-only.** A patient cannot read a `proposed` or `rejected` row at all (RLS and the patient read function).
4. **Sign.** `sign_care_plan_change`: tied prescriber, stamped from `auth.uid()`; for a medicine change the safety checks run first (allergy match, allergy list empty and not confirmed, duplicate active drug, controlled list); a finding needs `p_override_reason`, stored. Same checks run in `prescribe_medication` and `amend_medication`.
5. **Patient confirms.** Signed changes reach the patient as a plain card with a neutral notice (INV-07). `confirm_care_plan_change` applies it: a medicine change creates the signed prescription, supersedes or stops the old row and projects it; a target or schedule change updates the plan. Decline sends a neutral notice to the signer and changes nothing. Equal buttons, no countdown, no pre-ticked box. An unanswered change expires after the window (sweep, cron) and the signer is told. Until confirmation the patient's medicines are unchanged (safety case 10).
6. **Prescription form.** A confirmed medicine change yields an ordinary clinician `medications` row, so the existing PDF, QR, verify page and supply record apply unchanged. The form works at any pharmacy and never promises acceptance (research item 1).
7. **Referrals** keep the S05 gate and the existing create, closure and letter code. Added here: a recorded patient consent to share the record before send (a `consent_shared_at` column and a CHECK that `status` past draft needs it), and a chase date so a referral cannot sit open (task-free: shown in the lead's list and the sweep notices). Specialist matching is untouched.

## 4. Events, audit, notices
Events (ids only, INV-07): `care_plan_change.proposed|signed|confirmed|declined|expired`. Every staff read is `audit_chart_read`; every transition writes `audit_log`. Templates (in-app, push, neutral): `care_change_ready_patient`, `care_change_declined_staff`, `care_change_expired_staff`. No notice names a drug, reading or condition.

## 5. Screens
Clinician chart: "Care plan changes" panel (suggest next step, propose, review evidence, sign with override reasons, reject). Patient web and mobile Medicines: "Your care team has a change for you" card (what changes, why, confirm or decline). en and pcm strings through `packages/i18n`; no em dashes.

## 6. Proof
DB proof `s24_prescriptions_and_care_plan_changes.sql` (registered): unsigned change cannot be saved or applied; signature stamp cannot be forged; patient cannot see a draft; confirm applies and nothing applies before; decline and expiry change nothing; direct medicine edit refused; anon has no grants; RLS refuses an untied clinician; two sabotages that must flip. Jest: titration evaluator with 100 percent branch coverage and every stop reason; safety case 10; wording lint; config mirror; server loaders.

## 7. Out of scope, logged
Drug interaction, renal and dose-range checks (no reference data; OQ), real step table (CMO), diabetes titration, a new task type for proposal review (needs CMO sign-off, S16b pattern), caregiver or guardian confirmation for dependants (OQ-70), a pharmacy-side e-prescription push (S28).
