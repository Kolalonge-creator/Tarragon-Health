# S20 Design: quality and safety (audits, tier 1 counts, hand-back review, speak-up, expiry removal)

Spec 7.8, 9.1, 9.5 and rows 23.15 to 23.21; safety case 16. Depends on S17 (PR #927, unmerged; this branch is stacked on `s17/queue-next`). Research: `docs/research/S20.md`. Invariants touched: INV-07 (no clinical content in a notice or event), INV-10 (the audit case file read is logged), INV-12 (the reviewer reads only through the logged function), INV-13 (`is_test`), INV-16 (every audit records the config and form version).

## 1. What already exists (extended, not rebuilt)
- S15: `credential_expiry_sweep()` (05:00 UTC) already warns at 90/30/0 days and **suspends** on expiry (grace and missing dates honoured), `clinician_is_eligible()`, `suspend_clinician_internal()`, `set_clinician_level()`, `credential_notify()` (the generic `credential_notice` template).
- S17: `expire_task_claims()` already releases the live claims of anyone no longer eligible; `clinician_reliability_events` already allows `audit_result`; the 3-in-7-days hand-back flag event; `availability_blocks`.
- Not present: any audit, concern, hand-back review or rota-removal object. S18 (lead assignment, rota) and S19 (paging) are unmerged, so lead-patient reassignment and on-call removal are signalled by an event, not performed here.

## 2. Decisions taken (all PROPOSED, CMO owned, in `quality_config`, mirrored as `quality.audit` in the code registry with a drift test)
- **Sampling:** every red-event task and every titration is audited; a deterministic 10 percent draw of the rest (a hash of the task id and month, so the draw is repeatable and stored on the audit); a per-clinician monthly floor of one audit once they completed 3 tasks; a per-reviewer monthly cap of 40.
- **Tier 1:** while a clinician is credentialing level 1, every completed task is audited until 20 audits are on file. At 20 with an average of at least 85 and no critical miss, a `clinician.tier1_audits_complete` event goes to the clinical lead. **Nothing is promoted automatically**; the lead uses S15 `set_clinician_level`. The lead can extend the count.
- **Form (version 1):** 6 pass/fail safety items (a fail is a critical miss and needs a written reason) and 6 quality items scored 0 to 4. Outcome from the score: `satisfactory` (85+), `minor_concerns` (70 to 84), `significant_concerns` (below 70), `unsafe` (any critical miss). A reason of at least 20 characters is required for anything but `satisfactory`.
- **No self-audit.** The reviewer is an active, eligible chief medical officer who is not the audited clinician and not at the monthly cap. If none exists the audit stays `unassigned` and is shown at the top of the lead's queue. A CMO's own work therefore has no reviewer until a second reviewer exists (OQ-138).
- **Reliability:** a submitted audit writes an `audit_result` event (weight 2; good = 1, 0.6, 0.2, 0 by outcome). The score stays advisory and a tie-break only (S17).
- **Hand-back review:** S17's threshold (more than 3 in 7 days) opens one `handback_reviews` row per clinician (one open at a time) holding the reason breakdown. The lead records an outcome (`no_action`, `coaching`, `competency_check`, `capacity_issue`, `concern_raised`) with a note. It never suspends and never changes pay.
- **Speak-up:** `safety_concerns` are readable only by the person who raised them and by the active chief medical officer (plus named backup readers). **Admin accounts, the "ops" capability, cannot read the table, the messages or the detail.** Acknowledge within 48 hours and respond within 14 days (config). Past the acknowledge time the concern also reaches the backup readers; if there are none, a neutral ops incident ("a clinical safety item is overdue", no content) is opened so the failure is visible. A concern can open a neutral incident for the lead. Any adverse action on someone who raised a concern in the last 12 months (human suspension or offboarding, a lowered level, and later rota or pay changes through `private.note_adverse_action`) opens a `retaliation_reviews` row for the lead.
- **Removal on expiry:** `private.remove_clinician_from_work(profile)` (claims released, future queue, on-call and bookable blocks cancelled, offered or pushed tasks back to the pool, `clinician.removed_from_work` emitted with `lead_reassignment_required`) runs at once when a clinician stops being active, and `private.remove_ineligible_from_work()` is the nightly safety net at 05:15 UTC, after the S15 sweep. A missing licence date never removes anyone (S15 rule).

## 3. Data model (one migration `*_s20_quality_and_safety.sql`)
`quality_config`; `clinical_audits` (+ `clinician_tier1_audit_extensions`, append only); `handback_reviews`; `safety_concerns`, `safety_concern_messages` (append only), `safety_concern_readers`; `retaliation_reviews`. Triggers: task completed schedules an audit (never able to block a completion), a hand-back opens a review, a clinician stopping work triggers removal, an adverse action checks for retaliation. Every table has RLS and select-only grants; every write goes through a function.

## 4. Events (ids and neutral facts only)
`clinical_audit.assigned`, `clinical_audit.completed`, `clinical_audit.concern_found`, `clinician.tier1_audits_complete`, `clinician.removed_from_work`. **No event and no `audit_log` row is ever written about a safety concern** (both are readable by operations); the concern's own protected message log is its record.

## 5. Screens
None here: the clinician "Raise a safety concern" button (9.1) and the clinical lead audit, concern and review screens (9.5) are S35 and S36. This session fixes the RPC contract they call (section 6).

## 6. RPC contract for the console
Clinician: `raise_safety_concern`, `my_safety_concerns`, `add_to_safety_concern`, `tier1_audit_progress` (own), and a plain select on `clinical_audits` (RLS shows only their own submitted audits). Clinical lead: `clinical_audit_queue`, `audit_case_file` (logged read), `submit_clinical_audit`, `close_audit_followup`, `reassign_clinical_audit`, `request_clinical_audit`, `extend_tier1_audits`, `handback_review_queue`, `close_handback_review`, `safety_concern_inbox`, `acknowledge_safety_concern`, `respond_to_safety_concern`, `close_safety_concern`, `open_incident_for_concern`, `add_safety_concern_backup_reader`, `remove_safety_concern_backup_reader`, `retaliation_review_queue` (also backup readers), `close_retaliation_review`.

## 7. Safety rules
1. A concern is never visible to ops, in a table, a function result, an event, a notice or an incident.
2. Scheduling an audit can never fail a task completion; a failure is logged and the nightly job catches it.
3. Nothing here suspends, promotes or changes pay without a person; only expiry removal is automatic, and it follows the S15 rules exactly.
4. No audit result, concern text or reason goes into a notice or event (INV-07).
5. The reviewer cannot be the audited clinician; the lead cannot read a case file unless assigned (or the lead), and every read is logged.

## 9. Open questions (written to `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` as OQ-137 onward)
1. Who are the backup readers for concerns (no superadmin role exists; admin is ops)? Recommend the founder profile, added by the lead.
2. Who audits the CMO's own tasks? Recommend a second senior reviewer once hired; until then unassigned.
3. May a concern be anonymous? Recommend no: named but protected, because follow-up and retaliation review need an identity.
4. Audit numbers (10 percent, 20 tasks, thresholds, SLAs) need CMO sign-off.
5. S15 warns at 90/30/0 days; the research suggests 60/30/14/7 and a November reminder for the 31 December cluster. Config change in S15's rules, not built here.
