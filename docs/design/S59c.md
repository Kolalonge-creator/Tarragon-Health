# S59c design note: the assessment experience on top of S59 and S60 (follow-up, describe it, why this result, safety net)

Branch `feat/s59-assessment-ux`, off `origin/main-dev`. `symptom_checker_enabled` stays OFF and everything below is unreachable while it is off (database refusal or no-op, plus the app check, both tested). Nothing is applied, deployed, signed or switched on. Every piece of wording and timing is an UNSIGNED DRAFT that behaves exactly like the S59 urgency map: unsigned, malformed or drifted means the feature falls back to the safe behaviour it had before.

## 1. Reused, not rebuilt
The signed v1 pathways and `runSymptomCheck`, the bundled on-device red-flag floor and its parity guard, `symptom_triage_assessments` and its escalation trigger, `private.patient_may_page_doctor`, the existing `emergency_events` path, `private.patient_reminder_channel`, the S10 outbox, `private.go_live_open_patient` (INV-14), the S59 audited read pattern (`can_staff_read_clinical`, `audit_chart_read`), `NotADiagnosis` (one label, one catalogue entry), the shared danger-phrase screen (`screenAssistantMessage`), the proposed-config registry, the CMO sign-off hub, `symptomOptionLabel`.

## 2. Follow-up check-back (`checkback`)
A completed check that ended as self-care or a routine appointment is followed up 48 or 24 hours later (config): "better, same or worse?", "did you get care?", and any warning sign that has started since (ticked from the pathway's OWN signed vocabulary, so nothing free-form reaches an alert). Delivery: an in-app item always, plus the patient's own channel (`patient_reminder_channel`), neutral text and ids only (INV-07), notices held to 07:00 to 21:00 Lagos, one reminder, expiry, a late answer still taken. Never required for anything; a failed notice or event is audited and opens a sev3 incident but never fails the assessment or the answer.
Decision table (database function `answer_symptom_checkback`, TypeScript twin `decideCheckback`, both pinned by tests): floor emergency -> emergency; a new sign, floor urgent or "worse" -> escalate; "same" at or after the nudge time -> book; "same" earlier -> keep watching and ONE more check is scheduled at the nudge time (round 2, never a third); "better" with nothing new -> close. The floor is re-run from the STORED answers plus the ticked signs on the device first and on the server, and the database ALSO derives it itself from the signed protocol (`private.symptom_red_flag_floor`) and takes the more urgent of the two, so a direct caller can never lower it. Escalate raises ONE urgent alert carrying the original summary (complaint, date, result, onset, severity, what was ticked, red flags then; never a pregnancy answer) when the plan includes doctor time; otherwise the screen gives guidance and says so. Emergency uses the existing emergency path. Urgent and emergency results, and a check that raised an emergency event, are never followed up; a later urgent or emergency check by the same person cancels a pending one.

## 3. Describe it
Deterministic, no model, no embeddings. The shared danger-phrase screen runs over the raw text FIRST, on the device; a hit shows emergency guidance and throws the words away, and ALSO records the bare fact on the existing emergency path (OQ-S59c-05, decided: `record_describe_danger`, session key plus coarse code `danger` or `self_harm`, once per session and once per `danger_event_min_gap_minutes` (PROPOSED, 30, in the describe config), any plan, closed with the checker, never messages the emergency contact (`suppress_contact_notify`), refused while acting for someone else; it answers `doctor_alerted`, and the screen says the care team was told only when a clinician was really paged, otherwise it says it was saved and points to the app or a clinic; a failure is audited with a sev3 incident and never blocks the guidance), and a screen that throws counts as a hit. Only then the synonym table (`describe`, English and common Nigerian English, no Pidgin) routes to one of the SIGNED, ACTIVE pathways: a clear match is offered for the person to confirm, every pathway that scores above zero is offered as a choice (more than two if more fit), no match goes to the ordinary checklist and picker, never to reassurance. The words are component state only (no storage, URL, log or network) and are cleared as soon as used. The bundled table works offline. The box is shown only when a signed table matches the live one; unsigned leaves the checker exactly as it was.

## 4. Why this result
`explainResult` looks reasons up by the ids of the rules that FIRED in this result (all of them, not just the first) plus the question-tree outcome and any tightening entry, and shows the person's own answers beside each. No fallback text: no signed entry, no section. No ranking, no cause, no diagnosis (the output type has no field for one); the shared label is on the screen. Pregnancy answers are never repeated.

## 5. Safety net and the save card
A generic block is always shown from the catalogue. Pathway lines (`safety_net`) are added only when signed; the national emergency number only when signed AND marked confirmed ("where it connects"), and no helpline number exists anywhere. "Save this card" is a plain-text result card (copy, share, screenshot), built from what is on screen, never stored; "Clear this screen" forgets the result and, if copied, overwrites the clipboard.

## 6. Data (migration `20261022115652_s59c_assessment_ux.sql`)
`symptom_ux_config` (kind, version, immutable draft-form `rules`, signature in the row's own columns, one active per kind, an older version can never be re-activated), `sign_symptom_ux_config` (CMO only), `symptom_ux_active_config` (adds the signature to the stored draft; what the apps compare their bundle with). `symptom_checkbacks` (patient reads own; no staff policy; outcome columns for the accuracy audit; data registry and minor guard). `schedule_symptom_checkback` (exception-safe AFTER INSERT trigger on assessments), `symptom_checkback_sweep` (pg_cron every 15 minutes: bounded backfill of at most 500 and only inside the window, cancel superseded, cancel too-old rows that were never opened, expire, open, remind; in-app notices are never held by the quiet window), `my_symptom_checkback`, `answer_symptom_checkback`, `read_symptom_checkback_audited` (tied, reason required, coordinator refused, every read and refusal audited). Events `symptom_checkback.sent` and `.answered` (ids only). Templates `symptom_checkback_due` and `_nudge` (neutral). Mirrored configs: `symptom.checkback`, `symptom.describe_synonyms`, `symptom.result_explainer`, `symptom.safety_net` (registry, bundle and migration seed; drift tests on all three).

## 7. Safety rules that hold
Red flags first, on the device; urgency is never lowered by anything here (describe returns a route, never a category; the explainer and card receive a result and return none; the follow-up decision only moves up). Closed means closed (database and app). No LLM anywhere (source scan). Shared-phone safe (no result detail in notices, clear-screen, nothing persisted).
