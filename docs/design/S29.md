# S29 Design: Care Circle (invites, permissions, supporter views, neutral red alerts, pay for a loved one)

Spec 4.7, 8.6, safety case 21, Module 17 rows 17.1 to 17.4 (17.5 stays a dormant flag, 17.6 to 17.9 are S69). Depends on S26 (not built) and S13 (done). Built on the S25 branch because pay for a loved one needs real orders (founder choice 2026-10-06). Invariants touched: INV-07, INV-08, INV-09, INV-10, INV-13, INV-14, INV-15, INV-16. Research: `docs/research/S29.md`.

## 1. What exists, and the one decision that differs from the reconciliation
- **`profile_access`** (live, 0 rows) is the older family and caregiver grant: `permissions caregiver_permission[]`, `clinical_access`, 8-category `profile_access_categories`, `care_access_requests` + `request_care_access(phone)` (invite for someone who already has an account, found by phone, same organisation). It stays as it is for the family screens.
- **The reconciliation (RECONCILIATION line 210) said "live wins: use `profile_access`". This build does not, and the reason is a finding.** A scan of live policies found read paths that admit ANY `profile_access` grantee with no permission test at all: `profiles_select` (the whole patient profile row), `booking_requests_select`, `vaccination_adverse_events_select`, `vaccination_card_extractions_select`, and the vaccination record and schedule updates. A Care Circle member stored in `profile_access` would inherit every one of them, which is exactly what safety case 21 forbids. So the Care Circle is a **separate table, `care_circle_members`, which no existing policy reads**. A member therefore opens nothing by default; the supporter reads through one function that checks one permission per block. This is also the spec's own shape (4.7). Recorded as OQ-192 (the older paths are a pre-existing breadth issue for legacy grantees and are not changed here).
- Reused: `care_access_events` and `private.log_care_access` (the patient's "who looked" log; its scope vocabulary gains `care_circle`), `profiles.receives_care` (false is the supporter-only account, 2026-08-01; the proxy already confines it to `/patient/supporting`), S25 `orders` and `create_order` (its `beneficiary` seam, recorded as OQ-171), S19 `pages`, S13 neutral templates and the INV-07 lint, `private.page_incident`, `platform_modules`.
- The old sponsor and care voucher screens and tables stay untouched. Pay for a loved one is the new path through S25 orders only; no stored balance.

## 2. Decisions taken in this build
1. **Permissions** are `text[]` on the member and the invite, CHECK-limited to the five names in spec 4.7: `adherence_summary`, `weekly_bp_trend`, `appointments`, `red_alerts`, `pay_for_care`. They are summary level: none opens a table, each opens one block of `circle_supporter_view`. Off until ticked (an empty list is refused).
2. **Invite delivery is by the patient's own share sheet, not by the platform** (INV-08: SMS only for sign-in codes; WhatsApp is removed). The link carries a one-time token **in the path** (so it survives the sign-in redirect). The invite is bound to the phone or email the patient typed, and accepting needs a signed-in account whose **verified** phone or email matches. Platform-sent email is OQ-190.
3. **Token**: 32 random bytes, only the SHA-256 is stored, shown once, expires in 72 hours (PROPOSED), single use. The contact is stored only as a hash plus a masked hint. Every failure to preview or accept (unknown token, used, expired, wrong account, too many tries, the patient themself) returns the same `{ok: false}`; a wrong account counts an attempt (5, PROPOSED) and the function RETURNS rather than raises so the count is not rolled back. A new invite for the same person cancels the old one. The patient makes at most 5 invites a day (cancelled ones count) and the circle holds at most 8.
4. **Grant**: accepting writes `care_circle_members` (default 12 months, PROPOSED). Expiry is checked inside the one gate `private.circle_member_for`, so an expired member is a revoked member on the very next call, before the hourly sweep marks the row. The patient edits ticks any time, removes in one call, and a supporter can leave. Revocation and expiry are logged to `care_access_events`. Only the patient is told, and only when a supporter LEAVES (`circle_left`, no name); a supporter the patient removes is told nothing, because a "you were removed" message can cause conflict or coercion at home (research), and their next look says the page is not available.
5. **Supporter view** (`circle_supporter_view`): `adherence` (doses taken of due in the last 7 days, no medicine names), `bp_trend` (weekly averages for 8 weeks and a direction word against the week before; never a single reading, time, note or symptom), `appointments` (next booked time, number missed in 30 days; no reason, clinician or notes), `can_pay`. A block appears only with its permission; every read is audited (the patient's own "who looked" list). Any non-member, expired or removed caller gets `circle_not_found`.
6. **Neutral red alert (INV-07; closes the supporter half of OQ-132).** An AFTER INSERT trigger on a root `pages` row notifies each active member holding `red_alerts` (matching the page's `is_test`): in-app and push, critical priority (quiet hours never hold it), template `circle_check_in`, empty payload, one per member per channel per page. The trigger catches its own failure, opens a sev1 `ops_incidents` row and never stops the page. Opening the app shows only the person's name and "please call them now" (`circle_open_alerts`), with no grade, reading or cause. The clinical lead's review task stays S20.
7. **Pay for a loved one**: `create_order` allows a beneficiary when the caller holds an unexpired `pay_for_care` membership of that patient's circle, same organisation and same test flag. Membership and lead capacity are checked on the beneficiary. A supporter-only account cannot buy for itself. Paying opens no record access (nothing in the order path touches the circle) and the payer sees only their own orders and receipts. The patient is told, once, "someone has paid for your care" (no payer, item or amount). A gifted care pack starts the lead assignment like any care pack; the patient's acceptance of a gift is OQ-191. Refunds go to the original payer (S26). Naira only. The edge function `order-checkout` takes an optional `beneficiary` and sends the payer's own email to Paystack.
8. **Config** (`packages/shared/src/proposed-config` key `care_circle.rules`, mirrored by `care_circle_config`, a test keeps them identical): invite ttl 72 hours, default grant 365 days, 5 invites a day, 8 members, 5 attempts, 8 weeks. All PROPOSED, owner Founder; the database validates the shape (a missing key would quietly lift a limit).
9. **17.5 concierge** is a dormant row in `platform_modules` (`care_circle_concierge`), no code behind it.

## 3. Data model (one migration, `20261006190842_s29_care_circle.sql`; live `list_migrations` to be checked before it is applied)
- `care_circle_config`, `care_circle_invites`, `care_circle_members`; RLS on; the patient reads their own rows, a supporter reads the rows that name them; the invite table grants select per column and never exposes a hash; no direct writes.
- Public functions (`security definer`, `search_path = ''`, execute to `authenticated` only, `from public` revoked and checked with `has_function_privilege`): `create_care_circle_invite`, `preview_care_circle_invite`, `accept_care_circle_invite`, `cancel_care_circle_invite`, `update_care_circle_member`, `revoke_care_circle_member`, `my_care_circle`, `my_supported_people`, `circle_view_log`, `circle_supporter_view`, `circle_open_alerts`; `create_order` replaced.
- Triggers: `pages_notify_circle`, `orders_notify_paid_for_you`. Cron: `care-circle-expiry` (hourly).
- Templates in code (`templates.ts`, `describe-in-app.ts`) so the INV-07 lint covers them: `circle_check_in`, `circle_joined`, `circle_left`, `circle_paid_for_you`.

## 4. Screens (all strings in `packages/i18n`, en and pcm, no em dashes, "your care team")
- Web patient `/patient/care-circle`: members with switches and expiry, waiting invites with Cancel, an invite form that shows the link once (copy and share), and "who looked". The "Your people" card links to it.
- Web `/patient/supporting/join/[token]`: shows who asked and exactly what is shared; Accept only on the button; one message for every failure.
- Web `/patient/supporting` gains the Care Circle list and any open check-in request; `/patient/supporting/circle/[patientId]` is the read-only supporter view (no export) with Leave and Pay; `/pay` reuses the S25 shop in beneficiary mode. The proxy lets a supporter-only account reach the join, view and pay pages and the Paystack return page `/patient/membership/paid` only.
- Mobile: a Care Circle section in "Your people" (patient side) and a Care Circle section in "People you support" (supporter side, including Pay). The join link opens in the browser; native deep linking is a follow-up.

## 5. Safety rules and tests
1. **Safety case 21**: as a member without `weekly_bp_trend`, `vitals_readings`, `observations`, `patient_timeline`, `symptoms`, `medication_logs`, `medications`, `appointments`, `booking_requests`, `vaccination_adverse_events` and the patient's `profiles` row all return nothing, and the supporter function has no BP block and no reading value; with the permission the function returns weekly averages and the raw tables are STILL empty. Sabotage: the gate removed must flip.
2. Invite: hash only, contact never in clear, identical failures, attempt cap, rate limit, expiry, cannot invite yourself, a child or a supporter-only account cannot invite.
3. Revoke and expiry end access on the next call; the sweep marks and logs.
4. Red alert reaches only members holding `red_alerts`, never an expired or removed one, once per page; a failed alert opens an incident and the page still exists. Sabotage: the filter removed must flip.
5. Pay for a loved one: refused without `pay_for_care`, expired, a different patient, a stranger; allowed with it; the entitlement is the patient's and once; the payer reads no record. Sabotage: the pay gate removed must flip.
6. RLS, grants (anon none), config validation, templates through the INV-07 lint, screens through axe, the edge function (Deno), the parsers.

## 6. Not built here (and why)
Platform-sent invite email (OQ-190); patient acceptance of a gifted care pack (OQ-191); the older `profile_access` breadth (OQ-192); entitlement lifecycle and refunds (S26: a payer refund path is documented, not built); concierge (flag only); group challenges and sessions (S69); cross-organisation supporters (same organisation only, as the existing guard); native deep links for the join link; the clinical lead's red-event review task (S20); a live Paystack test payment (needs the founder's test key, as for S25); production migration apply.
