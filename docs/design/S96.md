# S96 design note: MSK pain programmes (back, neck, knee, hip) on the S63 engine

Founder decisions 2026-10-27: all four programmes; exercises as text steps plus simple line drawings (no video); no physiotherapist now, the Chief Medical Officer approves content through the existing mechanism, and a physiotherapist reviewer can be added later without a rebuild (the content is versioned rows and approval is one function; this build adds no physiotherapist role). Module 14, spec lines about 1500 to 1550.

## Reused, not rebuilt
The S63 engine: the four scaffold rows `pain_back`, `pain_neck`, `pain_knee`, `pain_hip` (guard `therapy_pain_enabled`, OFF), the table-driven fail-closed entry screen, the per-session re-check, checkpoint scores, the worsening check and task, content versions frozen on approval, CMO-editable exclusion lists, the offline cache and completion queue, the shared-phone gate. The S48a activity safety gate (`private.activity_gate_for`) is asked before every pain session. The S16 clinical task path (`create_clinical_task`) and the F1 crisis path are reached through the unchanged S63 routing functions.

## What changed
- Migration `20261027184235_s96_msk_programmes.sql` (NOT applied): `therapy_programme_sessions.exercises`; `flare_up_review` as an enrolment stop reason; `resume_therapy_enrolment` patched by exact-match anchor so a tied clinician can resume a flare-up pause; table `therapy_msk_events` (own-read only, in the data registry); four functions; exclusion list version 2 for the four programmes (68 items); config version 2; 32 draft session rows; the programmes move from `scaffold` to `draft`.
- `start_msk_session` asks the activity gate, then calls `start_therapy_session` unchanged. `rest` opens nothing and counts nothing; `restrict` passes a ceiling to the screen, which shows gentle exercises only. The gate's reason is never returned (it could name a pregnancy or a condition). An error asking the gate is treated as gentle only.
- `record_msk_exercise_rating` (1 to 5, one row per exercise per session per day, the "too hard twice" flag). `complete_msk_session` is the completion gate: a week finishes only after enough different days have a rating, then `complete_therapy_session` runs unchanged (checkpoint scores, worsening).
- `report_msk_flare_up` ("my pain got worse"): re-runs `private.therapy_evaluate_screen` on the latest list. Positive: the plan is paused as `flare_up_review` (the hospital-now route stops it and runs the crisis path), the S63 routing raises a `clinical_tasks` row, the flare-up is recorded. Clear: a step-down is recorded for the configured days and the screens show fewer, gentler exercises. No language model anywhere (INV-01).
- Outcome measures at weeks 1, 4 and 8 (checkpoint sessions 1, 4, 8): the free PEG (average pain, enjoyment of life, general activity, each 0 to 10) plus one function goal item (0 to 10). The licensed Oswestry is not used. A rise of 2 on any item (PROPOSED) pauses the programme through the existing `therapy_assess_progress`. There is no absolute line, so a high first score alone never pauses it. Session 1's scores are asked when session 1 is finished, so the baseline is the end of the first week, not before it (the engine's existing rule).

## Content
Shared: `packages/shared/src/therapy-content/msk-drafts.ts` (22 exercises, 4 x 8 weekly sessions, 3 exercises at most per day, one gentle at least), `msk-illustrations.ts` (original line drawings as shape data, 22 of them), `proposed-config/msk-config-data.ts` (the 68 entry items and the `msk` settings). Doses, hold times, repeats, the "mild rise that settles within an hour" rule of thumb, the daily cap, the 3 different days, the 3 day step-down and the PEG rise are all PROPOSED. Basis for the CMO to verify against the source: NICE NG59, NICE NG226, general neck, knee and hip self-management guidance. Nothing says digital MSK programmes work well: the evidence is a small effect on pain, and the text promises no result.

Drawings are data rendered as inline SVG on the web and with react-native-svg on the phone from the same shapes. They ship inside the app code, so they work offline with nothing to cache or download. They use the text colour (light and dark both read), carry alt text and a title, and have no third-party image, font or file. Opened sessions (with their exercises) are kept on the device by the existing S63 cache.

## Guards and sign-off
`therapy_pain_enabled` stays OFF. Its four conditions are unchanged and sufficient: content approved, the NEWEST entry list confirmed (this migration makes version 2 the newest, so version 2 must be confirmed), clinician cover attested, outcome thresholds attested. The guard's old `blocks` wording still says "scaffolds: no content exists yet"; it was left alone because a guard row update was not needed to ship and `go_live_guards` has its own change trigger (see OQ-S96-8).
The sign-off hub (`/clinician/clinical-signoff`) has a new line `msk_programmes` (`readPendingMskProgrammesSignoff`, `MskProgrammesPanel`, `msk-programmes-actions.ts`): the CMO reads every entry question with its route and every session with its exercises, then approves the content version and confirms the list. The panel calls the existing `approve_therapy_programme_version` and `confirm_therapy_exclusion_list`. The build never calls them. The two attestations are on the go-live page. No new page was added, so no admin-search entry was needed; no navigation entry was added while the guard is off.

## Review fixes (2026-10-27)
- The activity gate, the filtered exercises and the days rule are enforced in the database: the S63 start and completion refuse pain programmes unless called through the MSK functions (transaction-local flag `tarragon.msk_path`, anchors fail loudly); `start_msk_session` returns only the exercises at or under the gate ceiling, within the daily cap and, in a step-down, gentle and at most the configured number. A gate error fails closed to gentle and is written to the audit log (no patient data).
- Warning signs: `non_paging` is read from the rule row for every stop (answered or not); the task is due in `route_due_minutes.crisis_non_paging` = 90 (config v2, PROPOSED); no more urgent non-paging task class exists, so the type stays `symptom_review`.
- `report_msk_flare_up` is retry-safe: a repeat on a stopped or paused plan returns the same stopped status and route; a clear repeat within `msk.flare_dedupe_minutes` returns the first step-down.
- The two migrations were merged into `20261027184235_s96_msk_programmes.sql`, so the non-paging flag can never apply apart from list v2.

## Offline
Opening a session needs a signal (the safety re-check and the gate run in the database). An opened week, with its exercises and drawings, can be read again with no signal. A finished week that cannot be sent is queued with its programme code and sent later through `complete_msk_session`; a not-enough-days answer keeps it queued and says so (OQ-S96-6, closed). Ratings and the flare-up need a signal and say so plainly if they fail; they are not queued.

## Not built
A clinician-facing summary of the MSK outcome trend (the existing consent-gated `read_therapy_progress_audited` returns the scores, but no MSK view was built); a physiotherapist role; video; audio; a "flare-up since last review" count in a clinician view; the monthly-doctor linkage of the Membership tiers; any message to a patient outside the app.

## Follow-up decisions (founder, 2026-10-27), migration `20261027191255_s96b_non_paging_and_physio_marker.sql`
- Possible heart or stroke warning signs stop the programme with the hospital-now guidance but do NOT page: a per-rule `non_paging` flag on `crisis` rules, read by `private.therapy_route_stop` (a crisis whose stops are all non-paging raises the normal same-day `symptom_review` task; any paging crisis stop still runs the S63 crisis path). Patched by exact-match anchors on `therapy_evaluate_screen`, `therapy_route_stop` and `save_therapy_exclusion_list` (so a CMO-saved list keeps the flag). Mental-health crisis rules are untouched (flag false) and still page. The flare-up flow needed no change: it already stops on `crisis` through `therapy_stop_enrolment`, which now routes without a page.
- Internal "not physiotherapist-reviewed" marker on the four pain content versions (`not_physiotherapist_reviewed`), shown on the hub panel, never patient-visible; cleared later by recording a review in the two nullable hook columns. No physiotherapist role is built.
