## 2. S75a: one minimum-cell rule
`private.min_cell_floor(org_floor, attributes)` is the greater of the platform minimum (20, or 30 for two attributes) and an organisation's own `min_cohort_size`; it fails closed without configuration. `suppress_cell(n, categories, min)` returns the reason a cell may not be shown (null = may be shown); `suppress_difference` is the held-back-month rule and the "people who did not agree" rule; `suppress_groups` does primary plus complementary withholding (one withheld group takes the next smallest; two or more that together are under the minimum do too). Every aggregate surface now calls them: BP reports (90 and 180 days, generalised in one body), analytics view, sponsor report and held-back month, risk distribution, triage agreement, corporate subsidy summary, reproductive analytics, payer dashboard and payer board report.
Gaps closed on the way: a withheld BP cohort still printed "enrolled" and "not yet due"; triage grades were withheld one by one without their neighbour; the subsidy breakdown counted claims, not people, and `institution_subsidy_summary` was executable by `anon`; the reproductive frequencies counted log rows and included test accounts; `generate_payer_board_report` would have raised on the S38 `bp_control_*` specs (it looped every spec) so it now computes only keys it knows. `organisations.min_cohort_size` is reconciled (greater of the two; column default left alone, now never the binding number).
`triage_accuracy_report` stays as a thin deprecated wrapper of `triage_agreement_report` until the web deploy lands.

## 3. S75b: other outcome measures
`outcome_measure_config` (immutable per version, mirrored with a drift test) and `outcome_measure_snapshots` (append-only, no policy, no grant). Windows: day 0 from before to after joining, days 90 and 180 plus or minus half-width; the nearest result wins; due after the window plus 3 grace days. HbA1c from final `lab_analyte_readings` (% or mmol/mol converted); weight from validated `vitals_readings`; PHQ-9 and GAD-7 from `mental_health_screens`. Adults only; pregnancy excludes HbA1c and weight; unknown date of birth excludes. A PHQ-9 screen with item 9 above zero, the crisis flag or a score of 20+ gives `excluded/safeguarding` with no value or band stored, and `ensure_mental_health_followup` raises the same kind of clinician alert if the existing trigger left none. `outcome_measures_report` shows nothing until `proposed_config_signoffs` holds a "confirmed" newest decision for the active version; unsigned or "changes requested" means withheld. Mental-health results are grouped by starting band. Nothing is signed by any migration.
