## Behaviour
- **Guard (INV-14):** `isAssistantOpen` calls `go_live_guard_is_open('assistant_enabled')`, fails closed, checked first in `runCoachTurn`, `runQuickAction`, the mobile `message` and `quick-action` routes, and inside `hasCoachAccess` (so screens do not draw the assistant early). The mobile hand-off route ("I want to speak to someone") and the prep-draft send are deliberately NOT behind the guard: contact with the care team never waits on an AI guard (OQ-284).
- **Red flags (INV-01):** `keyword-guardrail.ts` has no list of its own. It calls `screenAssistantMessage` in `@tarragon/clinical`, built on the shared `WRITTEN_QUESTION_DANGER_PHRASES` plus extra wordings and word pairs ("arm" with "numb"). A drift test fails if a shared phrase is dropped. No model call on a match.
- **Deterministic screens around the model (`reply-screen.ts`, `info-question.ts`, `graph.ts` `finalizeModelReply`):** sensitive-result question and reply; dose requests (a change such as stop, skip or double also flags the care team, an "how much should I take" does not) and a sentence-level dose-advice reply screen that lets a restatement of the record, a "do not stop it" and a pointer to the care team pass; the structural no-source refusal, which needs REVIEWED content or an already-shown explanation (a record lookup says what the numbers are, not what they mean) and does not depend on the model flagging the question (a deterministic floor too); the symptom hand-off floor, kept even when the answer is refused. Pure functions, each unit tested.
- **Red-flag matching is whole-word** ("arm" never matches "pharmacy", "numb" never "numbers"), with a regression test over the old private list's positives.
- **Sources:** built from rows the code read (reviewed content with owner, version, review date; "your record"; the shown explanation; protocol limits), returned in the API result and stored on the message.
- **Prep:** `changesSinceLastReview` and a question list from the record; a draft the patient edits and sends as their own message (`start_care_thread` on their session). Never written to the record by the assistant (INV-11).
- **Nudges:** deterministic `chooseDailyNudge` and `composeWeeklyReflection`; a daily cron (`/api/cron/assistant-nudges`) inserts generic in-app notifications only for real patients when the guard is on, and for `is_test` patients always.

## AI registry
No new AI call site: the nudge, weekly reflection, prep and question list are deterministic. The coach (AI-001) and the AI-002 nudge proposer are already registered and routed through `runGovernedAi`. `COACH_PROMPT_VERSION` moved to `2026-10-07.1`; if a governed prompt override is active it must be re-approved by the CMO (nothing was approved or signed here).

## Go-live and sign-off
`assistant_enabled` stays off. It cannot be switched on until reviewed, owned, dated KB rows exist, the CMO has reviewed a passing red-flag run, and the CMO presses the switch. The extra red-flag phrases, the minimum row count, the INV-04 token list and every fixed reply are PROPOSED wording for the CMO.
