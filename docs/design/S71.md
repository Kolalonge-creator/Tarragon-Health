# S71 design note: checkout and payments, part 1 (Module 19, functions 19.1 to 19.7)

Founder decisions: `docs/DECISIONS.md` S71-1 (HMO adapter per HMO, manual operations check as the fallback, hashed member number, no capitation), S72-2 (no instalments). Research: `docs/research/S71.md`. Invariants touched: INV-07, INV-09, INV-13, INV-14, INV-15, INV-16, I8. Open questions raised: OQ-400 to OQ-410.

## 1. What exists and is reused
S25 `catalog_items`, `prices`, `orders`, `payments`, `entitlements`, `record_order_payment`, the Paystack adapter and mock, `order-checkout`, `order-verify`, `order-reconcile` (the 5-minute sweep that catches late bank transfer and USSD), `paystack-webhook`; S26 refunds; S29 Care Circle and `pay_for_care`; S37 go-live guards; S38e `sponsor_cohorts` and `profile_cohorts`; S73 `price_quote_calc`, `price_quote`, `create_order`, price books, `pricing_config`; S10 events; the OQ-223 Vault-pepper HMAC pattern; caregiver permissions (`private.can_act_for(..., 'manage_payments')`). Nothing is rebuilt; the legacy per-domain checkouts (OQ-23) are not touched.

## 2. Data model (migration `20261007223107_s71_checkout_core.sql`)
- `catalog_items.kind` gains `home_kit` (and `entitlements.kind`). Three placeholders (`consultation_single`, `lab_panel_standard`, `home_kit_sample`) are OFF with no price row, so they cannot be switched on (`item_has_no_price`). No price is invented (OQ-400). No `device` kind: Tarragon does not sell hardware (CLAUDE.md, 2026-08-02).
- `sponsor_config` (versioned PROPOSED rules, mirrored as `sponsor.rules` in the code registry, drift test): HMO checks per day, member number length limits.
- `sponsor_programmes`: sponsor organisation, item codes, `share_percent`, `budget_kobo` (bigint), optional `per_person_cap_kobo`, eligibility by `cohort` (S38e) or `hmo_eligibility`, `rules` (the HMO pass validity in days), dates, status draft/active/closed, `is_test`. Immutable after creation except status; the budget moves only through an audited function that cannot go below what is reserved or committed.
- `sponsor_reservations`: one per order, `reserved` then `committed` or `released`. The budget is a CEILING held at the sponsor, never value a person holds (INV-09): nobody can spend, transfer or see it as a balance.
- `hmo_eligibility_checks`: `patient_id`, `hmo_org_id`, `member_number_hash` (HMAC-SHA256 under the Vault secret `hmo_member_pepper`, fails closed without it), `result` (`pending_manual`, `eligible`, `not_eligible`), `decided_via` (`adapter` or `manual`), `checked_at`, `is_test`. A decided check never changes.
- `orders.amount_kobo` may be 0 when `sponsor_share_kobo` is above 0 (a fully covered order). Reference prefix `spo_` (never sent to Paystack).
- Finance account `1210 Sponsor receivable`. `post_order_to_ledger` (live body from S25b/c kept) posts buyer part to 1020, sponsor part to 1210, one credit for the whole price; the recognition schedule spreads the whole price.
- Event `sponsor_entitlement.granted` v1 (required keys `order_id`, `programme_id`; payload also carries the item code and kind only, INV-07).
- Every new table: RLS on, no grant to `authenticated` or `anon`; people reach data only through audited security-definer functions.

## 3. Behaviour
- **Quote** (`private.price_quote_calc`, S73 body with step 4 extended): a price-book share needs a programme of that sponsor behind it; a programme-only share (cohort or HMO pass) applies only when the buyer IS the beneficiary (a supporter neither gets the cover nor learns it exists, OQ-223/OQ-408). A share the budget cannot carry is not promised: the person is quoted the ordinary price with `sponsor_budget_short`.
- **`create_order`** (same six-argument signature, so no 42725 ambiguity): checkout guard, then the S73 flow, then `sponsor_reserve` (programme row locked, budget and allowance checked, raises `sponsor_budget_exhausted` and rolls the whole order back). A fully covered order is settled in the same call: paid, entitlement, `order.paid`, `sponsor_entitlement.granted` once, ledger. No payment row, no Paystack call, no payment screen. OQ-328 is resolved: the sponsor share is accepted only when a programme budget covers it.
- **Order effects trigger**: cancelled or failed releases the reservation, paid commits it and emits the event once (a late payment re-commits and opens an incident if the budget moved on), refunded releases it and reverses the receivable. `cancel_sponsor_entitlement` (admin) ends an unused covered entitlement and frees the budget; `request_order_refund` says "covered by sponsor" for a zero order.
- **HMO eligibility**: `hmo-eligibility` edge function (patient JWT) calls `request_hmo_eligibility` AS THE PATIENT (HMO must have an active programme, rate limit, shape check, hash), asks the adapter for that HMO (5 s timeout; throws or hangs mean "unavailable"), records the answer with the service role. The only adapter shipped is `stub`, which connects to nothing, so every check goes to operations. Operations confirm with `ops_verify_hmo_eligibility`, which re-hashes the number they type and must equal the stored hash. A pass lets the person claim items of an HMO programme (cover applies through the quote). No capitation, no HMO billing (I8).
- **Channels (19.2)**: `order-checkout` sends `channels: ["card","bank_transfer","ussd"]` explicitly. The strings are from Paystack's published reference and are proved only against the test double (OQ-401). The return page says "Waiting for your bank" for a pending transfer and keeps polling; the sweep is the safety net.
- **Itemised checkout (19.7)**: `itemiseCheckout` arranges clinician fee, partner fee, Tarragon service fee, quote reductions, the processing estimate and the total, and returns nothing unless the lines sum. A price with no recorded split shows one honest "Price" line.
- **Foreign card (19.4)**: `commerce.processing_fee_estimate` v3 (PROPOSED) adds the international estimate; the checkout shows it as a separate total (and prominently when a supporter pays for someone).
- **OQ-192**: a payer is a Care Circle member holding `pay_for_care`, or a `manage` grantee whose permissions include `manage_payments`. The four older SELECT policies are rewritten from their LIVE text: `profiles_select` (unexpired grant with at least one permission), `booking_requests_select` (`view_appointments` with clinical access), the two vaccination reads (`vaccinations` category). A bare `view` grantee can no longer pay, and no longer reads the booking or vaccination rows.

## 4. Go-live guards (S37 pattern, born OFF)
- `checkout_enabled`: conditions v5_checkout open, an active priced item, Paystack channels tried with the test key (attestation), refund tested (attestation), admin switch. Enforced in `price_quote` and `create_order` (a test pair passes, so the flow can be proved). Closes the OQ-184 checkout gap.
- `sponsor_payment_enabled`: counsel opinion (OQ-07), finance confirmation of 1210 (OQ-403), a real active programme with budget, admin switch. Enforced in the quote and in `request_hmo_eligibility`.
- **Minimal additive change**: `private.go_live_conditions` and `public.attest_go_live_condition` are WRAPPED (renamed `*_base`, a new wrapper answers the two new keys and hands every other key to the base), never replaced wholesale, so S70, S73 and S74 conditions survive. The proof asserts the older guards still answer.

## 5. Merge order
This migration (stamp `20261007223107`, ahead of the clock like S73, OQ-334) must sort AFTER the S70 and S74 migrations (their stamps are at most 220513) and after S73. It does not copy any other function body except the S73 `price_quote_calc`, `price_quote` and `create_order` (the latest) and the live `post_order_to_ledger`; if another branch changes those, rebase this migration on the new definition. The S36b and S37 proofs count guards (now 11 with S71); a branch that adds more guards changes that number. The four OQ-192 policy edits read live text and fail loudly if the clause is gone.

## 6. Screens
Web `/patient/membership`: itemised block, "Covered by {sponsor}" with "Use this cover" (no price, no fee, no code box, no card), part-cover line, budget-run-out note, foreign-card line, "Check my health plan" card; `/patient/membership/paid`: "Waiting for your bank". Mobile: a covered reply opens nothing; the pending notice has the honest title; covered orders show "Covered by ...". Strings in `packages/i18n` (English only, D-14), no em dashes.

## 7. Safety rules
Third-party cost floor and the rule order of S73 are unchanged; sponsor cover never leaves an order below zero; the quote equals the charge (`quote_changed`); a sponsor-only order cannot be paid by a stray webhook (mismatch); a member number never appears in a reply, a log, an event or a table; an adapter failure is never "not eligible"; a decided check never changes; no stored balance anywhere.

## 8. Not built (see OQ-400 to OQ-410)
Admin screens for programmes and the manual HMO queue (audited functions exist and are tested), sponsor invoicing and collection, a real HMO adapter, the mobile quote/itemised/HMO screens, rewiring the legacy per-domain checkouts, a live Paystack proof, instalments (decision S72-2), Access Fund and payment requests (S72).
