# S25b Design: pay the annual Membership in parts

Founder decision, 2026-10-20, explicit: build pay-in-parts for the annual Membership. Access unlocks after the FIRST part and LAPSES if a later part is missed. This brushes INV-09 (no stored balance) and carries credit risk, which the founder has accepted. It reverses S72-2 ("no instalments, reopen only on an explicit ask"; this is that ask). It ships DORMANT behind the platform module `membership_instalments`, which defaults OFF, and nothing here switches it on. Counsel and founder questions: OQ-715 to OQ-718 in `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.

Depends on: S25 (orders, payments, entitlements, Paystack checkout), S26/S26b (refunds, reversal hook), S22b (`patient_memberships`). Migration `20261021102845_s25b_membership_instalments.sql`. Proof `packages/db/tests/s25b_membership_instalments.sql`.

## 1. What it is, and what it is not
- **Is**: one order for the full Membership price, paid as N separate Paystack payments (default 3), each in integer kobo, adding up to the price exactly. The schedule is PROPOSED config (`instalments.membership_schedule`: 3 parts, due at 0, 30 and 60 days after the first part, 5 days of grace, reminders 3 days before and on the day), versioned in `instalment_policy_config` with a drift test against the registry.
- **Is not**: a wallet, a credit line, a stored balance, a card on file or an auto-debit. Nothing is ever charged to the patient by us; the patient pays each part. There is no top-up path. INV-09 is brushed (a patient owes the later parts) and not broken (no balance is held for them).
- **One Membership at a time** still holds: a plan order goes through `create_order`, which refuses a second Membership (`already_member`).
- **Only for the buyer, for themselves, at the plain list price.** No discount code, no Health Points, no sponsor share, no gift. The database refuses any of those on a plan (`instalment_not_available`).

## 2. Data model
- `order_instalment_plans`: one per order. `state`: `awaiting_first | active | lapsed | completed | cancelled`. Snapshot of the schedule that was in force (`schedule`, `schedule_version`).
- `order_instalments`: one per part. `amount_kobo` (immutable), `due_at` (set when the first part is paid), `state`: `pending | paid | cancelled`. A **deferred constraint trigger proves the parts add up to the price exactly** and that there are exactly `parts` of them.
- `order_instalment_attempts`: one row per Paystack reference (`thi_<hex>`). A part may be tried more than once; every reference maps back to its part. Not readable by patients.
- `order_instalment_reminders`: one row per part and step, so a reminder is sent once.
- `refunds.payment_id` and `refunds.refund_group`: see section 6.
- All writes are SECURITY DEFINER functions; patients and admins read plans and parts through RLS (buyer, beneficiary, org admin).

## 3. Flow
1. **Choose** (web): at checkout, when the module is on and the order is eligible, "Pay in full" or "Pay in 3 parts". The plan is shown in plain words first (each part, the days between, what a missed part does, no automatic charge, each part has its own small card fee).
2. **Create**: `create_instalment_order(code, client_key, parts)`. It refuses if the module is off (`instalments_not_open`), if the screen's part count is stale (`instalment_schedule_invalid`), if the item is not a Membership (`instalment_not_available`). Otherwise it calls the ordinary `create_order` in the same transaction (so the dormant gate, price, capacity and "already a member" all apply), checks the order is eligible, and writes the plan and parts. A retry of the same tap returns the same plan.
3. **Pay**: the order-checkout edge function initialises Paystack for the FIRST PART under the part's own reference, with the same metadata (`kind: order`, order id only) every order uses. **The webhook, the return page and the sweeper need no new branch**: `record_order_payment` hands any reference that belongs to a part to `private.record_instalment_payment`.
4. **First part paid**: the order becomes paid, one entitlement is created (`ends_at = paid_at + 365 days`), the membership row is written, every part gets its due date, one `order.paid` event. Access unlocks.
5. **Later parts**: `prepare_instalment_payment(part)` (buyer only, the earliest pending part only, not after the year has ended) returns a reference; order-checkout initialises Paystack for exactly that part. A still-fresh hosted page is reopened rather than duplicated.
6. **Missed part**: the hourly job `private.instalments_hourly()` lapses a plan when a part is unpaid `grace_days` after its due date: plan `lapsed`, entitlement `revoked`, membership ended (`private.end_membership_now`), the patient told once. Nothing is deleted.
7. **Restore**: paying the overdue part (the patient pays it, no one else) restores the entitlement and writes a membership row to the ORIGINAL end date, provided no other part is still overdue beyond its grace and the year has not ended. Paying the last part completes the plan.
8. **Reminders**: 3 days before and on the due day, once each, on the channel `private.patient_reminder_channel()` picks. The text names no service (INV-07).

## 4. Idempotence and money safety
- Every payment is recorded under one lock order: plan, then part, then order. A replayed webhook returns `replay` and changes nothing (proved with three replays, and with the guards removed).
- A wrong amount, currency or fee is a visible `mismatch` payment row plus an ops incident, never a quiet credit. A part paid twice, or paid after its plan was closed, is also a mismatch plus an incident (money to give back), never a second credit.
- A verified payment for the first part that arrives after the order expired is honoured and flagged, exactly as for an ordinary order.
- A plan order's own reference (`tho_...`) is never offered to Paystack. A charge on it anyway (for example a retry under a key that was first used for a plan) is a visible `plan_order_reference` mismatch plus an incident, never a paid order with no ledger entry. The screen also uses a separate retry key for each way of paying.
- Every way an unpaid plan order can close (the sweeper expiring or failing it, the patient cancelling it) closes the plan, its parts and its open attempts together; a part of a closed order cannot be prepared; a retry of a closed plan answers "this order has closed". A verified late first payment still revives it.
- A lapse remembers whether it closed the entitlement (`lapse_revoked`); a restore reopens only an entitlement the lapse closed, never one that was ended for another reason.
- The hourly order catch-up (`post_unposted_orders`) skips plan orders (they post part by part); the part catch-up counts a part done only when an entry exists, newest first.
- Fees: each part is a separate Paystack charge with its own customer fee (OQ-97). `orders.fee_kobo` accumulates the fees of the parts paid; the price is never changed by a fee.

## 5. Ledger (earnings and GL)
- `private.post_order_to_ledger` returns null for a plan order (full price was never received).
- Each part posts `Dr 1020 / Cr 2000` for that part (journal `source_ref = 'orderpart:<payment id>'`, idempotent), with its own straight-line recognition schedule from the day it was paid to the end of the access window, so **revenue is never recognised ahead of cash**. A part paid on the last day of access has no window and is earned at once (Cr 4100). A failed posting raises an incident and the hourly job retries it.
- Doctor earnings: shares are linked per order (`order_encounter_links`); a refund reverses them pro rata, now per paid part (section 6). Whether clinician pay should wait for a part to be paid is OQ-717.

## 6. Refund of a partly paid order (S26b rules, extended)
- The cooling-off rule is unchanged (Membership: `membership_refund_window_days`, counted from the FIRST part). Inside it, `request_order_refund` creates **one refund per paid part** (`refunds.payment_id`), in one group (`refunds.refund_group`; the first row's id is the group id). Each is sent against its own Paystack charge for exactly what that part paid. Parts not yet paid are never charged: approving the group cancels the pending parts and the plan.
- A part paid between the request and the decision is refunded too: approval adds a refund row for every successful payment that has none.
- The admin and the patient see ONE refund of the whole amount (`collapseRefundGroups`), whose state is the least finished of its rows. The patient gets one message per step naming the whole amount: the decision and the sending come from the first row, "your refund has been sent" only when EVERY part is sent, and a part that fails (or is retried) is told from whichever row it happened on, so a failure is never hidden behind a part that succeeded. The weekly reconcile and the send both use each part's own charge.
- The reversal hook is per row: `post_refund_reversal` releases the unrecognised deferred revenue of that part's own schedule; the consultation share reversal is pro rata on the row's amount over the price.
- The `refunds_one_live` index is now on `(order_id, payment_id)`, so a group can hold several live rows while the same part can never be refunded twice.

## 7. What a lapsed or part-paid Member can use (decision)
Membership access is as normal while the plan is not lapsed; **doctor time is not pro-rated by the fraction paid**. A lapsed plan ends the entitlement, so `consume_entitlement` refuses it (no doctor time). One-off doctor-time entitlements follow their existing rules. Whether a Member who has used a lot of doctor time and then stops paying is a loss to be written off is OQ-716.

## 8. Screens
- Patient (web): the choice at checkout (`instalment-choice.tsx`), the plan card with each part, its date, "overdue" and the next part's pay button (`instalment-plans-card.tsx`, rendered nothing at all for a person with no plan, so with the module off the page is unchanged), the receipt list showing what has been paid so far, and the refund dialog quoting what would be returned.
- Admin: `/admin/instalments` (module state, counts by state, money still to come, money overdue, open plans). Switching the module on or off is a superadmin act with a stated reason (`set_platform_module`) and is deliberately not on that page.
- Mobile: NOT built here (the mobile Membership screen is unchanged). The edge function and the RPCs are the same, so a later mobile screen needs no backend work.

## 9. Not built, and why
- Mobile UI (above). Instalments for anything but the yearly Membership. Gifted or sponsored plans. Discounts or points on a plan. A different number of parts at the patient's choice (one proposed schedule, versioned). Automatic retries or card-on-file debits (would be a stored mandate: counsel first). Collections or credit scoring.
