# S54 design note: medicines (2 of 2): pharmacy comparison, quality, chat, dependants

Scope: spec Module 8 rows 8.9 to 8.16 and decisions D2 (patient chooses, take it anywhere) and D5 (no delivery, already carried out by OQ-261). Research: `docs/research/S54.md`. Stacked on S53 (#1002) and on main-dev's S28 as it now stands (S28c plus the OQ-261 delivery removal; the first S28 build #993 is not part of this). Built under "build everything, switch nothing on" (OQ-290).

## 1. What existed (extended, not rebuilt)
S28 (live in the database): the patient chooser, collection code, pharmacist verify and dispense. `pharmacy_medications` with price, `stock_status`, `stock_updated_at` (D1: these are the price and stock columns; no `pharmacy_prices` table). Licence fields on partners and locations. `care_messages` and `start_care_thread`. `private.screen_care_message_for_emergency` (the red-flag phrase list). S08 refill reminders and supply. The pay-through-Tarragon pharmacy order engine, left dormant and untouched.

## 2. Data model (migration `*_s54_pharmacy_price_compare_quality_chat.sql`, independent of S28 objects; `*_s54b_chooser_*` edits S28c's chooser list and check)
- Quality (8.11): `pharmacy_partners` gains `nafdac_source_attested_at/by/note`, set only by `attest_pharmacy_nafdac_source()` (admin or partner manager, audited). `private.pharmacy_partner_listable()` = active, approved, licence verified and in date, attested. A trigger refuses to route a prescription to an unlisted pharmacy through any door, so S28's chooser inherits the rule.
- Verified batch (8.11): `pharmacy_medications.verified_batch/_at/_by`, set only by Tarragon staff and only for a listable pharmacy; the patient reads the flag and when, never who.
- `patient_price_compare(prescription)` (8.9): listable pharmacies that can supply the prescription, exact strength only, ordered by items supplied, stock, total price, name. Reads price, stock and the verified flag; never a commission (its source is checked by a test and the output by the proof).
- Pharmacist chat (8.12): `pharmacist_chat_threads` and `_messages`. Patients read through functions and policies; pharmacists only through audited functions; no staff or admin policy, so a clinician sees nothing. Pharmacist sees the first name, the words and the one medicine the thread names. Emergency phrases flagged (the screen then shows the emergency steps). Notices are generic and never carry the words. Escalation: the pharmacist suggests, the patient chooses to send her own words to the care team through the existing `start_care_thread`.
- Refill (8.10): the refill reminder payload gains the chosen pharmacy id (text stays generic); `medication_refill_pharmacy()` tells the app where to collect.
- D5: nothing here. OQ-261 (S28d) removed the delivery columns, statuses and functions from the database, so S54 adds no pickup-only CHECK (the column it would constrain no longer exists).

## 3. Screens
Web: the S28 collect page shows the comparison above the chooser, "take it to any pharmacy" stays prominent, and the NAFDAC prompt shows at collection; `/patient/pharmacy-chat` and a link from Medications; pharmacist `/pharmacist/messages` with a nav entry (the pharmacist portal has no global search; the page is in the portal nav and header map); admin pharmacies page: NAFDAC-source attestation and a verified-batch tick. Phone: dependants' reminders and catch-up (OQ-70, OQ-76), "collect at X" under a medicine. The phone has no comparison or chat screen yet (OQ-315).

## 4. Safety rules
Nothing here changes a prescription (INV-02). The patient chooses; a clinician never picks a pharmacy and never sees a price ranking or an earning (8.16, proved with a clinician session). Chat text is never in a notification (INV-07) and every pharmacist read is audited (INV-10). A pharmacy cannot mark its own batch. "Batch checked" is never shown as "genuine". No LLM anywhere in this work.

## 5. Acceptance
Duplicate ACE inhibitor warns (S53 tests). Refill reminder fires below the configured days (S08 proof, and S54 proof E1 shows the reminder carries the chosen pharmacy). No delivery: OQ-261's proof, and S54 proof F1 (no delivery column on the order table).

## 6. S54c: the care team suggests a pharmacy, the patient confirms (OQ-310 option b, founder decision 2026-10-07)
Migration `*_s54c_clinician_suggests_pharmacy_patient_confirms.sql`; proof `s54c_clinician_suggests_pharmacy_patient_confirms.sql`.
- **Record**: `prescription_pharmacy_suggestions` (prescription, patient, partner, branch, status pending, accepted, declined, chose_other, withdrawn, lapsed; `recorded_by`, `source`). Client roles can only SELECT, and only the patient's own rows (no staff, pharmacy or finance policy exists). One pending suggestion per prescription; a new one withdraws the old.
- **A suggestion routes nothing.** It never writes `prescriptions.state`, the pharmacy, the code or anything a pharmacy can read, so a pharmacy cannot see it. Accepting calls `patient_choose_pharmacy` itself, so every S28 and S54 rule (listable only, change before supply, new code, audit, notices, events) applies unchanged. A trigger settles the suggestion when the prescription moves by any door: sent to the suggested branch is accepted, sent elsewhere is chose_other, cancelled or dispensed first is lapsed.
- **Clinician** (INV-12, INV-10): `care_team_prescriptions_for_routing`, `care_team_pharmacy_options`, `care_team_suggest_pharmacy`, `care_team_withdraw_pharmacy_suggestion`. Only a clinical-tier clinician tied to the patient (a care coordinator, pharmacist, finance, admin, untied clinician and the patient are refused); the two reads write an audit row, a denial included. What is shown is neutral: pharmacy, branch, place, same city (the branch address names her city, or the pharmacy has one branch in that city) or same state (a tier, OQ-330), whether the medicines on THIS prescription are in stock (yes, no, unknown; exact strength), and verified partner. Ordered by proximity then name; no price, margin, payout or earning is selected or sorted by (a scan test on the function source and a runtime test that swaps the commissions and sees the same order).
- **Patient**: `patient_pharmacy_suggestion`, `patient_accept_pharmacy_suggestion`, `patient_decline_pharmacy_suggestion`. The existing collect page shows "Your care team suggests X" with "Send it to this pharmacy", "Choose another pharmacy" and "No thanks", and still says she can take the signed prescription anywhere. Web only (OQ-331).
- **Notice and event**: one in-app template `pharmacy_suggestion_patient` naming no medicine, condition or pharmacy (INV-07; nothing depends on it being seen); event `prescription.pharmacy_suggested` (prescription id only) through the outbox.
- **Audit load**: the routing read is audited once per chart view; the options read only for a signed prescription with no live suggestion, so a page view does not write an audit row per prescription. A pending suggestion whose pharmacy is delisted, or whose medicine is no longer live, is shown to the clinician as unavailable, not as waiting.
- **Screen**: a "Suggest a pharmacy" card in the Medications tab of the clinician's patient chart (clinical tiers only). No new page, so no new search entry; the chart is already searchable.
- **Expiry (coordinator review)**: `suggestion_valid_days` (PROPOSED, 14) is a new version of the `pharmacy_config` rule (v2, mirrored as `pharmacy.collection_rules` v2 with a drift test). Each suggestion stores its own `expires_at`. An expired one is not shown to the patient, cannot be accepted, and is lapsed to `expired` by the next read or suggest (no timer); the clinician sees "expired" and can suggest again (the patient is told again).
- **Acting for the patient**: S28's `patient_choose_pharmacy` matches only `patient_id = auth.uid()`, so the suggestion is exactly as wide and no wider. A supporter or guardian cannot see or answer it and a dependant's account takes none (OQ-332).
- **Who may suggest**: an explicit allow-list in `private.may_suggest_pharmacy`: account role `clinician`, a clinical doctor tier, and a tie. corporate_admin, hmo_admin, care_coordinator, analyst, lab_liaison and any role added later are refused, even holding a clinical tier row (proved, with a sabotage that removes the role check).
- **Denied-read evidence**: a denied read is audited only for a `clinician`-role account and once an hour per clinician and patient; patient, pharmacist, finance and admin accounts write nothing.
- **One matcher**: `private.pharmacy_item_matches(items)` (name key plus exact normalised strength, listable pharmacies, normalised once) feeds both the clinician's stock flag and S54's `patient_price_compare`, which was re-pointed at it with the same output (the S54 proof still passes).
- **Refresh**: suggest and withdraw return the patient id and the screen revalidates that chart, not the form's claim.
