# S54 design note: medicines (2 of 2): pharmacy comparison, quality, chat, dependants, pickup only

Scope: spec Module 8 rows 8.9 to 8.16 and decisions D2 (patient chooses, take it anywhere) and D5 (no delivery). Research: `docs/research/S54.md`. Stacked on S53 (#1002) and on the live S28 build (#993; main-dev also carries the first S28 build #990, revert #1001 pending, see OQ-312). Built under "build everything, switch nothing on" (OQ-290).

## 1. What existed (extended, not rebuilt)
S28 (live in the database): the patient chooser, collection code, pharmacist verify and dispense. `pharmacy_medications` with price, `stock_status`, `stock_updated_at` (D1: these are the price and stock columns; no `pharmacy_prices` table). Licence fields on partners and locations. `care_messages` and `start_care_thread`. `private.screen_care_message_for_emergency` (the red-flag phrase list). S08 refill reminders and supply. The pay-through-Tarragon pharmacy order engine, left dormant and untouched except for the pickup-only CHECK.

## 2. Data model (migration `*_s54_pharmacy_price_compare_quality_chat_pickup_only.sql`, independent of S28 objects)
- Quality (8.11): `pharmacy_partners` gains `nafdac_source_attested_at/by/note`, set only by `attest_pharmacy_nafdac_source()` (admin or partner manager, audited). `private.pharmacy_partner_listable()` = active, approved, licence verified and in date, attested. A trigger refuses to route a prescription to an unlisted pharmacy through any door, so S28's chooser inherits the rule.
- Verified batch (8.11): `pharmacy_medications.verified_batch/_at/_by`, set only by Tarragon staff and only for a listable pharmacy; the patient reads the flag and when, never who.
- `patient_price_compare(prescription)` (8.9): listable pharmacies that can supply the prescription, exact strength only, ordered by items supplied, stock, total price, name. Reads price, stock and the verified flag; never a commission (its source is checked by a test and the output by the proof).
- Pharmacist chat (8.12): `pharmacist_chat_threads` and `_messages`. Patients read through functions and policies; pharmacists only through audited functions; no staff or admin policy, so a clinician sees nothing. Pharmacist sees the first name, the words and the one medicine the thread names. Emergency phrases flagged (the screen then shows the emergency steps). Notices are generic and never carry the words. Escalation: the pharmacist suggests, the patient chooses to send her own words to the care team through the existing `start_care_thread`.
- Refill (8.10): the refill reminder payload gains the chosen pharmacy id (text stays generic); `medication_refill_pharmacy()` tells the app where to collect.
- D5: `CHECK (fulfilment_method = 'pickup')` on `pharmacy_orders`. Live counts first: 0 orders, 0 non-pickup, 0 couriers, 0 delivery attempts (in the migration header). No table, RPC or template is dropped (OQ-16).

## 3. Screens
Web: the S28 collect page shows the comparison above the chooser, "take it to any pharmacy" stays prominent, and the NAFDAC prompt shows at collection; `/patient/pharmacy-chat` and a link from Medications; pharmacist `/pharmacist/messages` with a nav entry (the pharmacist portal has no global search; the page is in the portal nav and header map); admin pharmacies page: NAFDAC-source attestation and a verified-batch tick; delivery option, address form, delivery fee and courier controls hidden behind `PHARMACY_DELIVERY_ENABLED = false`. Phone: dependants' reminders and catch-up (OQ-70, OQ-76), "collect at X" under a medicine. The phone has no comparison or chat screen yet (OQ-315).

## 4. Safety rules
Nothing here changes a prescription (INV-02). The patient chooses; a clinician never picks a pharmacy and never sees a price ranking or an earning (8.16, proved with a clinician session). Chat text is never in a notification (INV-07) and every pharmacist read is audited (INV-10). A pharmacy cannot mark its own batch. "Batch checked" is never shown as "genuine". No LLM anywhere in this work.

## 5. Acceptance
Duplicate ACE inhibitor warns (S53 tests). Refill reminder fires below the configured days (S08 proof, and S54 proof E1 shows the reminder carries the chosen pharmacy). No delivery: the CHECK, the UI scan test and the fee test.
