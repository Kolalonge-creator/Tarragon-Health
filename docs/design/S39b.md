# S39b Design: staff read patient records only through a care relationship

OQ-260, INV-10, INV-12. Founder decision 2026-10-07: tier 1 and tier 2 together. Builds on S39 (`docs/design/S39.md`).

## 1. Problem
Any active staff member of an organisation (including a care coordinator) could read 226 patient tables with no tie to the patient. A live check showed an untied clinician reading a patient's encounter and HIV and hepatitis status.

## 2. What changed (migration `20261007133517_s39b_tied_staff_reads.sql`)
- `private.staff_may_read(patient, organisation, category)`: a staff member passes only through `private.can_staff_read_clinical` (care team, open escalation or alert, live consultation, active task, post-consult window, assigned referral), an active break-glass grant, or an active support view-as session. A care coordinator never passes on a clinical table. `private.is_org_staff` is not edited.
- `staff_read_scope`: every patient table with a staff read policy is either `tied` (128) or `org` (103, each with a reason). The 128: all clinical records (labs, imaging, ECG, care plans, encounters, serology, mental, sexual and reproductive health, timeline, medication reviews and the like), plus `scribe_transcripts` (through its consent), `wearable_readings`, `risk_assessment_responses`, `prevention_risk_scores`, `vaccination_records`.
- The SELECT policy of each tied table is rewritten (an ALL policy is split so its writes keep the old text). The old text is saved in `staff_read_policy_backup`.
- Kill switch: platform module `tied_staff_reads`. Off gives back the old organisation-wide read at once (`set_platform_module('tied_staff_reads', false, '<why>')`).
- `scribe_transcripts`: reading and the staff delete (used when the patient revokes consent) both need a tie now.
- Eight organisation-level quality and safety views (counts only) move to owner rights with the staff predicate so they keep counting the whole organisation; `patient_care_gaps` lists patients and follows the tie.

## 3. Proof
`s39b_tied_staff_reads.sql` (see its header): all 128 tables, five roles, break-glass, support view, the switch, registry completeness, two sabotages. Every other SQL proof in `ci.manifest` was run with and without the migration to find assumptions about untied reads (see BUILD-PROGRESS).

## 4. Known limits
Writes unchanged (OQ-279); per-read audit only on the core tables (OQ-278); 103 tables left organisation-wide on purpose (OQ-280); the policy calls the tie check per row, which is fine at today's volumes and should be watched.

## 5. Rollout
On from the start. Live today: 4 real patients, 5 real staff and 3 test accounts (checked 2026-10-07), so the blast radius is small and this is the cheapest time to do it. If a staff screen goes empty, turn the module off first and fix the screen second.
