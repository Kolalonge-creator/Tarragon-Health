# S43 design note: Health Passport, record, vaccination, emergency card (spec 2.1 to 2.9)

Invariants in play: INV-02/11 (no unsigned or unconfirmed content in the record), INV-03/04 (released and non-sensitive results only), INV-07 (events carry ids only), INV-10/12 (staff reads through audited, tied functions), INV-13 (`is_test`), INV-14 (guards), INV-16 (versioned config). Research: `docs/research/S43.md`. Founder waiver of the S40 gate recorded in `docs/BUILD-PROGRESS.md`. Migrations were written against `origin/main-dev` and validated on a fresh local `supabase db reset`; **none was applied to production**.

## 1. What already existed (reused, not rebuilt)
`patient_timeline` (append-only spine, vitals and prescription triggers from S09, **already applied to production**; the plan said they were not), `patient_documents` + private bucket (0 rows, no UI anywhere), `family_history` (0 rows, no UI), `record_shares` + lookups (S09, 0 rows), `emergency_cards` + printable card + QR text + mobile offline cache, `vaccination_catalog/records/schedules/schedule_signoffs` + a live daily reminder cron, the three AI extraction pipelines (AI-005/006/012), the audited staff reads `read_patient_chart_audited` and `open_patient_document_audited` (S05), the event bus (S10), go-live guards (S37), the PROPOSED-config registry and its mirror-test pattern.

## 2. Data model (three migrations, `20261007230331`, `...122142`, `...122358`)
- **Timeline:** `patient_timeline.trust_tier` (8 values) set by a BEFORE INSERT trigger from source and a real clinician actor, backfilled (13 rows). Two event types: `symptom_logged`, `procedure_recorded`.
- **Photo capture:** `patient_documents.ocr_state` (pending, suggested, confirmed, rejected, failed), `ocr_text`, `extracted jsonb`, `ocr_model_id`, `ocr_confirmed_at`; CHECKs make a rejected or failed row hold nothing and a confirmed row hold no raw text; a trigger lets only the S43 functions change them.
- **History:** `procedures` (new: source, recorded_by, `verified_by_clinician`, tombstone, `is_test`); `family_history` gains verification and tombstone columns; one write-guard trigger fixes source and verification for a patient writer.
- **Symptoms:** `symptoms.assessment_id`.
- **Sharing:** `record_shares` gains `token_hash`, `pin_hash`, attempts, lock, `max_views`, `has_pin`; `record_share_lookups.outcome`; `record_share_config` (versioned, v1 = 72 h default, 720 h ceiling, 5 PIN tries, 4 digit minimum).
- **Emergency card:** `emergency_card_fields` (eight show flags, lock-screen opt-in, off).
- **Vaccination:** `vaccination_schedule_signoffs.schedule_config` + an UNSIGNED version 2 draft (HPV two doses, typhoid excluded, R21 per-state); `immunisations` view with the spec's names over `vaccination_records`.

## 3. Events (outbox, ids only)
`document.uploaded`, `document.ocr_suggested`, `immunisation.recorded`, `share_link.accessed`. No subscribers are written here.

## 4. Functions and services
- Patient: `confirm_document_extraction`, `reject_document_extraction`, `remove_history_item`, `create_record_share(sections, hours?, pin?, max_views?)`, `revoke_record_share`, `patient_biomarker_list/trend`, `patient_symptom_journal`, `vaccination_schedule_status`.
- Service role only: `record_document_suggestion` (the one writer of the reading columns; fails closed behind the guard).
- Staff, audited and tied: `clinician_record_procedure`, `verify_history_item`, `read_patient_history_audited`. A refusal returns `denied` (it does not raise) so the denial's audit row survives.
- Anon: `record_share_open(token, pin?, commit)` (status: ok, ready for a preview, gone with reason, pin_required, pin_wrong, locked, not_found; a GET passes commit false and spends no view) and the unchanged-name wrappers `record_share_by_token` and `emergency_card_by_token`.
- Web: `lib/document-capture` (reading through `runGovernedAi`, AI-018), `/share/[token]` as a route handler answering 200/401/404/410/423 with a no-script CSP, the doctor summary PDF (`?variant=doctor`).
- **Why the card and reminder functions are renamed, not rewritten:** `emergency_card_by_token` and `queue_vaccination_reminders` are renamed and wrapped, so whatever body is live (including hardening from branches not yet on main-dev) is kept exactly and the renamed original cannot be called by a client.

## 5. Screens
Web: `/patient/documents` (camera or file, per-field confirm, original photo kept), `/patient/biomarkers`, `/patient/health-history`, `/patient/symptom-journal`, share controls with PIN, view cap, one-time link and QR and an attempts list, emergency card field chooser (+ lock-screen opt-in), timeline trust-tier line, passport nav and the facility summary link. Mobile: trust-tier line on the timeline; the offline emergency card honours the chosen fields. **Not built:** mobile screens for capture, trends, history, share; in-app crop; the lock-screen widget (native build).

## 6. Safety rules (each has a test)
Suggestions are never the record; a rejected reading leaves no values; nothing outside the capture module may read `patient_documents` (scan test); reminders pause until a signed schedule with a config is active; an expired, revoked or capped link answers 410 and logs the attempt, and a plain GET (a link preview) spends no view and shows no record; mental health and reproductive health have no share section (items inside the medicines, conditions and results sections are not classified by what they are for: OQ-S43-4); a held, withdrawn or sensitive-positive result never appears in a share, trend or summary; a target is only ever "your care team's"; a detail the patient hid from the emergency card is shown as "not shared by the patient" on every surface (page, QR text, mobile), never as "None recorded".

## 7. Go-live guard
`document_capture_enabled` (off; cannot be switched on until its conditions are added to `private.go_live_conditions`, which I did not restate because that function is changed by several branches). The reminder gate is the signed schedule itself. Share PIN, expiry and view cap need no guard (they only narrow access).

## 8. Seams for S44 and later
`external_records`, FHIR export and import, the Nigeria Core mappings and partner-lab push are S44. `procedures`, `immunisations`, `patient_documents.extracted` are the tables S44 maps. The schedule generator still reads the old catalogue ages (see the open questions).
