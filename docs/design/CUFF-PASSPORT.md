# Cuff display camera reader and result passport

Items 6 and 10 of the S51-S55 patient tools batch (2026-10-22). Both are dormant behind go-live guards born OFF. This note records what was reused and what was added.

## Item 6: camera capture of a blood pressure cuff display
- Reused: S70a photo screen (`apps/mobile/src/screens/photo-reading-screen.tsx`), `draftFromRecognisedText`, `finalisePhotoReading`, `POST /api/mobile/photo-readings`, `vital_source.photo_confirmed`, `classifyPlausibility` and its `devices.plausibility` ranges, the AI-007 pack reader's stateless pattern and `runGovernedAi()`.
- Added: AI-022 and `POST /api/mobile/cuff-display-proposal`. The server returns numbers as lines of text; the phone feeds them to the same `draftFromRecognisedText` any recogniser would. The person sees the photo beside editable boxes and must tick every number. Nothing is stored by the proposal.
- Refusals are deterministic: no display or low confidence, a missing top or bottom number, a top number not above the bottom, a value outside the physiological ranges. All ask the person to retake or type.
- No enum change. The saved reading is `photo_confirmed` and takes the same red-flag path as a typed one (the S70a route is untouched).

## Item 10: result passport
- Reused: the visit summary PDF builder and route (section choice, audit row, no verdict language), the share links (time limit, PIN, view cap, revoke, patient-visible access log) and the sensitive-positive row level security.
- Added: `glucose` and `lab_results` sections, the guard, a page, a bearer route and a phone card that keeps an offline copy.
- Rules kept: patient-initiated only (a POST from the person, never a page view); the person chooses sections; sensitive positive results and reproductive records are never offered; the PDF says it was generated by the patient on a date; no "reviewed by" line is printed because no real reviewer is recorded on a lab reading.
