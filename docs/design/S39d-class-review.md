# S39d: registry class review (OQ-284)

For the CMO and the DPO. `public.data_registry` holds 302 patient tables, each with a retention class that was assigned by table name and is marked `reviewed = false`. Mark each class confirmed or corrected; the correction goes in a migration. Periods (PROPOSED, counsel to confirm, OQ-283): clinical record 8 years after last contact, mental health 20, maternity 25, consent relationship plus 6, financial 6, access log 8, operational 90 days to 2 years. Nothing is deleted automatically.

## 1. Most likely to be wrong (look at these first)

| Table | Now | Suggest | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| sti_partner_notifications | operational | clinical_record | Sexual health partner notification is clinical and sensitive, not a delivery log. |
| risk_reassessment_queue | operational | clinical_record | A clinical work queue that records a risk decision. |
| medication_dose_reminders, medication_refill_reminder_rules | operational | clinical_record or keep operational | Reminder settings are operational, but they show a medication history. Decide which. |
| vitals_reminder_rules, vitals_reminder_state | operational | operational | Probably right. Confirm. |
| wellness_points_ledger | financial | operational | Loyalty points, not money. |
| care_access_events | consent | audit | An access event log, not a consent record. 8 years instead of relationship plus 6. |
| wellbeing_checkins, wellbeing_checkin_preferences | mental_health | clinical_record | Self tracking. 20 years may be longer than needed. |
| invoices, payment_fraud_signals | financial | financial | Probably right. Only 3 tables carry the financial class because most payment tables have no patient_id column, so they are outside this registry. |
