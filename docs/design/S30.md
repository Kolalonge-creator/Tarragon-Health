# S30 Design: fee schedules and the earnings ledger

Spec 7.7 (and rows for fees in Section 17). Depends on S17 (done, merged). Invariants touched: INV-15 (integer kobo), INV-16 (every earning records the fee version it used), INV-13 (`is_test` is carried and excluded), INV-07 (nothing clinical in a notice). Founder decisions applied: F-03 (employed doctors are paid by salary, so they get no ledger line; `employment_type` decides the path), D-11 (fixed fee per task type; consultation share configurable), D-09 (no tax calculated). Research: `docs/research/S30.md`. Money moves in S31, not here.

## 1. What already exists (extended, not rebuilt)
- `clinical_tasks.fee_kobo_at_completion` and `fee_schedule_version_id` (S16, empty, no foreign key): filled and given the foreign key.
- `public.queue_summary()` already returns `next_fee_kobo: null` (S17): filled for contracted clinicians.
- `availability_blocks.minimum_guarantee_eligible` (S17/S18, a flag only), `on_call_rota` (S18), `lead_assignments` (S18), `encounters` (S21): read as the sources of earnings.
- The September `analytics_doctor_income` and `analytics_doctor_paid_jobs` views report what a doctor earned the business. They say they calculate no commission. They stay as reports; this ledger is what the business owes. No overlap, no change.
- Not present: fee schedules, a ledger, a payout. `payouts`, bank verification and Paystack transfers are S31.

## 2. Data model (one migration `*_s30_fee_schedules_and_earnings_ledger.sql`)
- `fee_schedules`: `version`, `status` (`draft`, `approved`, `superseded`), `items jsonb`, `created_by`, `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `superseded_at`. One approved row at a time. A draft is edited freely by an admin; an approved row can never change (a trigger), a mistake is a new version. No schedule is seeded in migrations (the founder sets amounts in the admin console; zeros are for staging and tests only).
- `items` (validated by the database): `task_types.<code> = { base_fee_kobo, wait_multiplier_steps: [{at_pct, add_pct}] }`, `on_call_shift_fee_kobo`, `lead_fee_per_patient_month_kobo`, `consultation_share_pct` (`video`, `audio`, `phone`), optional `consultation_reference_price_kobo` per type, `pilot_minimum_per_declared_hour_kobo`.
- `earnings_config` (versioned, PROPOSED, mirrored as `earnings.rules`): lead month needs 15 active days, backup on-call fee 0 percent, which ledger kinds count toward the pilot minimum.
- `earnings_ledger` (append only): `clinician_id`, `kind` (`task`, `consultation`, `on_call_shift`, `lead_month`, `minimum_topup`, `adjustment`), `reference_type`/`reference_id`, `amount_kobo bigint`, `fee_schedule_version_id`, `calculation jsonb` (every input, so a clinician and an auditor can recompute it), `earned_at`, `employment_type`, `payout_id` (null until S31; the one column that may change, once, from null), `is_test`. Unique per clinician, kind and reference, so a retry never pays twice.

## 3. Calculations (pure code in `packages/queue/fees.ts`, same cases in SQL)
- Task fee = `base_fee_kobo` + `floor(base x add_pct / 100)`, where `add_pct` is the step with the highest `at_pct` not above the share of the due window that had passed **when the clinician claimed it** (100 means the due time was reached). Not cumulative. Read at claim time, so working slowly never pays more.
- Consultation share = `floor(price x pct / 100)` on a consultation the clinician delivered (`video`, `audio`, `phone`). Price is the paid purchase amount, else the schedule's reference price, else a visible zero line flagged for review. Async questions are paid as the `async_question` task, not twice.
- Lead month: the lead fee for each patient the clinician led for at least 15 days of a Lagos calendar month.
- On-call shift: the shift fee to the primary (and to the backup at the configured percent, default 0).
- Pilot minimum: for each merged run of confirmed, eligible declared hours, `floor(rate x hours)` minus what the ledger already shows for that run, if positive.
- Adjustment: admin only, a reason, and a request id so a double click posts once; positive or negative; can point at the line it corrects.
All integer arithmetic, rounded down to the kobo. The fee schedule used is the one in force at the moment earned; if none existed then, the first approved schedule applies and the line says so.

## 4. Where lines are written
- A task: an AFTER UPDATE trigger on `clinical_tasks` when it becomes `completed`, so every completion path (S17, S22, S24) is covered without editing them. A failure never blocks a completion: it is caught, a Sentry-visible ops incident is raised once, and the hourly catch-up posts it.
- A consultation: a trigger on `encounters` becoming `completed` (video, audio, phone).
- Shifts, lead months, minimum top-ups and catch-up: `pg_cron` jobs, idempotent by the unique key.
- Employed clinicians: nothing written; the task keeps null fee columns. Contracted clinicians only.

## 5. Screens
- Admin: `/admin/earnings` (status, the schedule form, approve or discard a draft, lines needing review, who has earned what, adjustments). Admin lives in `apps/web` until extracted (S01d).
- Clinician: `/clinician/earnings` (statement and every ledger line with its inputs, for a contracted clinician).
- Both are in the console search index (standing rule). Staff strings are inline English like every other staff page (OQ-183). The queue screen (S35) shows `next_fee_kobo` from `queue_summary`.

## 6. Safety rules
1. Money is `bigint` kobo everywhere; a CHECK refuses negatives except adjustments.
2. The ledger cannot be updated, deleted or truncated, even by an admin; `payout_id` can be set once.
3. No pay changes without a person: nothing infers a fee from reliability, audits or speed beyond the written step.
4. A line never contains a patient name, reading or condition (INV-07); the clinician sees task type and date only.
5. A missing fee or price is a visible flagged zero line, never a silent skip. A missing schedule defers the line and the backlog is shown to admins.
6. Test clinicians' lines carry `is_test` and every summary excludes them (INV-13).
