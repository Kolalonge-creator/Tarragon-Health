## 2. Decisions that came from the research

- **Adherence formula** (docs/research/S08.md section 2): doses marked taken (on time or late) over doses due in the last 7 Lagos days; as-needed excluded; skipped and "could not get it" count against but are reported apart from missed; no percentage under 3 due doses; a medicine added at noon owes no 08:00 dose. Labelled "doses you marked taken", never a clinical proportion of days covered. 80 percent over 7 days stays the existing `adherence.threshold` (a display line for the care team, not a patient grade).
- **Missed has two clocks.** On the phone a dose with no answer reads "no record yet" two hours after it was due (`reminders.behaviour.missedAfterMinutes`). The server writes its own `missed` row after 12 hours (`medicine_config.server_missed_after_minutes`), so a phone that was offline has time to sync a "taken" first. Both PROPOSED, in versioned configuration.
- **A person's row beats the server's `missed`.** A dose logged offline at 08:00 and synced at 14:00 has an earlier `logged_at` than the server's own `missed` row, and the view picked the newest. `medication_logs_latest_per_slot` and `evaluate_adherence_escalation` now rank a server-written (`source = 'system'`) `missed` row below any other row for the slot. The same rule is in `effectiveLog()` in the package. The proof sabotages it.
- **Reminders** (docs/research/S08.md section 3): the Today list is the source of truth; notifications are a convenience. A rolling plan of at most `reminders.behaviour.maxPending` (60) notifications inside `horizonDays` (14), under the 64 iOS allows, rebuilt on launch, on foreground, after any dose is logged, and in the background task. Doses due in the same minute share one notification. Wording is keyed and generic (INV-07): "Time for your care plan check." The health check lists what may be wrong (notifications off, nothing scheduled, plan stale, a maker known to stop background apps) and offers a test reminder and the phone settings.
- **Undo, not edit-in-place.** Logs are append-only. A just-logged dose is held on the phone for `undoSeconds` (120) before it is sent, so Undo genuinely takes it back; after that, "Change" appends a new row and the newest wins.
- **Pill count is its own table**, so the prescribed `medications` row is never touched (INV-02). The server stamps the count time, so a count cannot be backdated; the estimate counts doses taken since the count and walks the schedule forward.

## 3. Data model

New: `medicine_config` (one active row), `medication_supply` (RLS: self, acting guardian, medicines grant; no staff policy). Changed: `medications.schedule_spec jsonb` (shape CHECK); `medication_logs_latest_per_slot` (ordering); `clinical_rule_event_type` +3 values. New private functions: `medication_slots_on` (the SQL schedule expansion), `medication_supply_status`, `weekly_adherence`, `mark_overdue_doses_missed`, `emit_adherence_signals`, `emit_dose_recorded_event`, `medication_supply_guard`. New RPC: `public.medication_weekly_adherence(p_patient, p_reason)`. Replaced: `queue_medication_dose_reminders`, `queue_medication_refill_reminders`, `evaluate_adherence_escalation`. Cron: `medicine-missed-dose-job-every-15-min`, `medicine-adherence-signals-daily`. `dose_events` is still the view over `medication_logs`; `pending` is computed, never stored (OQ-56).

## 4. Events

| Event | Written by | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `dose.recorded` | trigger on `medication_logs` insert, people only | `medication_dose_recorded` |
| `dose.missed` | `mark_overdue_doses_missed` | `medication_dose_missed` (existed) |
| `refill.due` | `queue_medication_refill_reminders` | `medication_refill_due` |
| adherence below threshold | `emit_adherence_signals`, once per patient per week | `medication_adherence_low` |

All go to `clinical_rule_events` (the table the existing rules engine reads). S10's outbox will move delivery; S11 and S12 own what a signal triggers. None of them changes a medicine, a dose or a schedule.

## 5. Where the logic lives

`packages/medicines` (pure, no dependencies, 100 percent branch coverage enforced by its Jest config): Lagos date maths, schedule parse and expansion, dose state, adherence, supply, reminder planning and the reminder health rules. SQL twins (`medication_slots_on`, `weekly_adherence`, `medication_supply_status`) carry the same cases as `schedule.fixtures.ts`; the proof repeats them.

## 6. Screens

Mobile: Medications (today's doses with Taken / Earlier / Skip / Later, undo, weekly card, reminder health card) and the cabinet (schedule choices, pill count and countdown, dose lock note). Web: the clinician chart shows weekly adherence above the dose log; the patient checklist reads the structured schedule.
