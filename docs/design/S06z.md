# S06z design note: My phones

Closes risk (c) in `docs/design/S06x.md` section 4: the patient-facing screen that shows which phones hold their data and lets them remove one. Builds on S06x (leases, remote wipe) and follows the S09x `/patient/my-health` hub pattern.

## What exists, what was added

| Need | Found on main-dev | S06z |
|---|---|---|
| List my phones | `offline_device_leases` with select policy `account_id = auth.uid()` | Read directly, parsed with Zod (`packages/shared/src/my-phones.ts`); bad rows are counted, not guessed |
| Ask to remove one of my phones | `request_offline_device_wipe(p_device, p_reason)` already allows the owner for their own phone (audited) | **No new RPC.** Reused as is |
| Friendly name, "shared phone" | Nothing | Two additive columns (`device_label` max 40, `shared_phone` default false) and `update_my_offline_device`, owner only, refused once a phone is removed, audit row records that a rename happened, never the name. Proof `packages/db/tests/s06z_my_phones.sql` |
| Phone model | Not stored | Not added: needs a native module or a client-supplied string we could not trust. The patient names the phone; the default is "iPhone" or "Android phone" |

## Screens

- Web `/patient/my-health/my-phones` (card on the My Health hub, no new top nav item). The web cannot tell which phone the browser is, so it never says "this phone".
- Phone: section `myPhones` (row on the My Health screen). Knows its own device id, so it marks "This phone".
- Each phone: name, "This phone", "Shared phone", last seen, lease end (the server's value, renews on connect), status (in use / not connected for a while / waiting to be cleared / cleared with the counts it reported).
- Actions: rename, shared toggle, remove.
- Empty, offline (phone: from a failed fetch, with a retry and a calm line) and load-failed states are distinct from "no phones".
- A supporter or proxy acting for someone sees only the one-line "this list is for your own account" and no query is made. A patient never sees another person's phone (own-rows policy, plus the screens refuse while acting).

## Remove, in plain words (S06x rule)

1. The confirmation explains: the phone clears itself and signs out the next time it connects; it makes one last upload attempt first; anything marked urgent is never dropped quietly, but because the patient asked, what still cannot be sent is cleared with the phone and only a count is kept.
2. On the phone being removed, the app flushes the outbox first, then shows the real unsent and urgent counts before the patient confirms. Confirming sends the request and runs a check-in at once, which wipes (`wipeThisPhone` makes its own last attempt too).
3. For another phone, the request is recorded; that phone acts on its next check-in. The wiped phone later reports counts; the list shows them ("N entries had not been sent").
4. A failed request changes nothing and says so. Nothing here is silent.
