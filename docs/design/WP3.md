# WP3 design note: cascade of care and the selection-bias panel

2026-10-23. Builds on S75 (`docs/design/S75.md`, `docs/design/S75-outcome-measures.md`). One migration: `20261023175612_wp3_cascade_of_care_and_selection_panel.sql`. Proof: `packages/db/tests/wp3_cascade_of_care_and_selection_panel.sql`.

## What is reused, not rebuilt
The single minimum-cell rule (`private.min_cell_floor`, `suppress_cell`, `suppress_difference`, `suppress_groups`), `private.outcome_cohort_json` (strict rate, completers rate, missing share), `private.v_bp_cohort_day` (day 90 and 180 control), `private.sponsor_agreed_members` (only members who agreed), `private.sponsor_pack_build` and its causal-wording refusal in the database and again in the app, the audited admin and CMO gate, `/admin/outcomes`, the sponsor page and the pack route (the pack flattens every leaf, so the new sections reach the CSV and PDF with no change to the route).

## 1. Cascade of care
Stages (config `outcome_cascade_config`, mirrored as `outcomes.cascade_rules`): enrolled, screened, diagnosed, on a recorded medicine, controlled. All counted from real tables.
- enrolled: adult (18+, date of birth known) with a joining date (`private.outcome_anchor`), active, not a test account (INV-13), not pregnant.
- screened: hypertension, a cleared blood pressure reading; diabetes, a cleared glucose reading or an HbA1c result. It is a share of enrolled Members, not population screening coverage.
- diagnosed: an active care plan for the condition, or a problem-list entry with the ICD-10 prefix and an active, controlled or uncontrolled status. Includes people with no reading yet.
- on treatment: diagnosed, and an active, current medicine recorded under that care plan. The medicine class is not held, so any medicine under the plan counts.
- controlled: on treatment and the average of the cleared home readings in the last 7 days up to the day the report is run (Lagos), from at least 3 readings in those 7 days, under 140/90; diabetes, the most recent HbA1c in the last 180 days under 7.0 percent. The window ends on the report day, never on the person's own last reading, so a Member whose last readings are weeks old is UNKNOWN. Anyone on treatment without a readable value in the window is UNKNOWN: kept in the figure, never "uncontrolled", never dropped.
- Which readings count (WP3 code only, `private.wp3_reading_usable`): `validation_status = 'valid'`, or `requires_validation` with a non-empty flag list that is only `insufficient_context` (arm or position not recorded; the value is not in doubt). An empty flag list does not count: an empty array is a subset of every array, so the looser form admits any reading whatever its status. S38 and S75 snapshots still use the looser form (OQ-WP3-04); they were not edited.
- Differences from the S38 reports: S38 judges each person against their own home target (else 140/90) at fixed days after joining; the cascade uses the fixed line, as at the report day. Every output says so (`difference_from_bp_outcome_reports`). This is the HEDIS CBP and national programme threshold.
- Not available, stated in every output: population screening coverage, undiagnosed raised blood pressure or glucose, prevalence.
- Suppression: the enrolled count must pass `suppress_cell`; each later stage must pass it and its gap to its parent must not be 1 to minimum-1 people (`suppress_difference`), else it is withheld and its children with it; the control split goes through `suppress_groups` (zero is shown as zero). Months of joining go through `suppress_groups` on their enrolled counts.
- Governance: config born unsigned and inactive, immutable, only `sign_outcome_cascade_config` (CMO) activates it; until then every function returns `awaiting_cmo_signature`. Hub line `cascade_config` and inline panel on `/clinician/clinical-signoff`.

## 2. Selection-bias panel
For a sponsor programme: who is in the figures against who is not, on age band at joining, sex (not recorded is a category), baseline blood pressure band (day-0 average; "no baseline average" is a category) and how they joined (membership source). Two comparisons, both INTERNAL ONLY (admin view): in the figures against other adult Members who are not in the programme, and in the figures against members who joined but did not agree. The sponsor pack carries neither (both say `not_shown_to_sponsors`): the second because those members declined to share, the first because it describes Members outside the programme who agreed to nothing with this sponsor. The pack's panel carries only who is in the figures, seats, and the intent-to-treat view beside the completers. OQ-WP3-05 records the question if the founder wants the first shown to sponsors. The eligible roster is "not available"; seats are shown labelled as seats. Intent to treat (everyone who joined; missing counted as not controlled, shown as missing, split into membership ended and still a Member) beside the completers rate, at day 90 and day 180. Fixed note: descriptive, not causal. No per-clinician view: the proof scans keys and function bodies.

## 3. Roles and screens
Admin and the active CMO (as the existing S75 pack functions). The CMO account is `clinician` and cannot open `/admin/*`, so the cascade has a second page at `/clinician/outcomes/cascade` (gate `canAssignCases`, the `team-caseload` pattern) that renders the same shared component (`components/outcomes/cascade-reports.tsx`); it has a nav entry and search entries. The selection panel stays on the admin sponsor page only (the CMO can read it through the RPC but has no screen for it yet). Sponsor staff, clinicians, patients and anon are refused (invariant I9: institutions get aggregate-only access through the pack, never individuals).

## 4. Implementation notes (review fixes)
- No temporary tables: the cascade and the panel builders aggregate in one query (a rollup for the months; JSON arrays for the attribute groups), are `STABLE`, and so also run in a read-only transaction. The proof scans for `create temp` and checks `provolatile`.
- Drift risk: `private.sponsor_pack_build` is replaced here with `create or replace`, copying the S75c body (`20261007220513`) and adding only the cascade and panel keys (the existing `suppress_cell` calls were left as they were). Any later change to the S75c body in another migration must be merged into this one, or this one will silently undo it. The proof checks that every S75c key is still in the pack. At the time of writing nothing else on `origin/main-dev` redefines it (checked after rebasing onto f32cae87f).
- Performance: the report is O(members): it evaluates every Member's stages with correlated subqueries on each run. Before the 25,000-Member stage it should move to a nightly snapshot (as the S38 outcome snapshots do), with the report reading the snapshot. The per-person checks are cheap at pilot size and were not optimised.
