## Principle

The ladder only ever gets faster or safer. The 5 and 10 minute timers are unchanged (`paging.rules` v1). INV-05 (a red event never waits), INV-06, INV-07 (fixed neutral notices) and INV-12 (chart access only after acknowledging an open page) all still hold; INV-12 is tightened. No config was signed or activated: the new values are PROPOSED (owner CMO), seeded as `paging_delivery_config` v1 the same way `paging_config` v1 and `free_red_config` v1 were, and mirrored in the registry as `paging.delivery_rules`.

## Known limits

- Delivered receipts are reported by the client (the console, or the service worker through `/api/paging/displayed`). A clinician could call the RPC themself, so a receipt is evidence of a working route, not proof against a determined bypass of their own readiness check. A native (Expo) receipt does not exist yet, so a clinician who is only reachable through the mobile app cannot pass the test-page gate until the app records receipts (the clinician page surface is web only today). Their pages are not advanced early either (they report no receipts).
- A bulk upload of many out-of-band blood pressure readings produces many urgent events and nudges; only in-band readings are protected from that.
- `care_team_notice` (S18 patient notices) now reaches the patient's email for the first time (it used to fail with "no email address"). It is treated as patient mail for quiet hours and discreet wording. Confirm that these patient notices are wanted by email.

6. A merged repeat event is silent to the on-call clinician when the family is still unacknowledged (they are already being alerted). Is that acceptable, or should an unacknowledged family be re-sent?
