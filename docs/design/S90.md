# S90 design note: the governed care-gap rule engine (first slice)

2026-10-27. Research: `docs/research/S85-S90-competitor-gap-analysis-2026-10-22.md` section 3.6. Values: `docs/plans/S88-S100-decisions-and-cmo-signoff-pack.md` items 4.2, 4.5, 4.6. Migrations: `20261027175129_s90_care_gap_outreach_trigger_values.sql` (four enum labels, its own transaction) and `20261027180057_s90_care_gap_rule_engine.sql`. Proof: `packages/db/tests/s90_care_gap_rule_engine.sql`. Born OFF and UNSIGNED; nothing here signs or switches anything.

## Reused, not rebuilt
`patient_care_gaps` and `private.queue_care_outreach()` are untouched (the new types sit beside them). Tasks are `care_outreach_tasks` rows (one live per patient and trigger, assigned to the gap owner); the call log is the existing `care_outreach_contacts`. Owners come from `private.clinician_on_leave`, `private.clinician_deprioritised_now` and `private.pick_employed_clinician_competent`. The adherence rule reads the P6 `adherence.below_line` domain event; the trend rule reads `private.bp_window_stats` (so it follows the shared usable-reading rule). Channels use `private.patient_reminder_channel(patient, false)`. Small cells use `private.min_cell_floor`, `suppress_cell`, `suppress_groups`. The guard follows S37 (anchored patch of `private.go_live_conditions`, fails loudly); the config follows WP3 (`care_gap_rule_config`, immutable, CMO-only `sign_care_gap_rule_config`, `refuse_superseded_activation`, registry key `care_gaps.rules` with a mirror test, hub line `care_gap_config` with an inline panel).

## Three layers of off
1. Guard `care_gap_engine_enabled` OFF (born so): `care_gap_evaluate` and `care_gap_outreach_run` return at once. 2. Guard ON, config unsigned: **shadow mode**. Gaps are recorded with `shadow = true` and an owner so staff can see them; no patient is messaged, no task is made, no attempt row is written. 3. Guard ON, config signed: live. Signing never flips the guard; the guard never signs.

## The six rules (config v1, all PROPOSED)
`missed_reading` (hypertension 14 days: the pack's 7-day uncontrolled cadence plus the 7-day overdue allowance; a plan with no reading counts from the plan start). `no_hba1c_12_months`, `no_lipids_12_months` (365 days plus the pack's 30-day lab overdue line; a new plan counts from its start). `medication_refill_lapse` (7 days past the refill date; **not in the pack**, flagged). `adherence_lapse` (the P6 signal within 10 days). `rising_bp_trend` (14-day windows, 3 readings each). **Two numbers are deliberately null and their rules inert**: a diabetes home-glucose cadence and the systolic rise that makes a trend. The engine invents no clinical number; the CMO panel says "not supplied". Only Members with an active hypertension or diabetes plan, not minors.

## The record and the ledger
`care_gaps`: type, due date, owner, status, `closure_reason` (closed | declined | unreachable | wrong), `closed_by_signal` (the reading, lab or medicine row, or a recovered line). A CHECK makes `closed` impossible without a signal; `close_care_gap_manual` refuses `closed`, needs a note, and refuses `unreachable` before the configured logged staff attempts. A message sent or opened is never read as a signal. A closed row never changes (trigger); a gap closed by a person is not reopened for 30 days. The `why` column holds a criterion code, its line, a day count and a date, never a reading or a result, so the coordinator queue stays logistics. Staff read it only as the assigned owner or through the S39b tie (`private.care_gap_visible`); a patient, an untied coordinator, an untied clinician and an institution read nothing. No write grant.

## The ladder
Push (skipped, not counted, when there is no subscription), in-app card, email, then a coordinator call task; 3 days between steps; weekly cap of 2 sent messages per patient across all gaps; no send 20:00 to 08:00 Lagos; a capped step is recorded and retried the next day. A judgement item (trend) has no patient step and no coordinator task: it is owned by a doctor and shows on the doctor's care gap list. Copy is two neutral templates (`care_gap_checkin`, `care_gap_checkin_followup`), payload carries only `copy_variant`; the wording lives in `packages/i18n/src/care-gap-copy.ts` and a test pins it to the sender, the in-app preview and the database rows. A/B scaffolding (copy variant, send hour) is config with `ab.enabled = false`; each send records the variant and, 7 days later, whether the gap closed by a signal.

## Staff and institution views
"Why this gap is open" panel, ranking (risk, days overdue, trend), next attempt, contact log, log-attempt and close forms on both outreach worklists. Institutions: `care_gap_institution_summary` (service role only) counts distinct people by type, open and closed in 90 days from the ledger, test accounts and shadow gaps excluded; a small cell is withheld with a second cell, totals are withheld whenever a cell is, and a filter only selects published cells (proved under all 63 filter combinations, in SQL and again in the TS parser). The old closed-in-90-days approximation is removed.

## Not built (first slice)
Silent-patient re-engagement as its own trigger, the patient "today" card, outcome nudges from a patient's own trend, any ML. Doctor-owned gaps are not mirrored into `care_tasks`. Employer 50-person floor not wired. See OQ-S90-01 to -06.
