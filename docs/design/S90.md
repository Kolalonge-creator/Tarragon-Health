# S90 design note: the governed care-gap rule engine (first slice)

2026-10-27. Research: `docs/research/S85-S90-competitor-gap-analysis-2026-10-22.md` section 3.6. Values: `docs/plans/S88-S100-decisions-and-cmo-signoff-pack.md` items 4.2, 4.5, 4.6. Migrations: `20261027175129_s90_care_gap_outreach_trigger_values.sql` (four enum labels, its own transaction) and `20261027180057_s90_care_gap_rule_engine.sql`. Proof: `packages/db/tests/s90_care_gap_rule_engine.sql`. Born OFF and UNSIGNED; nothing here signs or switches anything.

## Reused, not rebuilt
`patient_care_gaps` and `private.queue_care_outreach()` are untouched (the new types sit beside them). Tasks are `care_outreach_tasks` rows (one live per patient and trigger, assigned to the gap owner); the call log is the existing `care_outreach_contacts`. Owners come from `private.clinician_on_leave`, `private.clinician_deprioritised_now` and an eligibility list copied from `private.pick_employed_clinician_competent` (see Owners below). The adherence rule reads the P6 `adherence.below_line` domain event; the trend rule reads `private.bp_window_stats`, and the missed-reading rule and every closing signal read blood pressure through `private.care_gap_last_usable_bp`, which applies exactly the same usable-reading rule (diagnostic view, both numbers, no pharmacy-machine entry, `bp_reading_usable`). Channels use `private.patient_reminder_channel(patient, false)`. Small cells use `private.min_cell_floor`, `suppress_cell`, `suppress_groups`. The guard follows S37 (anchored patch of `private.go_live_conditions`, fails loudly); the config follows WP3 (`care_gap_rule_config`, immutable, CMO-only `sign_care_gap_rule_config`, `refuse_superseded_activation`, registry key `care_gaps.rules` with a mirror test, hub line `care_gap_config` with an inline panel).

## Three layers of off
1. Guard `care_gap_engine_enabled` OFF (born so): `care_gap_evaluate` and `care_gap_outreach_run` return at once. 2. Guard ON, config unsigned: **shadow mode**. Gaps are recorded with `shadow = true` and an owner so staff can see them; no patient is messaged, no task is made, no attempt row is written. 3. Guard ON, config signed: live. Signing never flips the guard; the guard never signs.

## The six rules (config v1, all PROPOSED)
`missed_reading` (hypertension 14 days: the pack's 7-day uncontrolled cadence plus the 7-day overdue allowance; a plan with no reading counts from the plan start). `no_hba1c_12_months`, `no_lipids_12_months` (due on the last matching result, or with none on file the plan start, plus 365 days, and opened after the pack's 30-day lab overdue line; a new plan is not flagged in its first year). `medication_refill_lapse` (7 days past the refill date; **not in the pack**, flagged). `adherence_lapse` (the P6 signal within 10 days, and the adherence recomputed over the patient's hypertension and diabetes plan medicines only, so a contraceptive or a mental health medicine can neither raise nor hold it; the line P6 used is recorded on the gap and the gap closes against that line). `rising_bp_trend` (14-day windows, 3 readings each). **Two numbers are deliberately null and their rules inert**: a diabetes home-glucose cadence and the systolic rise that makes a trend. The engine invents no clinical number; the CMO panel says "not supplied". Only Members with an active hypertension or diabetes plan, not minors.

## The record and the ledger
`care_gaps`: type, due date, owner, status, `closure_reason` (closed | declined | unreachable | wrong), `closed_by_signal` (the reading, lab or medicine row, or a recovered line). A CHECK makes `closed` impossible without a signal; `close_care_gap_manual` refuses `closed`, needs a note, and refuses `unreachable` before the configured logged staff attempts. A message sent or opened is never read as a signal. A closed row never changes (trigger). Closure reasons: `closed` (a recorded outcome), `declined`/`unreachable`/`wrong` (a person did it) and `superseded` (the system did it: the person left the programme, the medicine stopped, the rule was switched off or no longer applies). Only the person-made reasons hold a gap back from reopening for 30 days; a lapse and renew reopens at once. The `why` column holds a criterion code, its line, a day count and a date, never a reading or a result, so the coordinator queue stays logistics. Staff read it only as the assigned owner or through the S39b tie (`private.care_gap_visible`); a patient, an untied coordinator, an untied clinician and an institution read nothing. No write grant.

## Re-validation against the current configuration
Every run checks each open gap against the rules as they stand now, in this order: still enrolled; a recorded outcome (closes as `closed`); the rule is on and its numbers are supplied in the CURRENT signed (else newest draft) configuration (else `superseded`, kind `superseded_config`); the person still meets the rule under the current numbers (else `superseded`, kind `premise_no_longer_holds`, `trend_stale` for a patient who stopped logging, `no_scoped_medicine`); or it cannot be judged (no adherence line, too few doses: left open and not messaged, counted once per batch in the audit log). Shadow versus live is decided per run from the configuration state, and the ladder re-checks each gap right before any step: a gap opened in shadow is promoted to live only there, only because it still qualifies under a signed configuration. The nightly pass checks every gap; the hourly pass checks only gaps never checked, older than `recheck_after_hours` (20), or with a new reading, lab or medicine change since `last_checked_at`.

## Owners
Same organisation only (a trigger refuses any other). Logistics: the least-loaded available coordinator; none, then the least-loaded eligible doctor (employed, a doctor tier, eligible, not on leave or post-call rest, no conflict with the patient; load = open gaps plus pushed clinical tasks); none, then unowned. An unowned live gap opens an immediate sev2 incident `care-gap-unowned` (sev3 while only in shadow) and is readable by an admin of the organisation, on `/admin/ops/care-gaps`, and by nobody else. A judgement item goes to a doctor only; a trigger refuses a coordinator as its owner. A fallback doctor hands logistics gaps back to a coordinator as soon as one is available. A doctor-owned gap carries a review clock (`review_due_at`, `doctor_review_days`): overdue, it goes once to the Chief Medical Officer with a new date; still unreviewed, an ops incident `care-gap-review-overdue` opens. No `care_tasks` or `clinical_tasks` row is created (OQ-S90-05).

## Failure, timeouts and overlap
A failure on one gap is counted, written to `audit_log` (`care_gap_engine.error_*`) and never stops the rest; any failure opens `care-gap-engine-failing` or `care-gap-close-failing`. A statement timeout ends the closure pass, the nightly pass or the ladder with the work done so far and a `timed_out` flag, and opens the same incident. Each pass takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock under its own name and a second overlapping pass returns `locked`; gap rows are taken `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; a send is one `care_gap_attempts` row per (gap, step, Lagos week) written before the notification, so an overlap cannot send twice (a lost race is counted as a duplicate, not a failure).

## The ladder
Push (skipped, not counted, when there is no subscription), in-app card, email, then a coordinator call task; 3 days between steps; weekly cap of 2 sent messages per patient across all gaps; no send 20:00 to 08:00 Lagos; a capped step is recorded and retried the next day. A judgement item (trend) has no patient step and no coordinator task: it is owned by a doctor and shows on the doctor's care gap list. Copy is two neutral templates (`care_gap_checkin`, `care_gap_checkin_followup`), payload carries only `copy_variant`; the wording lives in `packages/i18n/src/care-gap-copy.ts` and a test pins it to the sender, the in-app preview and the database rows. A/B scaffolding (copy variant, send hour) is config with `ab.enabled = false`; each send records the variant and, 7 days later, whether the gap closed by a signal.

## Staff and institution views
"Why this gap is open" panel, ranking (risk, days overdue, trend), next attempt, contact log, log-attempt and close forms on both outreach worklists. Institutions: `care_gap_institution_summary` (service role only) counts distinct people by type, open and closed BY A RECORDED OUTCOME in 90 days from the ledger (people who closed another way are counted apart, by reason, as declined, unreachable or wrong, and never as closed), test accounts and shadow gaps excluded; a small cell is withheld with a second cell, totals are withheld whenever a cell is, and a filter only selects published cells (proved under all 63 filter combinations, in SQL and again in the TS parser). The old closed-in-90-days approximation is removed.

## Not built (first slice)
Silent-patient re-engagement as its own trigger, the patient "today" card, outcome nudges from a patient's own trend, any ML. Doctor-owned gaps are not mirrored into `care_tasks` or `clinical_tasks` (they carry a review clock instead). Employer 50-person floor not wired. See OQ-S90-01 to -07.
